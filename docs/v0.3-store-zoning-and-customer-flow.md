# K&S OS — v0.3 Store Zoning & Customer Flow

**Milestone:** v0.3.0 — Store Design  
**Status:** Draft standard — ready for site-specific design

## Purpose

Define the functional zones and customer-flow rules for the K&S flagship concept. This is a concept standard, not a floor plan or construction drawing.

## Zone Model

1. **Arrival & Welcome** — immediate orientation, brand signal, and access to help.
2. **Discovery** — accessible introduction to high-interest products and current offers.
3. **Category** — clearly organized product families for browsing and comparison.
4. **Experience & Demo** — hands-on exploration with safe, maintained demonstration devices.
5. **Consultation** — comfortable conversations for advice, comparison, finance, or complex purchases.
6. **Service & Support** — visible, approachable post-sale and service assistance.
7. **Checkout & Departure** — clear completion, collection, and farewell without congestion.
8. **Staff & Back-of-House** — secure operational space that does not disrupt the customer environment.

## Flow Rules

- Arrival must make the store’s purpose and help points immediately understandable.
- Customers may browse freely without being forced through a fixed path.
- The path from discovery to comparison to consultation must be intuitive.
- Checkout and service queues must not block browsing or the entrance.
- Staff sightlines should support assistance and safety while respecting customer comfort.
- Accessible circulation is required between all customer-facing zones.
- Security measures should protect products without turning the environment into a barrier.

## Adjacency Principles

- Arrival should connect naturally to discovery and category zones.
- Consultation should be near comparison/product zones but offer appropriate calm and privacy.
- Service/support should be easy to locate and separate from checkout activity where practical.
- Checkout should support a smooth departure while remaining accessible to staff and secure processes.
- Back-of-house functions must support replenishment and service without crossing primary customer flow unnecessarily.

## Site-Specific Inputs Required

Final zoning requires verified site dimensions, entrances, exits, structural constraints, utilities, accessibility requirements, capacity assumptions, and local safety obligations.
