## Outcome

Provide secure, maintainable technology and governed AI that improve customer and employee outcomes while keeping humans accountable.

## Capability Architecture

| Layer | Required capability | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Experience | Customer portal/identity, assisted selling, service status | Consent, accessibility, channel continuity |
| Business systems | Product, order, inventory, CRM, service, finance, workforce | System of record and role-based access |
| Data/integration | Canonical identifiers, APIs, events, reporting | Data owner, quality rule, lineage and retention |
| AI | Concierge and internal assistant use cases | Approved purpose, human oversight and evaluation |
| Platform | Identity, devices, network, monitoring, backup | Secure configuration and lifecycle ownership |
| Governance | Vendor, change, incident, continuity and risk management | Approval, testing, logs, recovery and review |

## AI Controls

Every AI use case must document purpose, owner, users, data classes, prohibited uses, human-review point, quality/safety evaluation, fallback, monitoring, cost and retirement trigger. AI must not independently make high-impact employment, credit, legal, safety, pricing-exception or customer-remedy decisions. Protected data requires explicit lawful purpose, minimum access and approved safeguards.

## Data, Security and Privacy

- Name a system of record and owner for each critical data domain.
- Collect the minimum required data; publish retention and deletion rules.
- Use least privilege, strong authentication, device controls, encryption where appropriate, logging and timely access removal.
- Assess vendors for security, privacy, resilience, portability, support and exit risk before contract approval.
- Maintain incident classification, containment, notification, recovery and lessons-learned procedures.
- Test backups and continuity rather than relying on configuration claims.

## Delivery Governance

All changes require a business owner, acceptance criteria, risk classification, test evidence, rollback/fallback plan and release approval. Material architecture, vendor, data-sharing and AI decisions receive a decision record. Maintain service availability, incident, recovery, data-quality, adoption, outcome, cost and risk measures.

## Activation and Closure Evidence

Activation requires named technology, data, security/privacy and AI-accountability roles; an approved architecture; prioritized use cases; vendor/procurement authority; and a controlled pilot. Operational closure requires production evidence, security/privacy review, user acceptance, AI evaluations, incident/continuity tests, support readiness, measured value and leadership acceptance.
