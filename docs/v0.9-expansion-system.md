## Outcome and Sequence

Grow from a proven Nepalgunj flagship to Western Nepal, then selected major cities and ultimately a national chain without weakening trust, customer experience, operating control or cash resilience. Sequence is directional, not an approved store commitment.

## Readiness Gate

Expansion analysis may start only when the flagship has an accepted operating baseline. A new-site investment case requires evidence of repeatable customer standards, stable key roles, controlled inventory/cash, service capacity, reliable technology/reporting, sustainable store economics, available leadership capacity and resolved material risks.

## Market and Site Scorecard

| Dimension | Evidence expected |
|---|---|
| Demand/competition | Customer, category, competitor, channel and seasonality evidence |
| Strategic fit | Brand role, network logic and service reach |
| Site/customer access | Catchment, visibility, access, circulation and accessibility |
| Commercial | Occupancy, fit-out, working capital, staffing, logistics and downside case |
| Delivery | Utilities, permits, landlord, construction, supply and schedule feasibility |
| Operating capacity | Leadership, hiring, training, replenishment, service and oversight |
| Risk | Legal, safety, security, resilience, reputation and exit obligations |

Weights, thresholds and assumptions must be approved before scoring candidates; scoring does not replace judgment or due diligence.

## Stage Gates

1. **Thesis:** strategic region/format and learning objective approved.
2. **Screen:** comparable markets/sites assessed consistently.
3. **Due diligence:** legal, technical, commercial, customer and risk evidence verified.
4. **Investment:** full economics, funding, authority, contingency and exit conditions approved.
5. **Delivery:** owner, schedule, design, permits, vendors, hiring, systems and launch readiness controlled.
6. **Open/stabilize:** safe opening, daily review and issue escalation.
7. **Post-opening:** 30/60/90-day and mature-period review against approved case; learn before scaling.

## Replication and Adaptation

Mandatory elements include brand promise, customer/service minimums, safety, data/privacy, financial/inventory controls, decision records and reporting definitions. Store size, assortment, language, local marketing, fixtures and staffing may adapt only with evidence and recorded approval. Franchise, licensing and international options require separate legal, brand-control, economic, capability and exit analysis.

## Activation and Closure Evidence

Activation requires a named owner, approved thesis, flagship baseline, scorecard, capital envelope and due-diligence authority. Documentation closure does not close expansion operationally. Operational validation requires at least one authorized rollout evaluated against its case, closed material defects, stable controls, post-opening evidence and leadership acceptance before repeat scale.
