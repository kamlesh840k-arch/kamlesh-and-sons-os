# K&S Innovation Vault

**Document ID:** DOC-INV-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Foundation

## Purpose
Preserve potentially valuable ideas without confusing exploration with commitment.

## Innovation Principles
- Capture ideas early.
- Keep hypotheses distinct from decisions.
- Define the customer or business problem.
- Test cheaply where uncertainty is high.
- Record evidence and lessons.
- Scale only after explicit approval.

## Initial Innovation Themes
- AI Concierge
- Digital Customer Identity
- Experience Passport
- AI Trade-in Estimator
- Founder Dashboard
- Employee AI

## Lifecycle
**Captured → Framed → Explored → Tested → Validated / Rejected → Scaled / Archived**

## Governance
Material innovation commitments should connect to the decision log and project portfolio. Sensitive ideas and intellectual property require appropriate access controls.
