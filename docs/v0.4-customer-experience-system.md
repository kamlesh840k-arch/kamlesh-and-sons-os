## Outcome

Create one trusted, premium and repeatable journey from awareness through after-sales, with clear standards, ownership, escalation, feedback and measurement.

## Canonical Journey

| Stage | Customer promise | Minimum control | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Accurate, useful information | Approved claims and current product information | Content/claim approval |
| Arrive | Welcoming and easy to navigate | Greeting, accessibility and queue standard | Observation sample |
| Diagnose | Needs understood before recommendation | Consent-based discovery questions | Consultation record |
| Compare/demo | Clear, fair comparison | Demo readiness and disclosure checklist | Demo audit |
| Decide/pay | Transparent price, warranty and finance terms | Order recap and explicit consent | Transaction record |
| Handover/setup | Product works and customer understands it | Identity/order check, setup and data-consent steps | Handover checklist |
| After-sales | Ownership and timely updates | Case ID, service level and escalation | Case history |
| Recover/retain | Fair resolution and learning | Complaint authority and root-cause capture | Closed-loop record |

## Service Standards

- Greet respectfully without pressure; adapt assistance to customer preference and accessibility need.
- Diagnose needs, budget and intended use before recommending a product.
- Separate facts, comparisons, estimates, promotions and financing obligations clearly.
- Never conceal grey-market status, warranty limits, recurring costs, data use or material exclusions.
- Obtain consent before collecting customer data or performing setup that accesses personal information.
- Give every unresolved issue a named owner, next action and promised update time.
- Recovery should restore fairness, explain the decision and record the systemic lesson.

## Ownership and Escalation

Frontline staff own the current interaction; the shift/store lead owns exceptions; the Customer Experience Owner owns standards and recurring issues; the Founder approves material policy, compensation outside delegated authority, and reputational exceptions. Safety, fraud, privacy, legal and financial matters escalate immediately to their qualified owner.

## Measures

Track journey completion, wait time, consultation quality, conversion, setup completion, complaint rate, first-contact resolution, resolution time, repeat purchase/referral, customer effort and recurring root causes. Targets require baseline evidence and founder approval; no invented target is authoritative.

## Activation and Closure Evidence

Activation requires a named owner, trained roles, approved service-recovery authority, usable forms/system, privacy review, and a pilot plan. Operational closure requires real customer research, completed pilot observations, measured results, corrected failures, staff competence evidence, and leadership acceptance.

## Boundaries

CRM implementation belongs to v0.5; staffing and SOP deployment to v0.6; acquisition campaigns to v0.7; pricing/financial controls to v0.8. This document defines requirements for those systems.
