**Version:** 1.0.0-rc.2

## Purpose

This registry is the control layer between the historical DEC series and the active or planned operating-system master documents. It prevents premature deletion and gives every consolidated or deferred decision a destination.

## Deferred Destination Rule

When a roadmap milestone becomes active, the relevant historical DEC inputs should be consolidated into the implementation-level master document for that milestone. Until then, the original DEC records remain historical source material.

## Physical File Policy

Historical DEC files are not physically relocated as part of this audit. The consolidation architecture preserves their history and establishes a clear destination without creating duplicate records or losing traceability.

Physical relocation or archival is a separate controlled migration step and should occur only after all references are validated.
