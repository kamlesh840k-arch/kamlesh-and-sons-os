# K&S OS — v0.3 Lighting & Environment Principles

**Milestone:** v0.3.0 — Store Design  
**Status:** Draft standard — concept-level

## Purpose

Create a comfortable, premium, and practical store environment that improves product visibility and customer confidence.

## Lighting Principles

- Provide clear, comfortable general illumination for safe navigation.
- Use layered lighting to distinguish arrival, product, consultation, service, and checkout zones.
- Make product color, texture, and detail understandable without glare.
- Avoid overly dramatic lighting that reduces clarity or comfort.
- Design lighting with maintenance, energy efficiency, and replacement access in mind.

## Environmental Principles

- Support acoustic comfort for browsing and consultation.
- Maintain thermal comfort, air quality, and practical cleaning standards.
- Use materials and finishes that are durable, maintainable, and consistent with the brand.
- Avoid environmental choices that impair accessibility, product safety, or staff work.
- Plan for power, network, charging, and display needs at the concept stage.

## Validation Required

Detailed design must verify lighting levels, glare, emergency lighting, acoustics, mechanical systems, energy performance, and local safety/building requirements with qualified professionals.
