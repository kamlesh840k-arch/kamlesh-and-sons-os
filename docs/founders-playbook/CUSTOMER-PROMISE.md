## Our Promise

Every customer should leave feeling:

> **“I made the right choice.”**

## What Customers Can Expect

- Respectful attention without unnecessary pressure.
- Clear, knowledgeable, and honest guidance.
- The opportunity to experience suitable products.
- Transparent explanation of options, prices, and limitations.
- Calm, professional service before, during, and after purchase.
- Responsible handling of personal information and devices.
- Ownership when something goes wrong.

## What the Promise Does Not Mean

It does not guarantee that every product will always be available, that KAMLESH & SONS will always offer the lowest price, or that every requested outcome is possible. It requires clarity, care, fairness, and reliable follow-through.

## Recovery Standard

When expectations are missed: listen, acknowledge, establish the facts, explain the next action, assign ownership, and close the loop.
