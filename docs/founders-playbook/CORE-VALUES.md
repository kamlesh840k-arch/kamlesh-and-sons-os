## Our Values

### Customer First
Understand the real need and protect the customer's long-term interest.

### Integrity
Be honest about products, prices, availability, limitations, and mistakes.

### Excellence
Set high standards and improve details that shape trust and experience.

### Ownership
Take responsibility for outcomes, follow through, and fix recurring problems at the system level.

### Innovation
Use curiosity, evidence, and technology to create meaningful improvement.

### Respect
Treat customers, colleagues, partners, and communities with dignity.

### Learning
Seek feedback, document lessons, build capability, and improve continuously.

## How Values Are Used

Values guide hiring, leadership, recognition, customer recovery, and difficult decisions. They are not slogans. Specific conduct, performance, and employment requirements belong in approved policies and SOPs.
