## Purpose
Capture the founding operating philosophy so growth does not depend on undocumented assumptions.

## Vision
> **To transform retail in Western Nepal and become Nepal's most trusted premium technology brand.**

## Mission
Provide an unmatched technology buying experience through world-class products, knowledgeable people, exceptional service, and innovative technology.

## Founding Principles
1. Customer First
2. Experience Over Discounts
3. Documentation First
4. Continuous Improvement
5. Long-Term Thinking
6. Knowledge Before Sales
7. Transparency
8. Innovation
9. Professionalism

## Leadership Rules
- Make important decisions deliberately.
- Prefer durable systems over heroic individual effort.
- Protect customer trust over short-term sales.
- Document important reasoning.
- Measure outcomes, not activity alone.
- Fix recurring problems at the system level.
- Preserve what works while improving it.

## Decision Discipline
Material strategic, operational, financial, technology, brand and architectural choices receive `DEC-*` records. Established decisions are not silently changed.

## Knowledge Discipline
Chat is for brainstorming. GitHub is the official record. Important knowledge moves into controlled repository documents.

## Growth Philosophy
Build systematically through the roadmap: foundation, brand, store design, customer experience, AI and technology, operations, marketing, finance, expansion, then flagship readiness.

## Founder Review Questions
- Are customers becoming more trusting and loyal?
- Are margins and cash improving sustainably?
- Are operations becoming more repeatable?
- Are people becoming more capable?
- Are material risks visible and controlled?
- Is technology simplifying the business?
- Are we building an asset that can scale beyond individuals?
