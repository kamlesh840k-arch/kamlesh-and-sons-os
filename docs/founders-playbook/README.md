## Official Vision

> **To transform retail in Western Nepal and become Nepal's most trusted premium technology brand.**

## Official Mission

> **To provide an unmatched technology buying experience through world-class products, knowledgeable people, exceptional service, and innovative technology.**

## Founding Principle

> **We are not building a phone store. We are building Nepal's most trusted premium technology brand.**

Retail is the starting point, not the destination.

## Customer Promise

Every customer should leave feeling:

> **“I made the right choice.”**

## Authority

Accepted decision records remain authoritative. The Founding Charter integrates the founding direction; the focused documents make it easier to apply. Conflicts are resolved in favor of the controlling decision until formally superseded.

Material strategic, operational, financial, technology, brand, and architectural choices receive `DEC-*` records. Established decisions are not silently changed. GitHub is the official record; chat is for brainstorming.

## Founder Review Questions

- Are customers becoming more trusting and loyal?
- Are margins and cash improving sustainably?
- Are operations becoming more repeatable?
- Are people becoming more capable?
- Are material risks visible and controlled?
- Is technology simplifying the business?
- Are we building an asset that can scale beyond individuals?
