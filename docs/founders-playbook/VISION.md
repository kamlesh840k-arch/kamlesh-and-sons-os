## Official Vision

> **To transform retail in Western Nepal and become Nepal's most trusted premium technology brand.**

## What It Means

- **Transform retail:** improve how customers discover, compare, buy, and receive support for technology.
- **Western Nepal first:** build a strong regional foundation before wider expansion.
- **Trusted:** protect confidence through honest advice, reliable service, and consistent standards.
- **Premium technology brand:** create a durable identity beyond one store or product category.

## Strategic Test

A major initiative should strengthen customer trust, the premium experience, scalable capability, or sustainable expansion. Short-term gains that weaken these outcomes do not serve the vision.

## Evidence of Progress

Customer trust and advocacy, consistent experience, capable teams, sustainable store economics, brand strength, and successful expansion will provide evidence of progress. Specific targets belong in approved operating and financial plans.
