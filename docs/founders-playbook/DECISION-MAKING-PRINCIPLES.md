## Principles

- Protect customer trust and company integrity.
- Align material choices with the vision and mission.
- Seek evidence and state uncertainty.
- Consider customer value, strategic fit, economics, risk, and trade-offs.
- Prefer scalable systems over recurring improvisation.
- Assign a clear owner and define what success means.
- Document material decisions and preserve superseded history.
- Test reversible assumptions before making irreversible commitments.
- Escalate legal, safety, privacy, security, and financial concerns.
- Review outcomes and capture learning.

## Decision Test

Before a significant commitment, ask:

1. What problem and customer outcome are we addressing?
2. What evidence supports the choice?
3. What alternatives and trade-offs exist?
4. Does it strengthen trust and premium positioning?
5. What are the financial, legal, operational, people, data, and security implications?
6. Can it scale beyond one person or store?
7. Who owns implementation and review?
8. Which records, controls, and measures must change?

Routine choices should remain proportionate. Material strategic or irreversible choices receive a DEC record.
