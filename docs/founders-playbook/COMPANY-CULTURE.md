## The Culture We Intend to Build

KAMLESH & SONS combines warm human service with disciplined execution.

## Expected Behaviors

- Listen carefully and explain clearly.
- Act with honesty, calm, and respect.
- Take ownership and close loops.
- Share knowledge and document important learning.
- Ask for evidence and admit uncertainty.
- Raise risks early without fear or concealment.
- Improve systems after mistakes and recurring friction.
- Give useful feedback and challenge ideas respectfully.
- Protect customer, employee, and company information.
- Develop others and celebrate team outcomes.

## Leadership Standard

Leaders model the culture, make priorities clear, give people the context and tools to succeed, address harmful behavior consistently, and take responsibility for the systems they oversee.

## Learning and Accountability

Good-faith experiments may fail; lessons must be captured. Avoidable negligence, dishonesty, retaliation, concealment, or repeated disregard for approved standards are not excused as experimentation.

Detailed employment rules, performance processes, and grievance procedures belong in approved people policies.
