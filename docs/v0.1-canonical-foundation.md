# K&S OS v0.1.0 — Canonical Foundation

**Document ID:** DOC-FOUNDATION-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Canonical Foundation — Part 5  
**Decision basis:** DEC-0100 and DEC-0101

## Purpose

This document consolidates the v0.1 foundation into a compact, usable operating baseline. It is the active reference for foundation design.

The contributing DEC records remain preserved as historical source material. This document does not delete, replace, or erase them; it identifies the active foundation outcome they collectively establish.

## Foundation Outcome

v0.1 is complete when K&S has a governed repository, clear founding principles, accountable decision-making, durable institutional knowledge, safe technology and data principles, proportionate risk and controls, and a release gate for future milestones.

## Canonical Foundation Areas

### 1. Founding Direction & Customer Promise

K&S operates from a clear vision, mission, long-term ambition, customer-experience philosophy, store philosophy, research discipline, and founder operating principles. These are enduring constraints for every later roadmap milestone.

**Source decisions:** DEC-0003, DEC-0004, DEC-0005, DEC-0006, DEC-0007, DEC-0017, DEC-0020.

### 2. Governance & Decision Rights

Material decisions are documented, attributable, escalated appropriately, and governed through the K&S OS. Leadership responsibility, decision rights, and portfolio-level priorities are explicit.

**Source decisions:** DEC-0021, DEC-0080.

### 3. Documentation & Institutional Memory

The repository is the single source of truth. Decision records, innovation records, meeting records, templates, knowledge capture, and organizational memory are maintained so knowledge outlives individual contributors.

**Source decisions:** DEC-0022, DEC-0023, DEC-0076.

### 4. Technology, Data & Security Principles

Technology is selected and evolved through maintainable architecture principles, responsible data handling, security-by-design, privacy protection, and controlled change. Detailed platforms and AI implementation are deferred to v0.5.

**Source decisions:** DEC-0027, DEC-0042.  
**Consolidated inputs:** DEC-0014, DEC-0035, DEC-0050, DEC-0055, DEC-0061, DEC-0062.

### 5. Legal, Risk & Control Foundation

K&S maintains a defensible baseline for legal and compliance governance, financial control, fraud prevention, internal control, business continuity, and risk management. Detailed functional systems mature with their relevant roadmap milestones.

**Source decisions:** DEC-0034, DEC-0036, DEC-0071, DEC-0072.  
**Consolidated inputs:** DEC-0043, DEC-0051, DEC-0058, DEC-0079.

### 6. Management System & Continuous Improvement

Planning, portfolio governance, process improvement, operating-model integration, and performance reporting follow a single management-system discipline. Department-level KPIs and implementation are staged for later releases.

**Source decisions:** DEC-0077, DEC-0078, DEC-0081, DEC-0089, DEC-0099.

### 7. Release Control

Foundation readiness is verified before the organization begins the next roadmap milestone. Completion is recorded formally, and later domain work is not treated as a v0.1 prerequisite.

**Source decisions:** DEC-0100, DEC-0101.

## Lifecycle Rules

- The seven areas above are the active v0.1 foundation reference.
- A source DEC retains its historical authority and must remain traceable.
- A deferred decision becomes active only when its roadmap milestone begins and a domain master is created or updated.
- Detailed operating systems, commercial systems, store design, marketing, finance, and expansion work are not v0.1 completion requirements.
