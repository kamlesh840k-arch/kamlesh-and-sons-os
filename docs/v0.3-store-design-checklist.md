## Remaining Validation Before v0.3 Closure

- Confirm the design governance owner.
- Validate the design against an actual site, applicable accessibility and safety requirements, and qualified professional advice.
- Approve any detailed drawings, budgets, vendors, procurement, or construction through separate decisions.

## Out of Scope

- Detailed customer journey design belongs to v0.4.
- Detailed operating SOPs belong to v0.6.
- Marketing campaigns belong to v0.7.
- Finance/budget approval belongs to v0.8.
- Expansion replication strategy belongs to v0.9.
- Construction drawings, vendor procurement and final engineering require separate implementation decisions.

## Completion Gate

v0.3 should be considered complete only when the flagship concept and store standards are documented, consistent with the approved v0.2 brand, practically usable, site-validated where required, and ready to guide detailed design without ambiguity.
