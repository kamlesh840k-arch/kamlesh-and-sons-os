## Purpose
Provide a navigational index for significant K&S decisions. Individual `DEC-*` files remain authoritative historical records; the consolidation registry defines where deferred or overlapping decisions belong in the roadmap.

## Rules
- Every material decision receives a unique ID.
- Record rationale, implications and related references.
- Do not overwrite history when a decision changes.
- Link decisions to roadmap and implementation documents.
- Do not treat the number of DEC files as a measure of foundation completeness.
