# KAMLESH Mobile — Customer Policies and Service Operations

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-FLAGSHIP-003  
**Version:** 0.2.0
**Status:** Operating draft — legal, tax, distributor, privacy and insurer review required before publication  
**Accountable owner:** Founder until Customer Experience and Service Owners are appointed  
**Canonical dependencies:** v0.4 Customer Experience, v0.5 AI & Technology, v0.6 Operations & People, v0.7 Marketing, v0.8 Finance and v1.0 Flagship Ready

These policies are designed for authorised Nepal-market devices. Brand warranty rules and mandatory rights under Nepal law take priority where they provide the customer greater protection. Trade-in, pickup/drop and loaner terms remain conditional on the gates in [RESEARCH-OWNERSHIP-001](../../research/trade-in-and-ownership-support-economics.md).

## 1. Proof provided with every handset

At billing, KAMLESH Mobile will:

1. Match the device, box and invoice IMEI/serial number.
2. Validate all applicable IMEIs through Nepal's MDMS system.
3. Record the model, colour, storage, IMEI/serial number and condition on the invoice.
4. Identify the authorised Nepal distributor and applicable warranty.
5. Invite the customer to inspect the seal and physical condition before opening.
6. Obtain acknowledgement after activation, inspection and handover.

The customer should retain the invoice, box and warranty documentation.

## 2. Exchange and return terms

### 2.1 Unopened products

- A sealed, unused product may be requested for exchange within 3 calendar days of purchase with the original invoice and complete packaging.
- Exchange is subject to inspection, stock availability and payment of any price difference.
- If the replacement costs less, the balance will be issued as store credit unless a cash refund is required by law.
- Distributor-supplied promotional gifts must also be returned unopened and complete.

### 2.2 Opened or activated handsets

- A handset that has been opened, activated, configured, registered or had its seal removed cannot be exchanged for change of mind, colour preference or a later price reduction.
- A suspected manufacturing defect will be handled under the defective-on-arrival or manufacturer warranty process.
- Replacement is subject to confirmation by the authorised service centre or distributor when the brand requires it.

### 2.3 Defective-on-arrival requests

- A suspected manufacturing defect should be reported as soon as possible and preferably within 7 calendar days.
- The customer must bring the handset, box, accessories, invoice and warranty documents.
- Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repair, modified software, accidental damage and customer-caused faults are excluded.
- The applicable brand/distributor policy determines whether the remedy is replacement, repair or another resolution.

### 2.4 Accessories

- A sealed accessory may be requested for exchange within 3 calendar days with the invoice.
- A defective accessory should be reported within 7 calendar days or the warranty printed on the invoice, whichever is longer.
- Screen protectors, installed films, earphones and other opened personal-use items are not returnable unless defective.

### 2.5 Price changes

Prices, gifts and promotions can change after purchase. A later market-price or MRP reduction does not create an exchange or refund right unless a published KAMLESH Mobile price-protection offer applies.

## 3. Warranty assistance terms

- KAMLESH Mobile assists the customer in submitting eligible authorised products to the relevant distributor or service centre.
- The manufacturer or distributor makes the final warranty decision.
- Warranty normally excludes accidental damage, cracked or bent devices, liquid entry, corrosion, misuse, unauthorised opening or repair, altered IMEI/serial numbers, rooting or unauthorised software modification.
- Repair time depends on diagnosis, parts availability, brand approval and transport. Any date provided before confirmation is an estimate, not a guarantee.
- A temporary replacement device is not guaranteed unless explicitly written on the service receipt.
- The customer is responsible for backing up data and removing SIM cards, memory cards and sensitive accounts before service.
- KAMLESH Mobile will not request a customer's banking PIN, mobile-wallet PIN, email password or one-time password.

## 4. Repair-booking terms

Every repair receives a numbered job card containing:

- Customer name and contact number
- Device model, colour, IMEI/serial number
- Visible condition and received accessories
- Reported problem
- Lock-screen access arrangement, if genuinely required
- Estimated diagnostic charge and repair range
- Data-risk acknowledgement
- Promised update date
- Customer approval and staff signature

### Repair process

1. Photograph the device at intake with customer consent.
2. Perform diagnosis and issue a written estimate.
3. Obtain approval before additional work or parts.
4. Notify the customer if the estimate changes materially.
5. Test the agreed functions at handover.
6. Return replaced parts when safe and requested, except parts retained under supplier exchange programmes.

### Repair warranty

- KAMLESH Mobile workmanship is covered for 30 days for the same repaired fault.
- A replacement part is covered by the warranty stated on the job card, based on the part supplier's terms.
- The warranty does not cover a different fault, later impact, liquid exposure, tampering or damage unrelated to the completed repair.

### Data and privacy

- Customers should back up their data before repair.
- Data recovery is never guaranteed.
- Staff may access only the functions reasonably required for diagnosis and testing.
- Customer photos, messages, contacts and accounts must not be copied or viewed without a documented service need and consent.
- Temporary passwords must be destroyed or changed by the customer at handover.

### Uncollected devices

- The customer will receive collection reminders at 7, 30 and 60 days after completion.
- Storage charges may apply after 30 days if disclosed on the job card.
- Devices uncollected for 90 days will be handled only according to applicable law and a separately approved abandoned-property procedure; they will not be sold automatically under this draft.

## 5. Service Lounge

The Service Lounge is a visible customer area, not a hidden repair counter. It provides:

- New-device setup
- Data transfer
- Software update and basic troubleshooting
- Account and security education
- Warranty assistance
- Repair booking and status updates
- Device health checks
- Trade-in inspection by appointment

The Service Lounge must display a menu showing which services are free, member benefits, chargeable services and estimated turnaround times.

### 5.1 Trade-in and exchange — conditional pilot policy

KAMLESH Mobile should initially facilitate a qualified, disclosed partner-principal exchange rather than buy used devices into its own inventory. The receipt/quote must name the principal buyer, KAMLESH's role, indicative versus final value, quote validity, inspection/deduction method, exchange bonus separately from device value, payment/credit timing, return right, complaint route and data/custody responsibility.

Before final acceptance:

1. Match model/storage and IMEI/serial; record the MDMS result and lost/stolen check.
2. Obtain the legally reviewed ownership/authority declaration and proportionate evidence.
3. Check power, account/activation/finance/network lock, battery safety, display, body and agreed functions using the published checklist.
4. Show every deduction and obtain customer acceptance of the final value; a grade alone is not an explanation.
5. Let the customer back up, sign out and initiate erase without disclosing Apple/Google, banking, wallet or other passwords.
6. Validate the approved reset/sanitization outcome and record any exception; quarantine a device that cannot be reliably sanitized.
7. Transfer custody and reconcile partner payment/customer credit before closing the case.

The customer may reject a reduced final value and recover the same unchanged device unless a legally approved simultaneous instant-exchange process applies and the customer accepted the final inspection result. KAMLESH will not advertise a guaranteed buyback percentage, instant cash, “highest price,” certified wipe, refurbished condition or warranty unless the exact program and evidence are approved.

KAMLESH-owned used inventory, cash buyback and future guaranteed value are **not launch-approved** under this draft.

## 6. WhatsApp catalogue and ordering

- KAMLESH Mobile will publish one verified official WhatsApp number on invoices, the website and store signage.
- Catalogue prices are indicative until staff confirm live stock, current price, gifts, colour and warranty in writing.
- Customers must never send a PIN, OTP or full payment-card information through WhatsApp.
- An order is confirmed only after an official order number and payment acknowledgement are issued.
- Customer contact information will be used for the order, service updates and opted-in marketing only.
- Marketing messages must include a simple opt-out method.

## 7. Advance-order terms

- The order confirmation must state the exact model, colour, storage, estimated delivery, deposit and whether the price is fixed or estimated.
- A price is fixed only when the confirmation expressly says "price locked". Otherwise, the final authorised-distributor price applies and the customer may approve or cancel before dispatch.
- For ordinary stocked models, the suggested deposit is 10–20%.
- For special-order premium devices, the suggested deposit is 25–50%, depending on distributor cancellation terms.
- If KAMLESH Mobile cannot supply within the agreed extended deadline, the customer may cancel for a full deposit refund.
- If the customer cancels a special order after the distributor order becomes non-cancellable, only the clearly disclosed non-recoverable cost may be retained, subject to law.
- Refund method and expected processing time must be written on the order form.

## 8. Local delivery

- Stock, total price, delivery charge and expected window must be confirmed before dispatch.
- High-value deliveries require invoice-name verification and a one-time delivery code. The code is not a banking OTP.
- The customer should inspect the outer package and seal before accepting an unopened product.
- Opening and activation should occur with the customer present or through a recorded video-support process.
- Failed-delivery and re-delivery charges must be disclosed before the order is accepted.
- Delivery staff may not collect an undisclosed cash amount.

## 9. Pickup and drop service

- Pickup/drop is available only in published service zones and time windows.
- Every pickup receives a digital or printed receipt with a unique case and tamper-evident pouch/seal number, photographs, device details, condition and accessories.
- Devices must be switched off and packaged appropriately unless staff provide a secure pouch.
- The customer should remove SIM cards and memory cards unless they are necessary for the approved diagnosis.
- Identity verification and a handover code are required at return.
- The store must maintain chain-of-custody status: requested, assigned, collected, received, under diagnosis, ready and delivered.
- Liability limits, insurance coverage and exclusions must be approved by the insurer and legal reviewer before launch.
- The named runner/partner and time of every custody transfer must be recorded. No undocumented subcontracting or overnight vehicle storage is allowed.
- The published fee or membership allowance must reflect route-zone, failed-attempt and re-delivery rules. “Free” pickup/drop requires an approved funding source and service-zone cap.
- Any unexplained seal, serial, condition, accessory or custody variance freezes handover and triggers the incident process.

### 9.1 Loaner/standby devices — not launch-approved

A loaner may be offered only under a separately approved small serialized pool for defined eligible cases. The agreement must record the linked service case, device/serial/condition/accessories, issue and due dates, fees/deposit/deductible, late/loss/damage responsibility, prohibited uses, extensions, return test and privacy/sanitization steps.

Loaners are not guaranteed to match the customer's device. Customers should avoid banking/wallet setup where practical and must not give KAMLESH credentials. A returned loaner is quarantined until accounts/SIM/storage are removed and the approved wipe and condition validation passes. Legal, insurer, privacy, replacement-cost and per-case economics must be approved before the first issue.

## 10. KAMLESH Care membership

### Proposed price

NPR 2,999 for 12 months, covering one member and up to three registered personal devices.

### Improved benefits

- 10% discount on eligible accessories, capped at NPR 5,000 total discount per membership year
- Free new-device setup for devices purchased from KAMLESH Mobile
- Two assisted data-transfer sessions per year
- Two device health checks and cleanings per year
- Priority Service Lounge queue and repair updates
- 15% discount on eligible repair labour, capped at NPR 2,000 per year
- Up to two included pickup or drop trips per year within the approved core zone, only after route cost, capacity, insurance and the membership funding source pass the ownership-support gate
- Free warranty-submission assistance for eligible authorised devices
- Four free screen-protector installation services; protector cost is separate unless included in a promotion
- Member-only workshops on backups, privacy, parental controls and fraud prevention
- Early access to selected launches and pre-order windows
- Digital record of invoices, warranty dates and registered device details

### Membership safeguards

- Membership is not device insurance, an extended manufacturer warranty or a guarantee of data recovery.
- Phone purchases, gift cards, already discounted bundles and selected premium accessories may be excluded from percentage discounts.
- Benefits have no cash value, are non-transferable and expire at the end of the membership term.
- Lost, stolen and accidentally damaged devices are not replaced under membership.
- Parts, courier charges outside the included zone and authorised-service-centre charges remain payable.
- The member must approve all repair charges before work starts.

### Founding-member offer

For the first 300 paid members, test whether an extra pickup/drop allowance or one family device would improve uptake without destroying contribution. Approve the exact benefit only after route cost, capacity and membership economics are modeled; this draft does not promise it.
