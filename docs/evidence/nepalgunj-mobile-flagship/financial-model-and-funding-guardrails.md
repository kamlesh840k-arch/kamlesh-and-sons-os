## Funding concept

- Proposed total bank-facility ceiling: NPR 1 crore
- Proposed distributor bank-guarantee ceiling: NPR 60 lakh
- Proposed cash-credit/working-capital and/or term-loan ceiling: NPR 40 lakh; exact bank-approved product split remains open
- Recommended initial total utilisation: NPR 60–70 lakh
- Recommended initial BG-supported anchor/halo handset exposure: NPR 30–35 lakh
- Recommended undrawn/liquidity protection: NPR 30–40 lakh

The founder currently intends the BG side for approved authorised anchor/halo distributors such as Apple, Samsung, Nothing or equivalent relationships, and the NPR 40 lakh facility/equity mix for eligible short-cycle inventory and operations plus separately structured longer-lived fit-out/startup needs. The bank and each distributor must confirm product, permitted use and eligibility; naming a brand here does not establish supply, credit or BG acceptance.

A bank guarantee is a contingent liability, not free stock capital. Invocation creates an obligation to the bank. Commission, collateral/margin, expiry, renewal and claim procedures must be modelled separately.

## Finance-to-asset matching

- Term loan funds durable fit-out, electrical work, AC, power backup, security and service equipment.
- Cash-credit/approved working-capital funds only eligible short-cycle working capital and timing gaps. Durable fit-out should use equity or a bank-permitted term-loan structure matched to asset life; obtain written permitted-use confirmation.
- BG supports authorised distributor purchases under documented credit and settlement terms.
- Slow or premium handset inventory is not justified merely because the facility is available.

## Minimum controls

- Debt-service coverage of at least 1.5 times in the approved base case
- Interest sensitivity at the sanctioned rate and two percentage points higher
- Downside case with sales 25% below target for six months
- Six months of fixed-cost and interest protection
- No brand above one-third of handset capital
- No more than 10% of handset capital above NPR 100,000
- Weekly open-to-buy and stock-ageing approval
- Daily IMEI stock reconciliation
- Separate purchase approval, receiving, payment and reconciliation where practical
- Written authority for discounts, refunds, write-offs and emergency purchases
- No use of debt or BG for a brand until authorised-channel status, warranty route, invoicing, settlement, price protection and stock-rotation exposure are documented

## Expansion and stop rules

Increase inventory utilisation only if:

- Thirty-day sell-through exceeds 70% for the relevant category.
- Total ageing remains below 45 days.
- Effective realised margin meets the approved model.
- Supplier credit notes arrive within the agreed period.
- Cash and debt-service forecasts remain inside limits.

Freeze discretionary purchasing if:

- The store remains below 70 phones per month after month four.
- More than 10% of handset units exceed 45 days.
- Any unexplained IMEI variance remains open.
- A distributor protection term is disputed or not honoured.
- The downside liquidity floor is breached.
