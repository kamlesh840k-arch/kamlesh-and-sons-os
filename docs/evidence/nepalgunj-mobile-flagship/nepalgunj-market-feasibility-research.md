**Accountable owner:** Founder until Research/Commercial Owner is appointed

## Decision summary

Nepalgunj is a conditional-go market for an authorised, phone-led multi-brand technology flagship. The recommended concept is value-led Android volume, selective premium phones, an order-led laptop range, an attachment-led accessories range and a visible after-sales Service Lounge. A generic phone-only, premium-only or inventory-heavy laptop store is not supported by the evidence.

Proceed only when field validation demonstrates a credible route to at least 70 handset sales per month by month three, effective handset margin near 5% or better, controlled rent, six months of liquidity protection and written distributor safeguards. Laptop and accessory opening capital must be approved separately after local sell-through, attachment, margin, warranty and stock-rotation evidence is obtained.

## Evidence quality rule

- **Observed:** directly reported by an identified public source.
- **Calculated:** arithmetic derived from observed inputs.
- **Planning assumption:** a provisional commercial value requiring a quotation or approval.
- **Hypothesis:** a proposition to test through local fieldwork.

No public authoritative source reports Nepalgunj store-level units or model-level sell-through. Any local bestseller ranking in this package is an evidence-based stocking hypothesis, not an audited fact.

## Addressable market

- The 2021 census reports Nepalgunj at 164,444 people and 34,565 households.
- Banke district has 603,194 people and 129,307 households.
- Nepalgunj has 86,001 residents aged 15–44, approximately 52.3% of the population.
- Nepalgunj household smartphone access was 73.9% in the 2021 census.
- The same census recorded computer/laptop access in 6,347 of 34,565 Nepalgunj households, or 18.4%, and home internet in 16,335 households, or 47.3%.
- The 2024–25 national household survey reports smartphone access in 91.1% of Lumbini urban households and home internet access in 90.2%.

The city figure and later province-urban figure are not directly comparable samples. The local figures are household access/installed-base indicators, not annual device sales. Together they indicate a mature phone replacement-and-upgrade market and a materially narrower, but still meaningful, base for computers and laptops.

Local household source: [NSO NPHC 2021 Table 10 household-facilities dataset](https://censusresults.nsonepal.gov.np/files/longform-dataset/Hhld10_HouseholdFacility.csv). In that table, the relevant columns are smart mobile phone, computer/laptop and internet at home.

## Regional role

Nepalgunj is a western gateway with road and air connections serving a wider Banke, Karnali and western-Nepal catchment. Regional movement supports a larger commercial opportunity than the city population alone, but airport and transport counts are not retail-footfall measurements.

## Affordability

The NSO Living Standards Survey reported Lumbini Urban poverty incidence at 24.08% in 2022/23. High smartphone access therefore coexists with strong price sensitivity. The commercial centre of gravity should remain below NPR 30,000, supported by financing, repair, trade-in and accessories.

## Scale check

Applying the national FY 2025/26 smartphone-import rate mechanically to Nepalgunj's census population produces approximately 12,800 smartphones per year, or 1,065 per month, across the city. This is only a calculated scale check: it is not observed local demand and does not adjust for regional visitors, grey-market devices, existing inventory or competitor share.

A new store selling 70–90 phones per month would require roughly 7–8% of this city-only pro-rata quantity. That is demanding but potentially achievable for a differentiated regional flagship; distributor data and field counts must validate it.

## Laptop demand and opening role

**Verified facts:** Nepalgunj's 2021 household computer/laptop access was 18.4%; national FY 2025/26 laptop/notebook imports were 154,991 pieces worth NPR 11.14 billion. Neither source reports current Nepalgunj purchases, brand share or model-level sell-through.

**Opening hypothesis:** laptops should broaden the flagship's relevance without becoming its main working-capital bet. Use working segments only as field-research strata, not accepted demand shares:

| Research segment | Customer job to test | Opening treatment until validated |
|---|---|---|
| Student/value | Study, browsing, office work and first laptop | Compare at least one authorised Windows option and one order-led alternative; validate financing need and service expectations |
| Mainstream work/business | Reliability, battery, portability, invoicing and rapid support | Small demonstration range plus rapid authorised fulfilment |
| Creator/gaming/performance | GPU, thermal performance, display and upgradeability | Demonstration or advance order; no speculative depth |
| Premium/ultraportable | Build, ecosystem, battery and status | Halo demonstration and advance order unless repeat local demand is documented |

HP, Dell, Lenovo, Asus and Apple are brand candidates, not an approved opening allocation. For each proposed model, the commercial owner must record authorised source, landed invoice cost, effective margin, comparable local price, demo support, DOA window, warranty route, lead time, price protection and stock-rotation terms.

No laptop demand share, average selling price, monthly volume or opening stock quantity is accepted yet. Replace those gaps through the field programme below.

## Accessories demand and opening role

Accessories can improve basket value, solve immediate ownership needs and reduce dependence on thin handset margin. National import volumes confirm active charger, power-bank and audio categories, but do not validate Nepalgunj attachment rates or K&S margins.

The opening range should follow the existing [accessory sourcing and assortment gate](./distributor-system/supply-service/accessory-sourcing-and-assortment.md): original accessories where ecosystem trust matters, reputable compatible products with written specifications and warranty, and controlled generic products only after safety and quality checks.

**Hypotheses to test by transaction and competitor basket:**

- Cases and screen protection should attach most often to new-phone sales.
- Certified chargers, cables and power banks can win on safety explanation, compatibility and replacement clarity rather than lowest price.
- Audio, storage, laptop bags, mice, keyboards, hubs and adapters should be ranged by demonstrated basket demand, not category breadth.
- Installation, compatibility checking and written warranty can support conversion, but willingness to pay must be measured.

The current 65% accessory-attachment target and 35–45% blended gross-margin range remain planning assumptions. They are not market findings.

## Local category-sizing and validation method

Public sources cannot produce defensible Nepalgunj annual unit demand. The minimum evidence package is therefore:

1. **Comparable sell-through:** eight to ten distributor/retailer discussions covering the same last 30 and 90 days, separated into phones, laptops, tablets and accessory families; label system exports, invoices, verbal recall and estimates separately.
2. **Standard competitor basket:** the 12-phone basket plus at least three comparable laptops and six accessories across Nepalgunj, Kohalpur, Rupaidiha and selected Kathmandu benchmarks. Record price, stock, invoice, warranty, EMI, delivery, setup and repair handling.
3. **Customer demand:** 100 deduplicated interviews covering current phone and laptop ownership, replacement timing, device job, budget, cash/EMI preference, brand, official-versus-border choice, accessory basket and service concerns.
4. **Institutional demand:** at least twelve structured interviews across schools/campuses, hospitals/clinics, NGOs/INGOs and local SMEs. Record annual device count, procurement route, VAT/tender needs, configuration, support SLA, payment terms and replacement cycle; do not combine institutional and consumer demand without showing the split.
5. **Service signal:** ten recent phone/laptop service cases and repair-partner records for common failures, parts lead time, data/privacy concerns, repeat failure and customer downtime.
6. **Pre-launch proof:** controlled, refundable advance-order interest or an explicitly non-binding waitlist by category and price band. Interest is not counted as a sale.
7. **Post-opening learning:** weekly units, gross margin, days in stock, lost-sale reason, accessory attachment, return/DOA, warranty case and price-match pressure by SKU.

Convert these inputs into low/base/high cases with every source dated. Do not infer Nepalgunj demand by applying national import share alone.

## Competitive structure

The primary retail cluster is the Dhamboji Chowk–Setu B.K. Chowk–New Road/Ratna Rajmarg corridor. Identified competitors include Sanmati Electronics, Kantipur Electronics/Plaza, Saba Mobiles, Royal Karnali Electro Mart and Apple Hub. Samsung, Xiaomi and vivo after-sales infrastructure is also present in or near the corridor.

A secondary cluster exists near Pushpalal Chowk and Bus Park Road. Public directories expose at least fourteen mobile-store entries, but directory presence, ratings and promotions are not evidence of current sales volume.

Existing stores already advertise broad brand availability, accessories, EMI and delivery. The stronger unverified opportunity is a store built around visible MDMS/VAT/warranty proof, transparent repair intake, dependable updates, data-safe setup and later a controlled certified trade-in programme.

## Border and grey-market risk

Nepalgunj buyers can compare with Rupaidiha and informal channels. Strict customs enforcement at Jamunaha in April 2026 reduced cross-border shopping and benefited formal Nepali trade, but enforcement intensity can change. The business must compete through authorised stock, clear warranty, service and trust rather than assuming the border advantage will persist.

NTA states that buying or selling an unregistered mobile device is illegal. Every received and sold device must be traceable through supplier invoice, IMEI/serial record and MDMS validation.

## Store positioning

Recommended position: **Nepalgunj's trusted authorised technology and service destination**.

The promise is supported by:

- IMEI and MDMS validation in front of the customer
- VAT invoice and authorised Nepal warranty
- Needs-led comparisons rather than pressure selling
- Setup, transfer, software and warranty assistance
- Written exchange, warranty and repair terms
- Repair job cards, condition evidence and agreed updates
- WhatsApp catalogue, advance orders, local delivery and pickup/drop
- KAMLESH Care membership with explicit benefit limits

## Financial feasibility summary

Planning assumptions requiring written validation:

- Authorised handset gross margin: 4.5–7%; use 5.5% base case
- Blended accessories gross margin: 35–45%
- Laptop margin and monthly demand: unapproved; obtain current authorised quotations and local sell-through before modelling
- Repair/setup contribution margin: 50–70% after technician payout
- Average handset selling price: approximately NPR 29,000–30,500
- Prime showroom rent: obtain five actual quotations; do not use an online estimate as approval evidence

At NPR 29,000 and 5.5% handset margin, gross profit is approximately NPR 1,595 per phone. A NPR 50,000 monthly rent consumes the handset gross profit from about 31 phones before payroll, utilities, marketing, losses or financing.

For a fully loaded monthly cost of NPR 1.8–2.6 lakh, an early blended margin near 9% implies break-even around 62–90 phones per month. A stronger mix of accessories, membership and service can improve the blended result, but must not be assumed before evidence exists.

## Conditional go/no-go

### Proceed only if

- Effective handset margin is at least approximately 5% after promotion obligations.
- Price protection or stock rotation is documented.
- Rent and fixed costs keep break-even below 90 phones per month.
- Field evidence supports 70 or more phones per month by month three.
- Six months of liquidity protection is available.
- Authorised sourcing, MDMS, invoicing, warranty and customer-device custody controls are operational.
- Laptop and accessory allocations pass documented margin, warranty, lead-time, aging and stock-rotation gates.

### Do not proceed if

- Rent exceeds NPR 70,000 without exceptional verified traffic and economics.
- Effective handset margin is below 4.5%.
- The full NPR 1 crore facility must be utilised immediately.
- Premium inventory is required merely to make shelves look full.
- Laptop breadth or accessory variety is used to justify inventory without comparable local sell-through and attachment evidence.
- Distributor protection remains verbal.
- The concept depends primarily on being cheaper than grey-market or Indian sellers.

## Research limitations

- No audited Nepalgunj model-level or store-level sell-through was located.
- Online competitor listings may be stale or duplicated.
- Rent, payroll and distributor commercial terms remain unverified.
- National imports are not identical to retail sales.
- Customs accessory categories mix quality tiers and, in some cases, wider product uses; they cannot set an opening assortment by themselves.
- Province-level household and income evidence is not a Nepalgunj-specific sales forecast.
- The 2021 computer/laptop installed-base measure does not establish 2026 replacement demand.

The field-validation plan converts these limitations into required evidence before the founder's go/no-go decision.
