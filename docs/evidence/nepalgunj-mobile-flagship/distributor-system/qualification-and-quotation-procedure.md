# Distributor Qualification and Quotation Procedure

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-DIST-001

## One process for every brand

The Store Manager creates one row in the Qualification sheet and one controlled folder before the first meeting. The distributor receives the same written request regardless of brand.

## Phase 1 — identity and authority

Collect the distributor's legal name, registration/PAN/VAT evidence, authorised brands, official contact, authorisation certificate and brand/principal verification contact. Confirm whether KAMLESH may operate as a multi-brand retailer, sell through WhatsApp/social channels and use brand marks.

**Stop condition:** do not request opening stock when authorisation, VAT/MDMS compliance or the intended sales channel cannot be verified.

## Phase 2 — standard commercial quotation

Require a dated written response containing:

| Area | Required response |
|---|---|
| Opening | Minimum opening order, display units, model/colour requirements and showroom conditions |
| Margin | Base invoice margin by category, back-end incentives, calculation base and settlement timing |
| Credit | Limit, days, due-date basis, early-payment benefit, overdue charges and increase conditions |
| BG | Required amount, limit generated, invocation triggers/notice, expiry, renewal and release |
| Targets | Monthly/quarterly purchase and sell-out targets; consequence of missing them |
| Price protection | Eligible stock/age, display stock, price/cashback events, proof and credit-note time |
| Rotation | Eligible stock, ageing period, percentage/quantity, fee, freight and credit-note time |
| DOA | Definition, retailer/customer deadline, diagnosis, replacement standard and turnaround |
| Warranty | Start date, exclusions, Nepalgunj service path, turnaround, escalation and repeat repair |
| Supply | MOQ, ordering method, normal/launch lead time, allocation, freight and transit risk |
| Marketing | Fixtures, display phones, launch stock, content approval, reimbursement and expiry |
| Channels | Territory, nearby appointments, direct online pricing, pickup/fulfilment and restrictions |
| Exit | Term, renewal, termination, cure period, stock return and outstanding claim treatment |

## Phase 3 — evidence pack

Request the authorisation certificate, draft agreement, price/margin schedule, credit/BG terms, incentive schedule, price-protection policy, rotation policy, DOA policy, warranty/service list, supply policy, display agreement, marketing rules and escalation contacts.

Record each item in Documents Contacts with its source, confidentiality, verification, effective date and review/expiry date.

## Twelve-phone quotation basket

Each qualified distributor should quote comparable models across these bands:

- Three models below NPR 20,000.
- Three models from NPR 20,000–35,000.
- Three models from NPR 35,000–60,000.
- Two models from NPR 60,000–100,000.
- One model above NPR 100,000 where the brand participates.

For every model capture purchase price, MRP, margin, incentive, credit days, stock availability, delivery time, price protection, DOA and warranty path. Do not compare brands using only headline margin.

## Qualification decision

- **Approved:** all mandatory gates pass and detailed scoring meets the approval threshold.
- **Conditional:** mandatory gates pass, score is acceptable, and named conditions have owners and dates.
- **Rejected:** any mandatory gate fails or commercial risk is unacceptable.
- **Pending:** information is incomplete; no purchase commitment is permitted.
