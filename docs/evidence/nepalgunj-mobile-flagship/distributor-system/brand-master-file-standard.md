# Brand-Specific Master-File Standard

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-DIST-003

## Required structure

Create one restricted folder per approved distributor relationship:

```text
Brand - Distributor/
├── 01 Authorization and Identity/
├── 02 Agreement and Amendments/
├── 03 Credit and BG/
├── 04 Price Lists and Margins/
├── 05 Incentives and Claims/
├── 06 Price Protection and Rotation/
├── 07 DOA Warranty and Service/
├── 08 Orders Invoices and Deliveries/
├── 09 Marketing Displays and Launches/
├── 10 Reviews and Escalations/
└── 99 Superseded - Read Only/
```

## Master summary

The Brand Master sheet records the controlling relationship data: distributor identity; sales, accounts and service contacts; agreement and authorisation dates; credit/BG terms; targets; ordering/delivery; DOA deadline; policy links; escalation; owner; status and last review.

## File rules

- Use `YYYY-MM-DD_Brand_Document-Type_Version` names.
- Preserve the signed original as read-only.
- Mark superseded schedules; never silently overwrite them.
- Record the effective date, not merely the upload date.
- Restrict BG, bank, personal and confidential commercial records.
- Keep an audit trail for changes to credit, margin, price protection and claims.
- Test links during the monthly review.

## One-page staff summary

For operational use, maintain a current summary with ordering contact, payment days, DOA deadline, warranty path, price-protection steps, rotation deadline and escalation contacts. Staff use this summary; only authorised personnel access full agreements.

## Review frequency

- Weekly: price list, payment exposure, open orders and claims.
- Monthly: contacts, targets, incentives, stock ageing and performance.
- Quarterly: relationship score, credit/BG utilisation and commercial competitiveness.
- On every circular: record new effective terms and archive the replaced version.
- Before expiry: legal/commercial renewal review with sufficient notice.
