# Apple Accessory Merchandising and Storage Plan

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-SUP-003

## Decision

KAMLESH will cover every common Apple customer need, but will not stock every accessory, colour or device generation. Open with approximately **45–65 core Apple accessory SKUs**, then expand from actual sales. One display sample/facing per SKU is normally enough.

## Sourcing

- Original Apple: Generation Next Communications, subject to current written authority and reseller terms.
- Premium compatible: Belkin through GenXT and other verifiable certified suppliers.
- Reliable value: approved Nepal accessory suppliers after sample and warranty testing.
- Cases/glass: two verified wholesale sources, purchased by exact model and sales velocity.

Apple states that iPhone 15 and later use USB-C; older iPhones and accessories sustain Lightning demand. Maintain both. Source: https://support.apple.com/en-us/105099.

## Core opening assortment

### Charging and connection

- Original and premium USB-C power adapters.
- USB-C to USB-C, USB-C to Lightning, limited USB-A to Lightning and USB-A to USB-C cables.
- MagSafe and magnetic charging.
- Standard and magnetic power banks.
- Car chargers and selected travel/multi-port chargers.
- USB-C to 3.5mm, HDMI/multiport, card-reader and other common adapters.

### Protection

- For high-volume iPhones: clear, MagSafe clear, silicone, rugged and limited premium cases.
- Standard/full-cover glass, limited privacy/premium glass and camera protection.
- iPad folio/rugged cases and glass only for stocked models.
- Watch bumpers/glass and common band sizes.
- AirPods cases and basic cleaning products.

### Ecosystem

- Current official AirPods range in shallow quantities.
- USB-C and limited Lightning wired audio/adapters.
- Apple Watch charge cables/stands and limited bands.
- iPad Pencil/stylus, keyboard and hub options, with expensive versions advance-order.
- MacBook sleeves, hubs, HDMI/card adapters, cables, stands and limited input devices.
- AirTag and a limited range of holders.

## Display plan for an 800–1,200 sq ft flagship

Allocate about **10–14 linear feet of wall/display**, not a full stockroom wall.

| Fixture/zone | Display method | Backstock method |
|---|---|---|
| Apple charging wall, 4–5 ft | One facing per adapter/cable/MagSafe SKU; organised by connector and power | Small barcode bins by SKU |
| iPhone protection wall, 5–7 ft | Hooks labelled by exact phone model; 1–2 visible pieces per design | Model-labelled bins/drawers |
| Premium glass cabinet, 2–3 ft | AirPods, premium power, docks, Pencil and high-value items | Locked stock with controlled count |
| Screen-protection sample board | Show four quality levels and fitted examples | Flat-file drawers by model; do not hang all stock |
| Service Lounge | One sample, compatibility guide and fitting desk | Installation consumables in staff-only drawers |

## Stockroom requirement

Reserve approximately **60–100 sq ft** of secure back-of-house storage for all phone/accessory categories. Apple accessories should occupy one lockable shelving bay plus flat drawers:

- Numbered shelf bins for chargers, cables and utility accessories.
- Flat drawers for glass, film and camera protection.
- Model-labelled case bins: `Brand > Model > Case type > Colour`.
- Locked cage/cabinet for AirPods, Pencil, docks and other high-value stock.
- Separate quarantine bin for returns, damaged packaging and warranty cases.
- Separate demo assets; demos are never sale stock until formally converted.

## Visual-merchandising rules

1. Display one item/facing, not the entire quantity.
2. Display by customer problem: Charge, Protect, Connect, Listen and Travel.
3. Mark connector, power, compatible models, certification/status, warranty and price.
4. Use no more than four principal case colours initially: clear, black, blue and one seasonal colour.
5. Do not mix Lightning and USB-C without strong labels.
6. Keep high-value items locked but visibly represented.
7. Use QR/WhatsApp catalogue for colours and rare variants not physically displayed.

## Inventory controls

Every SKU needs barcode, exact compatibility, supplier, cost, selling price, warranty, location, minimum, maximum and reorder quantity. Model-specific products cannot be received as generic descriptions such as “iPhone cover.”

Suggested min/max logic:

- Fast adapters/cables: minimum 3–5, maximum 10–15 per core SKU.
- Common glass/clear cases: minimum 3–5, maximum 8–15 per model.
- Premium cases/power banks: minimum 1–2, maximum 3–5.
- AirPods/Pencil/docks: minimum 0–1, maximum 2–3 unless sales prove more.
- Rare colours/specialist Mac/iPad products: advance order.

Weekly cycle-count all locked Apple stock and top accessories; fortnightly count case/glass bins; monthly review ageing and delete inactive SKUs from reorder lists.

## Customer-order solution

The WhatsApp catalogue carries the extended range. Collect an advance for rare model/colour items, confirm compatibility in writing, give an estimated delivery date and do not promise availability before supplier confirmation.
