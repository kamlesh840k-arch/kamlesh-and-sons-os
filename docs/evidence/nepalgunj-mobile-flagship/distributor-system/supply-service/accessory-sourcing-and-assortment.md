### Channel 3 — controlled generic protection products

Covers and screen protectors are fragmented wholesale categories. Use one verified Kathmandu importer/wholesaler and one western-Nepal backup. Visit the wholesale market, inspect actual stock and collect written quotations. Do not treat marketplace or directory listings as proof of quality.

## Opening category rules

| Category | Range strategy | Stock rule |
|---|---|---|
| Chargers/cables | Original + premium + reliable value | No unbranded electrical products |
| Power banks | Two quality/price tiers | Battery documentation and written warranty required |
| Earbuds/audio | Original, recognised local and value | Display/demo controlled; avoid duplicate brands |
| Covers | Clear, slim, rugged, premium, limited wallet | Depth only for phones actually stocked or locally common |
| Screen protection | Standard, full-cover, privacy and limited premium | Store in model-labelled drawers; one sample board |
| Camera protectors | High-volume iPhone/Samsung/Redmi | Shallow stock and rapid replenishment |
| Car/desk/travel | Small tested assortment | Reorder from sales, not speculative depth |
| Wearables | Original and one recognised value brand | High-value stock locked; demo separate |

## Electrical-product gate

Before approving chargers, cables or power banks, record supplier/importer, VAT invoice, batch/model, input/output, supported charging protocols, safety documentation, protection features, written warranty/replacement, authenticity method and Nepal-compatible plug. For Apple-controlled technologies, verify relevant certification through https://mfi.apple.com/account/accessory-search.

## Supplier test order

Begin each non-original supplier with a small representative order. Test physical finish, connector fit, charging output, heat, cable durability, packaging accuracy, warranty response, delivery accuracy and return handling. Expand only after the trial passes.

## Replenishment discipline

- A-items: top chargers, cables, common glass and clear cases — review weekly.
- B-items: premium cases, power banks, popular audio — review fortnightly.
- C-items: rare cases, colours, specialist adapters — advance order or monthly review.
- Stop reordering a model-specific item when related handset sales cease or ageing threshold is reached.
