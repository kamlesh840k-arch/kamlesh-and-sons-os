## Governing decisions

1. The showroom is multi-brand; distributor authorisation and Nepal warranty must be verified before commitment.
2. Exact internal financing limits are confidential and excluded from supplier-facing requests.
3. Accessories use three channels: original brand, reputable compatible brand and controlled generic protection products.
4. One display facing does not mean deep stock. Backstock, reorder points and advance orders prevent visual clutter and dead stock.
5. KAMLESH opens with a Service Lounge and controlled repair intake, not an unrestricted full repair laboratory.
6. Public distributor, service and contact information can change. Signed current evidence replaces this research before purchase.
