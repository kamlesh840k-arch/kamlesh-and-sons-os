## Current assessment

Nepalgunj has credible authorised coverage for Samsung and Xiaomi and reported Vivo service, plus many independent technicians. Public evidence does not support calling the city a consistently professional, full multi-brand repair ecosystem.

## Opening service model

KAMLESH opens with setup, data transfer, software support, warranty assistance, cleaning, basic diagnosis, repair booking and pickup/drop. Warranty repairs follow the brand-authorised route. Independent repairs go only to an audited trial partner. A full in-house technician/laboratory is added only after demand, controls and economics are proven.

## Real-world mystery-shop conversation

Use a normal customer conversation, not a corporate audit:

1. `दाइ, यो मोबाइल यहीँ बन्छ कि बाहिर पठाउनुपर्छ?`
2. `कति जति खर्च आउँछ? खोल्नुभन्दा अगाडि भन्नुहोला है।`
3. `सस्तो र राम्रो दुवै option छ?`
4. `यो company वाला हो कि local वाला? दुवैको price कति हो?`
5. `आजै बन्छ कि भोलि? पक्का कति बजे आउँदा हुन्छ?`
6. `केही दिनमै यही problem आयो भने फेरि हेर्नुहुन्छ? कति दिनसम्म?`
7. `नबने पनि checking charge लाग्छ?`
8. `फेरेको पुरानो part फिर्ता दिनुहोला है।`
9. `फोनमा photo/data छ, delete त हुँदैन नि? Password दिनैपर्छ?`
10. `मोबाइल छोड्दा receipt/slip वा WhatsApp मा detail दिनुहुन्छ?`
11. `यो model पहिले बनाउनु भएको छ? यसको part पाइन्छ?`

## Observe without asking

Positive: phone checked in front of customer, price/time explained, permission before replacement, devices labelled separately, receipt/WhatsApp acknowledgement, organised bench, old parts returned, password not demanded unnecessarily and repeat issue handled.

Warning: devices piled together, phone opened before price discussion, every part called “original,” old part withheld, unnecessary password request, no acknowledgement, price changed after opening, device sent elsewhere without notice or careless heat/glue/tool use.

## Simple score

Score 0–2 for problem explanation, price before work, time, honest part choices, receipt/record, repair assurance, phone handling, password/data behaviour, customer treatment and partnership potential. Maximum 20:

- 16–20: trial partnership candidate.
- 11–15: selected repairs only.
- 0–10: do not refer KAMLESH customers.

## Partnership conversation after the customer test

Ask whether they can accept regular referrals, provide an estimate before work, confirm price through WhatsApp, give normal turnaround, recheck a repeated fault, offer a regular-work rate, arrange pickup/drop and accept responsibility for loss or additional damage. Document the agreed trial; do not depend on verbal assurances for customer devices.

Before appointment, obtain a written SLA covering legal identity, brand/technician authorisation, accepted jobs, diagnostic fee, genuine/alternative-parts wording, estimate variance, customer approval, turnaround by job class, parts delay, repeat-fault remedy, old-parts return, data-access method, subcontracting, custody/transport, loss/additional damage, status updates, invoice/VAT, insurance, disputes, audit and termination.

For at least three supervised workflow simulations, calculate:

`Case contribution = customer fee + disclosed partner commission/markup − staff/communication time − courier/pickup − payment cost − rework/refund reserve − custody/privacy incident reserve`

Track promised versus actual days, first-time resolution, estimate changes, no-fault-found cases, customer approvals, reopen/repeat failure, partner rejection, unrecovered cost and status-update compliance. Do not approve a partner solely from the 20-point customer-conversation score.
