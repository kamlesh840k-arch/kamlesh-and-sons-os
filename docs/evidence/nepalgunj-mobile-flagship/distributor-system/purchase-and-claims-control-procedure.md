# Purchase and Claims Control Procedure

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-DIST-004

**Version:** 1.1

**Last updated:** 2026-08-15

## Purchase gate

Before a purchase order is approved, verify:

1. Distributor and brand are approved or the Owner has documented a one-time exception.
2. Current price, margin, incentive and policy version are attached.
3. Expected sell-through occurs on or before the payment due date, with forecast sold-cost and unrestricted cash due cover recorded.
4. Brand/category inventory and BG limits remain available.
5. Existing ageing, claims and overdue payments are reviewed.
6. Model, colour, quantity, delivery and launch promises are supported by evidence.
7. The named approver has authority for the order value.
8. Title/control, payment trigger, tax/invoice and insurance treatment match the approved structure; consignment stock is not silently treated as K&S-owned purchase stock.
9. The order remains viable with unearned/unreceived rebate, MDF and price-protection claims set to zero in the downside cash case.
10. BG/security availability, invocation exposure and the founder-approved liquidity buffer remain within limits.

## Receiving

Cashier/Inventory Controller matches PO, invoice and delivered units; records both IMEIs where applicable; checks seal, model, colour, storage and physical condition; quarantines discrepancies; and reports shortages/transit damage within the distributor deadline. The person receiving stock cannot alone approve the related payment.

## Calendar ownership

The Purchase Calendar records PO/invoice value, VAT-exclusive landed cost, credit days, calculated due date, delivery, BG/security use, facility availability, rotation and price-protection dates, expected sold-cost by due date, unrestricted cash due cover, payment/delivery status, evidence and responsibility.

Review exceptions weekly:

- Payment due in seven days.
- Overdue payment.
- Sell-through forecast later than payment due date.
- Due-date sell-through cover below 1.00 or cash due cover below the founder-approved threshold.
- BG utilisation approaching the bank/owner limit.
- Delivery shortage or damage.
- Stock approaching rotation or price-protection deadlines.

## Claims

Create a Claims Register entry immediately when a qualifying event occurs. Claim types include price protection, incentive, DOA, rotation, marketing reimbursement, warranty recovery and other credit.

Every claim must have a unique ID, related PO/invoice, IMEI/SKU/model where applicable, program/policy version, preapproval reference, event and deadline dates, evidence checklist, amount, K&S spend, distributor reference, owner, status, settlement expectation, tax/accounting classification and escalation history.

Unreceived claims are not available cash. The downside cash forecast and supplier-payment approval use zero for an unreceived claim unless the accountant confirms it is legally nettable against the same due invoice. Purchase-price rebates, selling-expense reimbursements and other credits must be classified separately.

## Consignment and sell-through stock

Consignment/sell-through inventory must use a distinct ownership status and IMEI/SKU reconciliation. Record supplier control/title, deemed-sale events, sale report, settlement due, supplier recall, K&S return, physical count, damage/theft, insurance and final reconciliation. Do not include supplier-controlled stock in K&S inventory or borrowing-base reports unless the accountant and contract confirm that treatment.

## Claim segregation

- Service Lounge Adviser prepares DOA/warranty evidence.
- Digital Coordinator prepares approved marketing evidence.
- Store Manager reviews commercial eligibility and submits/escalates.
- Accountant reconciles credit notes/payments.
- Owner approves settlements, waivers and material disputes.

The preparer cannot mark their own claim settled without independent financial reconciliation.

## Weekly review

The Owner and accountant review payments, due-date/cash cover, facility and BG/security usage, overdue deliveries, ageing stock, claims due within three days, rejected/disputed claims, unreceived credit notes, support realisation and consignment reconciliation. Decisions and assigned actions are recorded.
