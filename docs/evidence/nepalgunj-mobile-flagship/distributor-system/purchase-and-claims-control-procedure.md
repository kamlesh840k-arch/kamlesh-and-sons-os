# Purchase and Claims Control Procedure

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-DIST-004

## Purchase gate

Before a purchase order is approved, verify:

1. Distributor and brand are approved or the Owner has documented a one-time exception.
2. Current price, margin, incentive and policy version are attached.
3. Expected sell-through occurs on or before the payment due date.
4. Brand/category inventory and BG limits remain available.
5. Existing ageing, claims and overdue payments are reviewed.
6. Model, colour, quantity, delivery and launch promises are supported by evidence.
7. The named approver has authority for the order value.

## Receiving

Cashier/Inventory Controller matches PO, invoice and delivered units; records both IMEIs where applicable; checks seal, model, colour, storage and physical condition; quarantines discrepancies; and reports shortages/transit damage within the distributor deadline. The person receiving stock cannot alone approve the related payment.

## Calendar ownership

The Purchase Calendar records PO/invoice value, credit days, calculated due date, delivery, BG use, rotation and price-protection dates, expected sell-through, payment/delivery status, evidence and responsibility.

Review exceptions weekly:

- Payment due in seven days.
- Overdue payment.
- Sell-through forecast later than payment due date.
- BG utilisation approaching the bank/owner limit.
- Delivery shortage or damage.
- Stock approaching rotation or price-protection deadlines.

## Claims

Create a Claims Register entry immediately when a qualifying event occurs. Claim types include price protection, incentive, DOA, rotation, marketing reimbursement, warranty recovery and other credit.

Every claim must have a unique ID, related PO/invoice, IMEI/SKU/model where applicable, event and deadline dates, evidence checklist, amount, distributor reference, owner, status, settlement expectation and escalation history.

## Claim segregation

- Service Lounge Adviser prepares DOA/warranty evidence.
- Digital Coordinator prepares approved marketing evidence.
- Store Manager reviews commercial eligibility and submits/escalates.
- Accountant reconciles credit notes/payments.
- Owner approves settlements, waivers and material disputes.

The preparer cannot mark their own claim settled without independent financial reconciliation.

## Weekly review

The Owner and accountant review payments, BG usage, overdue deliveries, ageing stock, claims due within three days, rejected/disputed claims and unreceived credit notes. Decisions and assigned actions are recorded.
