## Purpose

This system lets KAMLESH qualify, compare, appoint and manage several brand distributors without relying on memory, chat messages or inconsistent negotiations. The same gates apply to every distributor.

This distributor subsystem isolates NPR 70 lakh of the broader NPR 1 crore planning ceiling: NPR 60 lakh of BG-supported distributor purchasing plus NPR 10 lakh of proposed bank-approved cash-credit/working-capital capacity. The remaining broader ceiling is not additional distributor-purchasing authority and must be structured separately for approved fit-out/startup uses and liquidity protection. None of these figures is a bank sanction, distributor commitment or purchase authority. A BG is a guarantee, not cash: usable supplier credit depends on the distributor agreement and bank sanction. Working-capital capacity should protect eligible short-cycle liquidity and operating continuity; it must not automatically be exhausted on opening stock. Nepal Rastra Bank's current FAQ says overdraft-type lending under the Working Capital Loan Guidelines is limited to natural persons, so the exact company facility and permitted uses require written bank confirmation.

## Recommended opening portfolio

| Tier | Brands | Opening approach |
|---|---|---|
| A — volume anchors | Samsung; Xiaomi/Redmi | Negotiate first; broad but controlled assortment subject to terms and local demand |
| A — premium halo | Apple | Selective fast-moving models; official supply and warranty path required |
| B — offline/value strength | Select one or two of Vivo, OPPO and Realme | Award meaningful stock only after comparing margins, credit, sell-through and service |
| C — order-led/testing | Nothing, Infinix/itel, HONOR, Motorola/OnePlus | Small tests, advance orders or replenishment-led stock until demand is proven |

This is a negotiation sequence, not a claim that every listed brand will approve KAMLESH or be commercially suitable.

## Mandatory gates

A distributor cannot be approved unless KAMLESH has evidence of:

1. Verifiable authorisation and permission for the intended retail model.
2. VAT invoicing and MDMS-compliant devices.
3. Written DOA and Nepal warranty handling.
4. No undisclosed or commercially harmful exclusivity.
5. Acceptable agreement, credit and BG terms reviewed by qualified advisers.

A strong margin cannot override a failed mandatory gate.

## Ownership

- Owner: final distributor, BG, exclusivity and exception approval.
- Store Manager: coordinator, master-file owner and purchase workflow owner.
- Accountant/finance adviser: credit, payment, VAT, BG and credit-note reconciliation.
- Lawyer: dealership, liability, termination, dispute and guarantee review.
- Cashier/Inventory Controller: receiving, IMEI, invoice and inventory records.
- Service Lounge Adviser: DOA and warranty claims.
- Digital Coordinator: approved marketing content and reimbursement claims.

## Information governance

The repository contains standards and non-sensitive templates. Signed agreements, BG originals, bank records, personal data, passwords and commercially confidential price schedules must remain in a restricted controlled location. The workbook should link to those records rather than duplicate sensitive material.

No blank, verbal statement or “not confirmed” response constitutes approval.
