## Rating method

Score each factor from 0 to 5 only after evidence is received:

- 0 — unacceptable or disproven.
- 1 — materially weak.
- 2 — below requirement.
- 3 — acceptable.
- 4 — strong.
- 5 — exceptional and contractually supported.

## Weighting

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Margin quality | 15 |
| Credit and BG quality | 15 |
| Price protection | 15 |
| Service and warranty | 15 |
| Stock rotation | 10 |
| Supply reliability | 10 |
| Marketing/display support | 10 |
| Channel-conflict protection | 10 |
| Total | 100 |

The workbook calculates the weighted score. Mandatory authorisation, VAT/MDMS, DOA/warranty, title/accounting, exclusivity, downside cash and guarantee/security gates are separate and override the score.

## Decision bands

- 75–100: approve, provided every mandatory gate passes.
- 60–74.9: conditional approval with written corrective conditions.
- Below 60: reject or renegotiate.
- Missing ratings: pending.

## Economic normalization before scoring

Compare proposals on the same VAT-exclusive quotation basket and time horizon. Record:

- landed inventory cost after only contractually earned purchase-price reductions;
- expected collected support, separately from unreceived claims;
- foregone cash discount, BG commission/amendment/security cost, bank interest, insurance, audit, freight and administration;
- payment maturity, forecast sold-cost by due date and unrestricted cash available;
- price-protection exposure, expected claim settlement and net rotation value;
- exclusivity, fixture-space and customer-experience opportunity cost.

`Due-date sell-through cover = forecast landed cost sold by due date ÷ supplier amount due`

`Cash due cover = unrestricted cash receipts available by due date ÷ supplier amount due`

`Net support value = expected collected support − retailer spend − operating/tax/space/exit cost`

No score may treat “rolling credit” as payment-after-sale without written settlement terms, treat a BG as cash, or count an unreceived claim as available liquidity.

## Mandatory commercial gates

Place a proposal on hold, reduce exposure or reject it when:

1. Expected sell-through occurs after the invoice due date without approved downside liquidity.
2. Cash due cover is below the founder-approved threshold.
3. BG invocation/release, collateral, title/control or disputed-stock treatment is ambiguous.
4. A proposed consignment lacks clear sale/payment, recall/return, insurance, VAT/accounting and termination rules reviewed by advisers.
5. The proposal requires unconfirmed rebate/MDF support to be profitable or solvent.
6. Price protection or rotation is verbal/discretionary where material downside depends on it.
7. Promoter/data, fixture ownership/removal or exclusivity obligations create unacceptable customer, legal or space risk.

The workbook's default 1.20 cash-cover warning is a planning hypothesis, not an approved policy threshold.

## Portfolio controls

Do not give every brand a large opening order. Approve the portfolio by role:

1. Volume anchor.
2. Premium halo.
3. Offline/value alternative.
4. Order-led/test brand.

Set a maximum inventory amount, model count and ageing threshold for each approved brand. The current distributor-system scenario uses NPR 70 lakh of planned facilities: NPR 60 lakh BG-supported distributor purchasing plus NPR 10 lakh of proposed bank-approved cash-credit/working-capital capacity. This scenario is not a sanction or purchase authorization. The BG credit conversion and working-capital availability must be confirmed in signed bank and distributor terms. Preserve a founder-approved liquidity buffer instead of automatically converting all facilities into stock.

## Approval record

The Owner signs the decision summary showing selected distributor, gates, score, opening exposure, credit, BG usage, conditions, rejected alternatives and review date. Finance and legal reviews are attached where relevant.

No employee may divide a purchase into smaller orders to avoid the approval limit.
