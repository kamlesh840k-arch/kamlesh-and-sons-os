# Three-Month Distributor Implementation Plan

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-DIST-005

## Weeks 1–2 — establish authority

- Confirm legal business name, PAN/VAT, signatory and proposed site.
- Obtain written OD, term-loan and BG proposals; have the BG wording reviewed.
- Appoint the temporary distributor coordinator.
- Contact Samsung, Xiaomi/Redmi, the official Apple supply channel, Vivo, OPPO, Realme and selected test brands.
- Send the standard qualification request and create the contact/document register.

## Weeks 2–4 — collect evidence

- Verify authorisation independently.
- Obtain at least three complete commercial quotations, not merely price lists.
- Collect agreements and policies for credit/BG, price protection, rotation, DOA and warranty.
- Complete the common 12-phone basket and local competitor comparison.
- Obtain legal and finance review of material terms.

## Weeks 4–6 — select the portfolio

- Complete mandatory gates and weighted scores.
- Negotiate volume anchors first.
- Select at most one or two principal offline/value partners for opening depth.
- Keep unproven/niche brands order-led or on small test quantities.
- Approve brand and category inventory caps within NPR 50–70 lakh.

## Weeks 5–8 — establish controls

- Create every approved brand master file.
- Finalise ordering, receiving, payment, DOA, warranty and claims responsibility.
- Enter contract/authorisation expiry and claim deadlines.
- Train the Store Manager, Inventory Controller, Service Adviser, Digital Coordinator and accountant.
- Test restricted access and backup of commercial records.

## Weeks 7–10 — rehearse

- Run a sample PO from request through approval, delivery, IMEI receipt and payment scheduling.
- Run simulated DOA, price-protection and marketing claims.
- Confirm local warranty escalation and courier arrangements.
- Correct responsibility gaps, missing evidence and formula/calendar errors.

## Weeks 9–12 — phased opening stock

- Place purchase orders only for approved relationships and within sanctioned facilities.
- Stagger orders to protect liquidity and respond to price changes.
- Reconcile every delivery and display unit.
- Confirm price files, launch promotions, warranty scripts and staff product training.
- Complete an Owner-signed opening readiness review.

## Stop conditions

Delay or reduce purchasing when financing is unsigned, BG language is unacceptable, authorisation is unverified, warranty/DOA is unclear, price-protection exposure is excessive, expected sell-through exceeds payment terms, or combined commitments exceed the approved inventory envelope.
