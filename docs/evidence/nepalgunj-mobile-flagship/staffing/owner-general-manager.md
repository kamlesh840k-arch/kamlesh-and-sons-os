# Role Scorecard — Owner / General Manager

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-002  
**Role type:** Founder governance and commercial authority

## Purpose

Protect the business, set direction and ensure the flagship never trades growth for uncontrolled debt, inventory, customer harm or reputational risk.

## Accountabilities

- Approve strategy, capital, banking, BG and distributor relationships.
- Appoint owners and define delegated authority.
- Approve site, lease, fit-out, opening inventory and launch go/no-go.
- Maintain liquidity, downside plan and debt-service protection.
- Review margin, stock ageing, cash, controls, complaints and people performance.
- Ensure legal, tax, employment, privacy, safety and insurance reviews occur.
- Decide material exceptions, write-offs and customer recoveries beyond delegation.
- Preserve the repository as the operating single source of truth.

## Weekly routine

- Review sales, gross profit, cash, stock ageing and open-to-buy.
- Review distributor credit notes, overdue payables and BG exposure.
- Review major complaints, repairs, privacy/safety incidents and staff exceptions.
- Hold one manager coaching and decision meeting.
- Approve or reject the next week's inventory and marketing commitments.

## Monthly routine

- Review management accounts, bank/stock reconciliations and forecast.
- Compare actuals to base/downside plan.
- Review every brand, staff scorecard and KAMLESH Care economics.
- Approve hiring, compensation changes and material policy changes.
- Record decisions, owners, evidence and recheck dates.

## Reserved authority

- Bank borrowing, BG, guarantees and security
- Distributor contracts and credit limits
- Lease and capital expenditure
- Permanent hiring/termination unless formally delegated
- Price-policy exceptions above approved limits
- Material refund, write-off, compensation or settlement
- Public statements on legal, safety, privacy or reputational incidents

## Prohibited behaviour

- Using stock or cash for personal purposes without recorded lawful transaction
- Giving undocumented verbal approvals for high-risk commitments
- Overriding IMEI, cash or safety controls merely to close a sale
- Treating relatives or preferred customers outside approved policy without disclosure
- Pressuring staff to conceal complaints, returns, defects or variances

## KPIs

- Liquidity and debt-service coverage
- Gross profit and cash, not revenue alone
- Stock turn and ageing
- Control exceptions and closure time
- Customer trust/repeat/referral indicators
- Staff retention, competence and conduct
- Readiness-gate evidence quality

## Founder readiness evidence

- Approved authority matrix
- Bank and distributor term sheets understood
- Monthly management pack reviewed
- Named backups and escalation contacts
- Signed launch and major-investment decisions
