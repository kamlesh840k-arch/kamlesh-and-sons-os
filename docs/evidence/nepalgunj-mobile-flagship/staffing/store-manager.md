# Role Scorecard — Store Manager

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-003  
**Proposed fixed-pay planning band:** NPR 35,000–50,000 plus approved incentive  
**Reports to:** Owner / General Manager

## Purpose

Run a safe, controlled, customer-trusted and commercially effective showroom every day.

## Responsibilities

- Lead opening, briefing, floor coverage and closing.
- Assign daily targets by customer outcome, gross profit and service quality.
- Coach needs discovery, comparison, objection handling and policy explanation.
- Resolve customer escalations within delegated authority.
- Approve permitted discounts, exchanges and service exceptions.
- Verify staffing, grooming, demos, displays and Service Lounge readiness.
- Monitor queues, WhatsApp response and delivery/pickup status.
- Conduct daily cash/stock exception review with the controller.
- Report incidents, stock variance, fraud, privacy, safety and reputational risk immediately.
- Maintain shift, training, observation and corrective-action records.

## Daily checklist

### Before opening

- Confirm duty roster, absences and minimum coverage.
- Complete safety, CCTV, alarm, power, POS and internet checks.
- Review high-value stock and prior-day exceptions.
- Confirm current prices, promotions, availability and service capacity.
- Brief the team on target customers, content, risks and appointments.

### During trading

- Observe consultations and intervene without undermining staff.
- Check waiting customers and handoffs.
- Authorise only documented exceptions.
- Review hourly online-order and Service Lounge queues.
- Protect breaks and rotate staff fairly.

### Closing

- Confirm no customer case, repair or delivery lacks an owner/update.
- Review cash, payments, refunds and IMEI movement.
- Record incidents, missed targets and next actions.
- Confirm physical security and controlled closing sign-off.

## Weekly/monthly work

- Individual coaching and scorecard review
- Mystery-shop/self-audit of journey standards
- Stock-ageing and display review
- Staff schedule and leave plan
- Root-cause review of returns, complaints and missed repairs
- Monthly performance recommendation to Owner/GM

## Authority

May schedule staff, assign floor coverage, approve routine policy remedies and discounts within the signed authority matrix, pause unsafe work and remove a product from sale pending review.

May not sign distributor, banking, lease or employment commitments; write off stock; alter invoices/IMEIs; or publish material claims without approval.

## KPIs

- Gross profit versus plan
- Conversion and average gross profit per transaction
- Accessory attachment and service contribution
- Consultation and handover quality
- Queue/response and case-update performance
- Complaint and first-resolution performance
- Stock/cash exception closure
- Staff attendance, training and observation

## Hiring requirements

- Demonstrated retail/team leadership
- Strong Nepali communication; useful local-language capability preferred
- Numerical and digital confidence
- Calm complaint handling and ethical judgement
- Willingness to work peak retail hours under lawful scheduling

## Selection exercises

- Run a ten-minute team briefing.
- Resolve a warranty dispute without making a false promise.
- Review a mock daily sales/margin/stock report.
- Prioritise simultaneous floor, repair and WhatsApp queues.

## Probation gate

Must independently lead opening/closing, coaching, escalation and control review with no unresolved critical failure before confirmation.
