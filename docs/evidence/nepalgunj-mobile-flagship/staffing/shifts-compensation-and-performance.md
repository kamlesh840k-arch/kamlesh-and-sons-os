# Shifts, Compensation and Performance

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-012  
**Status:** Planning framework — payroll/legal approval pending

## Scheduling principles

- Comply with current Nepal working-time, overtime, leave, holiday and benefit requirements.
- Publish rosters in advance and record changes.
- Provide lawful weekly rest and breaks.
- Staff peak hours more heavily through overlapping shifts.
- Do not use unpaid opening/closing work.
- Keep at least one duty lead, two sales-capable employees, one cashier and one service/online-capable person available.

## Example overlapping coverage

Final times depend on approved store hours.

- Early coverage: opening preparation through afternoon
- Core coverage: late morning through evening
- Late coverage: afternoon peak through closing

The manager prepares a four-week rolling roster and a weekly confirmation. Absence replacement may not break cash, inventory or safety segregation.

## Incentive design

Incentives should reward sustainable gross profit and quality, not only revenue or one brand.

Suggested balance:

- 40% team gross-profit achievement
- 20% individual qualified sales contribution
- 15% accessory/service attachment quality
- 15% customer journey and feedback
- 10% control, training and attendance reliability

### Safeguards

- Cancelled, returned, unpaid or fraudulent transactions do not earn final incentive.
- Mis-selling, false claims or policy bypass can remove affected incentive under approved process.
- No secret brand spiff paid directly to staff; distributor incentives go through company payroll/accounting.
- Targets must not pressure staff to hide complaints or deny valid remedies.
- Incentive calculations and disputes are documented and reviewable.

## Performance cycle

### Daily

Briefing, target/capacity, observation and exception feedback.

### Weekly

Short scorecard: customer quality, gross profit, attachment, service, controls and training.

### Monthly

Formal performance conversation with evidence, context, actions and support.

### Quarterly

Role certification, development, pay/incentive review under authority, and staffing-capacity review.

## Underperformance

1. Clarify expected standard and evidence.
2. Identify training, tools, workload or conduct cause.
3. Agree support, measure and date.
4. Observe and document progress.
5. Use fair corrective process if unresolved.

Safety, fraud, violence, harassment, privacy, theft or deliberate control bypass follows immediate escalation and approved legal/disciplinary procedure.

## Recognition

Recognise trusted behaviour, customer recovery, learning, accurate controls, teamwork and useful content—not just top sales.

## Payroll controls

- Approved employee master and salary change
- Attendance/leave/overtime evidence
- Independent calculation and payment review
- Payslip and lawful deduction transparency
- Payroll-to-bank reconciliation
- Confidential access and retention
