# Role Scorecard — Delivery and Pickup Runner

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-009  
**Opening approach:** On-demand/contract  
**Reports to:** Digital Coordinator; duty control by Store Manager

## Purpose

Move products and customer devices safely through a documented chain of custody with identity, payment and condition controls.

## Responsibilities

- Accept only assigned jobs in the official system.
- Verify sealed package/device pouch, order number and destination.
- Protect stock from heat, rain, impact, theft and unauthorised access.
- Verify customer identity and handover code.
- Collect only the approved amount and issue official acknowledgement.
- Record attempted, completed or failed delivery accurately.
- Return failed jobs, cash and documents immediately under procedure.
- Maintain route and incident communication without unsafe phone use while driving.

## Pickup procedure

- Verify customer and job request.
- Photograph device/condition with consent.
- Record device, IMEI/serial and accessories.
- Place item in numbered tamper-evident pouch.
- Issue receipt and update collected status.
- Handover to Service Lounge with signatures/status.

## Delivery procedure

- Check recipient and one-time delivery code; never ask for banking OTP.
- Allow outer-package/seal inspection.
- Complete payment status confirmation.
- Record name, time and accepted condition.
- Do not leave high-value items unattended or with an unverified third party.

## Prohibited conduct

- Personal errands while carrying customer/store devices
- Changing address/payment without dispatcher approval
- Opening packages or viewing customer devices
- Collecting undisclosed fees
- Marking delivered before verified handover
- Sharing route/customer information

## KPIs

- Chain-of-custody completeness
- On-time delivery/pickup
- Failed-delivery reason accuracy
- Cash and device variance: zero
- Customer feedback and incident rate

## Vetting

Valid legal driving documents where applicable, identity/address/reference checks, safe vehicle, smartphone workflow ability and practical handover test.

## Permanent-hire trigger

More than approximately eight daily jobs with proven route economics, service reliability and approved payroll capacity.
