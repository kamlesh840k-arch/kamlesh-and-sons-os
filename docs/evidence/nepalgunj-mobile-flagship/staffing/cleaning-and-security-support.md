# Role Standard — Cleaning and Security Support

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-010  
**Opening approach:** Outsourced and vetted

## Cleaning purpose

Maintain a premium, safe environment without exposing stock, customer devices, records or credentials.

### Cleaning duties

- Opening/closing floor, glass, fixtures and Service Lounge cleaning
- Scheduled washroom/back-office cleaning
- Safe handling of approved chemicals around devices
- Immediate spill and trip-hazard response
- Waste segregation, including no handling of e-waste/batteries without instruction

### Cleaning restrictions

- No unsupervised secure-stockroom, safe or customer-device access
- No photography or customer-record access
- No movement of demo/repair devices without staff custody

## Security purpose

Deter theft, manage safe entry/exit and respond through approved escalation without creating an intimidating customer experience.

### Security duties

- Opening/closing perimeter observation
- CCTV/alarm status escalation, not unauthorised surveillance use
- Crowd/queue and high-value handover awareness
- Incident preservation and immediate escalation
- Emergency access support and evacuation assistance

### Security restrictions

- No customer search, detention or force outside lawful approved procedure
- No sharing CCTV or incident media
- No independent refund, custody or customer-data authority

## Vendor controls

- Identity/reference checks and lawful contract
- Named replacements only
- Confidentiality and access rules
- Insurance/liability where appropriate
- Training in emergency, customer dignity, privacy and escalation
- Attendance and incident records

## KPIs

- Cleaning/readiness inspection
- Hazard response
- Access-control exceptions
- Incident notification time
- Customer/staff conduct feedback
