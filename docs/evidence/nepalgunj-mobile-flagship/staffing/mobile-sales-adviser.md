# Role Scorecard — Mobile Sales Adviser

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-004  
**Opening count:** 3  
**Proposed fixed-pay planning band:** NPR 22,000–30,000 plus approved incentive  
**Reports to:** Store Manager

## Purpose

Help customers choose the right authorised device through respectful needs discovery, clear comparisons and complete handover—not pressure or misinformation.

## Core journey

1. Welcome and understand preferred assistance.
2. Ask intended use, budget, timing, current device and must-have needs.
3. Present two or three suitable options.
4. Separate facts, estimates, promotions and opinion.
5. Explain total price, warranty, MDMS, accessories and EMI obligations.
6. Confirm the order before billing.
7. Coordinate IMEI, setup, data transfer and customer acknowledgement.
8. Record follow-up or unresolved needs.

## Daily responsibilities

- Maintain assigned display/demo readiness and security.
- Learn current prices, stock, offers, warranty and comparisons.
- Follow up assigned qualified leads with consent.
- Offer compatible protection/power/audio based on need.
- Hand service and online cases to the correct owner.
- Record lost-sale reason accurately.
- Keep customer data and credentials private.

## Specialisation with cross-training

- Adviser A: Samsung and mainstream Android
- Adviser B: Xiaomi/Redmi, Realme, OPPO, Vivo and value comparisons
- Adviser C: Apple, Nothing, premium consultation, EMI and accessories

All advisers must cover core categories and the Service Lounge handoff.

## Authority

May recommend products, create a quotation, reserve stock under policy and provide approved product information.

May not change price, promise exchange/warranty approval, open a sealed device before payment/approval, access customer accounts without consent, receive personal payment or conceal grey-market status.

## Prohibited conduct

- Misrepresenting RAM/storage, camera, water resistance, warranty or availability
- Steering only toward the highest incentive
- Creating fake scarcity or insulting competitor/customer choices
- Asking for banking PIN, wallet PIN, email password or OTP
- Saving customer photos, contacts or credentials
- Moving stock without an IMEI transaction

## KPIs

- Qualified consultations and conversion
- Gross profit, not sales value alone
- Accessory attachment and compatible-return rate
- Setup/handover completion
- Customer feedback, repeat and referral
- Returns/complaints caused by misinformation
- Product and policy knowledge certification
- Lead follow-up within consent and response target

## Hiring requirements

- Honest communication and listening
- Smartphone literacy with ability to learn multiple brands
- Basic arithmetic and digital record use
- Nepali fluency; relevant local languages are an advantage
- No requirement for aggressive selling style

## Selection exercises

- Recommend a phone to three different customer scenarios.
- Explain MDMS, official warranty and EMI simply.
- Compare two devices without reading a specification sheet verbatim.
- Respond to “India is cheaper” respectfully.
- Demonstrate safe data-transfer consent.

## Training and probation

- Complete product, customer-journey, policy, privacy and fraud modules.
- Pass written and observed certification.
- Complete supervised consultations and handovers.
- Confirmation requires accurate advice, no critical conduct breach and acceptable observed journey quality.
