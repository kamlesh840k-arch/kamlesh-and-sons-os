# Role Scorecard — Service Lounge Adviser

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-006  
**Proposed fixed-pay planning band:** NPR 25,000–35,000  
**Reports to:** Store Manager

## Purpose

Own setup, data transfer, software assistance, warranty submission, repair intake and customer updates through a safe, transparent service process.

## Responsibilities

- Explain free, member and chargeable services before work.
- Obtain consent for setup, data transfer and device access.
- Complete condition photography and numbered repair job card.
- Record device, IMEI, accessories, reported fault and update date.
- Diagnose only within certification; route technical work appropriately.
- Obtain written estimate approval before repair/parts.
- Track authorised-service-centre and technician status.
- Test agreed functions and complete handover acknowledgement.
- Administer KAMLESH Care service benefits and caps.
- Protect customer privacy and escalate suspected loss/fraud/data exposure.

## Setup/data-transfer control

- Customer enters passwords where practical.
- Staff never asks for banking/wallet PIN or OTP.
- Transfer only agreed data and confirm source/destination.
- Do not retain a customer backup without approved service, consent and security.
- Customer changes any temporary password at handover.

## Repair intake checklist

- Customer and contact verified
- Device, IMEI/serial and colour recorded
- Visible condition photographed with consent
- SIM/memory card/accessories recorded
- Lock access and data risk agreed
- Diagnostic/estimate range disclosed
- Next update date issued
- Secure storage location assigned

## Authority

May accept a device under policy, perform certified basic services, pause work and request customer approval.

May not promise warranty acceptance/replacement, perform unauthorised repair, browse personal content, erase data without explicit approval or release a device without identity/handover verification.

## KPIs

- Intake completeness
- Promised update compliance
- First-contact basic-resolution rate
- Repeat repair and service complaint rate
- Data/privacy incidents
- Service contribution and member usage within caps
- Warranty submission accuracy
- Customer-device custody variance: zero

## Hiring and selection

- Patient explanation and emotional composure
- Strong Android/iOS setup literacy
- Accurate documentation and privacy judgement
- Exercise: intake a damaged phone and create a job card
- Exercise: data-transfer consent and warranty rejection conversation

## Probation gate

Complete supervised setup, transfer, repair intake, warranty submission and handover with policy and privacy certification.
