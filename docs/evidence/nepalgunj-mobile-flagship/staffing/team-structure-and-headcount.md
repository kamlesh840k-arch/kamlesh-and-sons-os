# Team Structure and Headcount

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-001  
**Status:** Opening organization proposal

## Opening organization

Recommended: **7 employees plus the Owner/General Manager**, with technical, delivery, cleaning and security support contracted initially.

| Role | Opening count | Employment approach | Reports to |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Owner / General Manager | 1 | Founder | — |
| Store Manager | 1 | Full-time | Owner/GM |
| Mobile Sales Advisers | 3 | Full-time | Store Manager |
| Cashier and Inventory Controller | 1 | Full-time | Store Manager; control access to Owner/Finance |
| Service Lounge Adviser | 1 | Full-time | Store Manager |
| Digital Commerce and Community Coordinator | 1 | Full-time | Store Manager; dotted line to Owner/Marketing |
| Technician | Contract/commission | Approved service arrangement | Service Adviser / Store Manager |
| Delivery and Pickup Runner | On demand/contract | Approved service arrangement | Digital Coordinator / Store Manager |
| Cleaning and Security | Outsourced | Vetted vendor/individual | Store Manager |

## Why seven employees

- Three advisers provide customer coverage without making the store feel unattended.
- Billing and IMEI inventory remain separate from selling.
- The Service Lounge has a visible owner during selling hours.
- WhatsApp, social content, advance orders and delivery do not become neglected side work.
- The manager can coach, resolve and control rather than serving every customer personally.
- Contract support controls payroll while demand is unproven.

## Peak coverage

Use overlapping shifts so five to six employees are present during the expected afternoon/evening peak. Minimum live coverage:

- One accountable manager or duty lead
- Two sales-capable employees
- One authorised cashier
- One service/online-capable employee

No store should open with only one employee present. Secure stockroom access requires approved role access and logged entry.

## Scale-up triggers

Add permanent roles only after evidence:

| Trigger sustained for 3 months | Addition |
|---|---|
| 90–100 phones/month and repair demand proven | 1 in-house Technician |
| More than 12 qualified consultations/day with service delays | 1 Sales Adviser |
| More than 8 pickup/delivery jobs/day | 1 dedicated Runner |
| Service queue exceeds approved target repeatedly | 1 setup/technical assistant |
| Digital leads exceed coordinator capacity and response target | 1 online-sales assistant |

Mature configuration: approximately 10 employees plus owner. Growth requires margin and cash evidence, not revenue alone.

## Segregation of duties

The same person must not independently:

- Approve a purchase, receive the stock and reconcile the supplier invoice.
- Sell a phone, collect payment, issue an unauthorised discount and close the cash variance.
- Accept a return, decide the remedy and put the unit back into saleable stock.
- Receive a repair device, perform undocumented work and close the job without handover testing.
- Create a delivery order, carry the device and mark it delivered without customer proof.

Founder-stage conflicts require a documented second-person or next-day independent review.

## Staffing cost envelope

Planning range for seven employees plus contracted support: approximately NPR 2.35–3.30 lakh per month, excluding owner salary. This is not an approved budget. Replace with signed offers, statutory obligations, incentives, benefits and vendor quotations.
