# Role Scorecard — Technician

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-008  
**Opening approach:** Approved contract/commission arrangement  
**Permanent-hire trigger:** Proven workload and economics  
**Proposed permanent-pay planning band:** NPR 30,000–45,000 plus approved productivity/quality incentive

## Purpose

Diagnose and repair only within verified competence using documented parts, customer approval, safe handling and test evidence.

## Scope categories

- Basic inspection and fault reproduction
- Software restoration/update under approved process
- Battery, display, charging and component replacement within certification
- Cleaning and preventive maintenance
- Technical support to trade-in grading
- Escalation to authorised service where warranty or capability requires it

The approved capability matrix must list permitted models and repair types. Work outside it is prohibited.

## Responsibilities

- Verify job card, condition and customer approval before opening device.
- Diagnose and issue parts/labour estimate through the Service Adviser.
- Record parts source, serial where relevant and work performed.
- Use ESD, fire, battery and tool-safety controls.
- Protect components and customer data.
- Run an agreed post-repair test and record result.
- Return replaced part when policy permits and customer requests.
- Maintain tool, parts and hazardous/e-waste records.

## Prohibited work

- Warranty-voiding work without written disclosure/approval
- IMEI alteration, lock bypass without lawful proof or suspicious-device work
- Undocumented parts substitution
- Personal use/copying of customer data
- Repair without intake record and estimate authority
- Concealing damage or failed repair
- Unsafe handling of swollen/damaged batteries

## KPIs

- First-time fix and repeat-fault rate
- Estimate accuracy and approval traceability
- Turnaround/update performance
- Parts variance and warranty returns
- Damage, safety and privacy incidents
- Customer acceptance and repair gross contribution

## Vetting and practical test

- Identity, reference and qualification evidence
- Demonstrated ESD/battery safety
- Supervised diagnosis and repair on test devices
- Explain when to refuse or route warranty work
- Parts-quality and data-privacy scenario

## Contract requirements

- Permitted work/capability
- Parts standards and warranty
- Payment basis and rework responsibility
- Customer-device custody and insurance
- Confidentiality/privacy
- Safety and e-waste obligations
- Incident notification and termination

## Permanent-hire gate

Sustained demand, approved economics, competence evidence, clean quality/safety record and payroll capacity.
