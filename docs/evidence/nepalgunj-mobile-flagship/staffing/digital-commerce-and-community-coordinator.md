# Role Scorecard — Digital Commerce and Community Coordinator

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-007  
**Proposed fixed-pay planning band:** NPR 25,000–40,000 plus approved lead/conversion incentive  
**Reports to:** Store Manager; dotted line to Owner/Marketing

## Purpose

Turn useful content and consented conversations into trusted store visits, advance orders, service bookings and long-term community relationships.

## Channels

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Google Business and the official WhatsApp account.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain accurate catalogue, price, stock, offer and service information.
- Respond to leads within the approved service level.
- Confirm orders only through official order number and payment acknowledgement.
- Administer advance-order, deposit, delivery and pickup/drop status.
- Maintain marketing consent, preferences and opt-outs.
- Produce the approved research-led content calendar.
- Recruit and administer survey participation without coercion.
- Moderate comments and route complaints to a named owner.
- Record source, lead stage, outcome and lost reason.
- Protect access credentials, customer data and brand assets.

## Content controls

- Use approved facts, prices, comparisons and claims.
- State stock, duration, eligibility and exclusions on offers.
- Obtain permission for customer images/testimonials.
- Do not publish confidential distributor, competitor, landlord or customer data.
- Correct material errors promptly and preserve the correction record.

## WhatsApp order controls

- Never request PIN, OTP or full card details.
- Confirm live stock, price, colour, warranty and gift in writing.
- Use approved deposit and refund terms.
- Handoff to cashier before payment is treated as settled.
- Handoff to runner with documented chain of custody.

## Authority

May publish approved calendar content, create quotations/reservations and schedule services/delivery.

May not invent promotions, change price, promise finance approval, publish allegations, use personal accounts as official channels or export customer lists without approval.

## KPIs

- Qualified leads and verified conversion contribution
- Response and follow-up time
- Catalogue accuracy
- Appointment/show-up and advance-order completion
- Consent and unsubscribe compliance
- Content saves/questions/qualified actions, not followers alone
- Complaint routing and correction time
- Delivery/order status accuracy

## Hiring and selection

- Clear Nepali writing/speaking and short-video confidence
- Customer-service discipline and spreadsheet/CRM ability
- Basic product knowledge and willingness to verify facts
- Exercise: create an educational video script and WhatsApp order recap
- Exercise: correct a wrong public price and handle an angry comment

## Probation gate

Run the channel calendar and order workflow for two supervised cycles with no critical claim, consent or payment-control breach.
