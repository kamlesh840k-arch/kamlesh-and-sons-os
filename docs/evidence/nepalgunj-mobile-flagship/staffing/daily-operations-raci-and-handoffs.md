## Opening sequence

1. Two authorised people enter and complete safety/security check.
2. Manager confirms roster and assigns duty roles.
3. Controller counts opening cash and high-value stock with witness.
4. Service Adviser verifies customer-device custody.
5. Digital Coordinator reviews appointments, WhatsApp and delivery queue.
6. Sales team checks displays, demos, prices and promotions.
7. Manager briefs targets, capacity, policy updates and risks.
8. Store opens only after critical exceptions are resolved or approved.

## Customer purchase handoff

Sales recommendation → customer order recap → manager exception if needed → cashier price/payment verification → IMEI match → Service Lounge setup/transfer if requested → customer inspection and acknowledgement → digital follow-up/receipt record.

No step assumes the next is complete; the receiving role confirms acceptance.

## Repair handoff

Customer/runner → Service Adviser intake and condition record → secure custody → Technician/authorised centre against signed handoff → estimate approval → repair/test → Service Adviser acceptance → cashier payment if applicable → verified customer release.

## Online/delivery handoff

Digital order confirmation → cashier payment/stock confirmation → controller packs and records IMEI/seal → runner accepts numbered custody → customer identity/code and handover → digital closes status → controller reconciles payment/device.

## Shift handoff

Outgoing and incoming leads review:

- Customers waiting or promised follow-up
- Open quotations/reservations
- Repairs/warranties and next updates
- Advance orders and deposits
- Delivery/pickup custody
- Cash/stock/system exceptions
- Safety, privacy and conduct incidents
- High-value appointments and staffing gaps

Record owner, next action and deadline. Verbal-only handoff is not sufficient for open customer devices, payments or stock exceptions.

## Closing sequence

1. Stop new complex services at the approved cut-off while completing existing customers fairly.
2. Close customer cases with owner/update—not necessarily resolution.
3. Reconcile sales, payments, refunds and IMEI movements.
4. Count cash and high-value stock with witness.
5. Confirm repair devices and delivery returns.
6. Secure records, devices, keys and credentials.
7. Record incidents, variances and next-day priorities.
8. Complete alarm, power and two-person exit check.

## Escalation levels

### Immediate manager

Routine complaint, queue, price misunderstanding, staff coverage or repair-delay risk.

### Immediate Owner/GM

Cash/IMEI variance, suspected fraud/theft, material refund, legal threat, serious conduct, distributor/bank issue or public escalation.

### Emergency/qualified authority

Fire, battery thermal event, injury, violence, major data/privacy breach, building danger or other immediate safety/legal emergency.

## Rehearsal evidence

Before opening, conduct and record:

- Normal sale and setup
- EMI/order cancellation
- Return and warranty dispute
- Repair intake and data-risk scenario
- Delivery failure and lost-custody simulation
- Cash and IMEI variance
- Power/internet/POS outage
- Fire/battery and evacuation response
- Angry customer/public complaint

Critical failures are corrected and rehearsed again before launch approval.
