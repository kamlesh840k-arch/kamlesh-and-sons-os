# Role Scorecard — Cashier and Inventory Controller

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-005  
**Proposed fixed-pay planning band:** NPR 25,000–35,000  
**Reports to:** Store Manager, with independent control access to Owner/Finance

## Purpose

Protect cash, payments, invoices and every IMEI-controlled inventory movement while enabling accurate, efficient checkout.

## Responsibilities

- Verify approved quotation, price, discount and customer details.
- Issue correct VAT invoice with model, serial/IMEI and payment method.
- Match device, box, system and invoice IMEIs.
- Receive and record cash, card, bank and approved EMI settlement.
- Maintain cash drawer and payment reconciliation.
- Record receipts, transfers, reservations, sales, returns, repairs and write-off status.
- Receive stock with a second person against purchase order and supplier invoice.
- Run daily high-value counts and scheduled full counts.
- Produce ageing, variance, negative-stock and exception reports.
- Secure keys, safe access and controlled stockroom logs.

## Daily opening

- Count opening cash with witness.
- Check POS, printer, payment terminals and invoice sequence.
- Confirm prior-day unresolved settlement or IMEI exceptions.
- Verify high-value stock sample and stockroom seal/access log.

## Transaction controls

- No sale without system stock and matching IMEI.
- No manual price without authorised reason/code.
- No refund to a different payment destination without approved legal process.
- No return to saleable stock until condition, data, activation and approval checks finish.
- No stock leaves through delivery, demo, repair or transfer without documented custody.

## Closing

- Reconcile POS sales to cash, cards, bank/QR, EMI and refunds.
- Reconcile all handset IMEI movements.
- Count cash with authorised witness and record variance.
- Secure stock, cash, keys and reports.
- Escalate variance before anyone edits a record.

## Authority

May stop a transaction with price, IMEI, payment or authority mismatch and request independent review.

May not approve their own refund/discount/write-off, change a completed invoice without approved process, hide variance, borrow cash or lend keys/credentials.

## KPIs

- Zero unexplained IMEI variance
- Cash/payment reconciliation accuracy
- Invoice accuracy
- Stock receiving accuracy
- Ageing and exception reporting timeliness
- Count completion and variance closure
- Refund/credit-note traceability

## Hiring and selection

- Strong integrity references and background checks appropriate to law
- Retail accounting/inventory experience preferred
- Accurate numerical and system work
- Test: mock receiving, sale, return and end-of-day reconciliation
- Test: identify duplicate IMEI, wrong price and payment mismatch

## Probation gate

Must complete supervised receiving, counting, billing, refund and reconciliation cycles with no unresolved critical control failure.
