# Hiring, Onboarding and Training System

**Document ID:** EVD-NPJ-PEOPLE-011  
**Status:** Pre-opening people plan

## Hiring principles

- Hire integrity, learning and customer judgement before aggressive sales style.
- Use consistent criteria and evidence for every candidate.
- Do not discriminate on protected or irrelevant grounds.
- Obtain consent for lawful reference/background checks.
- Give accurate role, hours, pay, incentive, probation and monitoring information.
- No candidate works unpaid productive shifts disguised as assessment.

## Hiring workflow

1. Approved role and budget
2. Published role scorecard
3. Structured application screen
4. Standard interview
5. Role practical exercise
6. Reference/background checks with consent
7. Scorecard decision and approval
8. Written offer and employment/contract documents
9. Pre-joining documents and roster
10. Onboarding, supervised work and probation gate

## Standard interview areas

- Customer respect and listening
- Integrity/conflict scenario
- Numerical/digital competence
- Privacy/security judgement
- Learning across brands and systems
- Complaint and pressure response
- Availability under lawful scheduling
- Reason for joining and realistic expectations

Score independently before interviewers discuss. Preserve decision evidence and access controls.

## Pre-opening training academy

### Module 1 — Brand and customer promise

K&S purpose, authorised trust position, Service Lounge and prohibited misleading conduct.

### Module 2 — Product and needs discovery

Price bands, use cases, comparison, demo, accessories and avoiding incentive bias.

### Module 3 — Customer journey

Welcome, diagnose, compare, decide/pay, handover/setup, after-sales and recovery.

### Module 4 — MDMS, invoice and warranty

IMEI matching, authorised sourcing, warranty boundaries and no false promises.

### Module 5 — POS, cash and inventory

Quotations, invoices, payments, discounts, returns, stock movement and variance escalation.

### Module 6 — Service and data privacy

Consent, setup, transfer, repair intake, credentials, updates and customer-device custody.

### Module 7 — Digital commerce

WhatsApp orders, deposits, delivery, pickup/drop, consent and public claims.

### Module 8 — Safety and incident response

Fire, battery, electrical, medical, theft, crowd, evacuation and manual fallback.

### Module 9 — KAMLESH Care

Eligibility, caps, records, exclusions and no insurance/warranty misrepresentation.

### Module 10 — Rehearsal

Full opening, customer scenarios, payment, setup, repair, delivery, complaint and closing.

## Certification

Each role requires:

- Knowledge test
- Observed practical assessment
- Policy acknowledgement
- System-access approval limited to role
- Supervisor sign-off

Critical failure—such as requesting a PIN/OTP, bypassing IMEI control, unsafe battery handling or concealing variance—requires retraining and reassessment before customer work.

## First-day onboarding

- Identity/employment documents and emergency contact
- Role, pay, schedule, leave and conduct briefing
- Safety tour and emergency exits
- Confidentiality, privacy and monitoring notice
- Systems/access issuance and credential rules
- Uniform/name badge and locker
- Assigned trainer and 30/60/90-day plan

## Probation reviews

### Day 30

Attendance, basic knowledge, conduct, supervised tasks and training gaps.

### Day 60

Independent routine work, customer quality, controls and coaching response.

### Day 90

Role certification, KPI context, incident record, team behaviour and confirmation decision.

Probation/termination timing must follow approved contracts and Nepal law.

## Continuing training

- Weekly 30-minute product/policy huddle
- Monthly observed customer/service scenario
- Quarterly control, privacy and emergency refresher
- Certification after material system/policy change
- Distributor training recorded but never treated as independent policy approval
