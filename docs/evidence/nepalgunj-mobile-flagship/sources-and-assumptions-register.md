## Source hierarchy

1. Government and regulator datasets, legislation and notices
2. Authorised distributor/manufacturer information
3. Industry association and bank information
4. Established news reports citing primary data
5. Local business directories, maps and public reviews
6. Planning calculations and hypotheses

Lower-level sources do not override higher-level evidence. Reviews, directory entries and promotions do not prove sales.
