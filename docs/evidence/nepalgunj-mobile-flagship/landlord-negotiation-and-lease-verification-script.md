## Purpose

Use this document to negotiate the proposed five connected shutters without accidentally treating a verbal indication as a final right. Record the landlord's answer beside every item and transfer the agreed terms into a lawyer-reviewed lease and signed alteration schedule.

Current planning basis only: all ten shutters are reportedly available; K&S intends to select five consecutive shutters including the highway-facing position; approximately 720 sq ft is assumed; one arrival is highway-facing and four gallery-facing shutters would receive glass. Internal measurements, rent and every material lease term remain unapproved.

## Before the meeting

Bring or request:

- Photos and a simple plan identifying all ten shutters and the exact five proposed units
- Tape/laser measurements of clear internal width, depth, height, openings and floor levels
- Positions of columns, beams, meters, distribution boards, drains and possible outdoor AC units
- Ownership document or authority showing who can sign the lease
- Draft alteration schedule and a page for written answers
- A total occupancy-cost sheet covering rent, taxes, service/common charge, electricity, water, parking/loading and restoration

Do not start demolition, pay a non-refundable contractor advance or announce the location before the lease, technical checks and finance gate are approved.

## Opening conversation

> We want to build a long-term KAMLESH & SONS authorised technology flagship here. Our plan is to take five consecutive shutters, including the highway-facing position, and operate them as one premium store. We will invest in the property, but we need predictable occupancy cost and clear written permission for the work. Today I would like us to agree the exact five shutters, complete cost, fit-out period, alteration rights, utilities, maintenance responsibilities and exit/restoration terms. Any agreement today will be subject to measurement, technical inspection, finance approval and the final written lease.

## Confirm the property first

Ask and record:

1. Which exact five shutter/unit numbers are offered, and will they remain consecutive for the whole lease?
2. Is the highway-facing unit included and can it be the principal entrance/signage point?
3. Are any units, galleries, access routes, roofs, meters or parking/loading areas shared?
4. Who legally owns the property, who is authorised to sign and are there any mortgages, disputes or approvals that could affect occupation?
5. Can the landlord give uninterrupted possession for the lease term if K&S meets its obligations?

Suggested transition:

> Before discussing the final rent, I want to calculate the full cost and make sure our investment is protected. Please help us put every charge and permission on one page.

## Commercial negotiation script

> We are taking five shutters together and improving the property at our cost. We therefore need a competitive combined rent, a fit-out period before rent starts, a stable escalation formula and a sensible deposit. Please give your best written offer for the whole five-shutter package, not five separate verbal prices.

Negotiate and record:

- Monthly base rent for all five shutters and whether VAT/tax is included or additional
- Security deposit and advance rent; refund timing and permitted deductions
- Rent-free fit-out period; ask for 45–60 days from vacant possession, then assess against the agreed build schedule
- Lease length; seek enough protected term to recover the fit-out, with a renewal option
- Escalation percentage and frequency; model the full lease, not only year one
- Common-area/service/maintenance charges, property tax responsibility and any new charge the landlord may introduce
- Rent commencement only after vacant possession, access, agreed utility readiness and alteration permission
- A tenant break right if bank/distributor approvals fail before construction, or if a material building/legal defect prevents the approved use
- A continuing break/termination remedy for loss of access, unsafe premises, prolonged utility failure caused by the property, title dispute or inability to use the premises lawfully
- Whether the five shutters are one lease and one deposit, not separately terminable units

Do not accept phrases such as “we will decide later” for escalation, deposit deductions, restoration or common charges.

## Alteration and signage permission schedule

Use this wording as the conversation checklist:

> Our design depends on one continuous store. We need the lease to expressly permit the following works, subject to competent technical approval and applicable law. Please confirm who pays, who approves drawings, how quickly approval is given and exactly what must be restored at exit.

Request written permission for:

- Removal/opening of specified non-structural partitions between the five shutters
- Retention and protection of all structural columns, beams and slabs identified by inspection
- Safety-glass shopfronts behind/across the four gallery-facing shutters, including doors, locks, manifestation and after-hours protection
- Highway-facing K&S entrance, fascia, projecting/illuminated signage and approved brand campaign panels
- New ceiling, lighting, counters, wall fixtures, floor finish and accessible threshold treatment
- Electrical rewiring, additional load, dedicated meter/sub-meter, earthing, UPS/inverter and generator connection if applicable
- AC indoor/outdoor units, refrigerant/drain routes and external mounting positions
- CCTV, alarm, access control, network/internet cabling, safe anchoring and security sensors
- Fire/life-safety equipment, emergency lighting and required exit changes
- Drilling, fixing, core cutting or rooftop/external work only where the technical plan permits it
- Defined opening hours, delivery/loading access and contractor access during fit-out

Attach the approved plan to the lease. State that undocumented verbal approval is not sufficient.

## Restoration clause discussion

> We can accept reasonable restoration, but it must be specific and costable now. We cannot agree to an unlimited obligation to return the property to any condition chosen later.

Seek:

- A dated photographic condition report before work
- A list of landlord-approved improvements that may remain without charge
- A precise list of items that must be removed or reinstated
- No requirement to rebuild removed non-structural partitions unless expressly priced and agreed
- Ordinary wear and tear excluded
- A joint exit inspection and written cost estimate before deductions
- Deposit deductions supported by invoices or reasonable documented costs
- A deadline for returning the undisputed deposit balance

## Utilities, safety and operating questions

Do not postpone these past lease signing. Ask:

1. What electrical load is presently sanctioned? Is supply single- or three-phase? Who pays for upgrade, meter and deposit?
2. Where are the distribution board, earthing point, generator/inverter connection and AC outdoor-unit positions?
3. Is water ingress or flooding known at the frontage, gallery, floor, roof or drain during monsoon?
4. Where will staff and customers access a legal, clean toilet and hand-washing facility? Who maintains it and during which hours is it accessible?
5. What fire exits, emergency route and extinguishing provisions exist for a five-shutter combined occupancy?
6. Can the four glazed fronts preserve required escape and emergency access?
7. Is fixed internet available, and can a second connection be installed?
8. Who repairs roof, structure, main wiring, drainage, common gallery and exterior defects?
9. What parking, motorcycle stopping, delivery/loading and waste arrangements are available?
10. Are there restrictions on operating hours, music, launch crowds, queue barriers, signage lighting or festival promotion?

## Cost-control negotiation points

- Ask the landlord to deliver the five shutters empty, dry, level, lockable and with agreed electrical/basic repairs complete.
- Prefer landlord-funded structural and base-building repairs; K&S funds only its retail-specific fit-out.
- Ask for contribution or rent credit for permanent improvements that raise property value, especially partition work, base electrical upgrades, glazing infrastructure and drainage repair.
- Use modular counters, replaceable brand panels and surface systems that reduce both initial cost and exit restoration.
- Do not allow the landlord's contractor to work without written scope, price, warranty and completion date.
- Require written approval before any variation. Record whether it changes rent commencement or the fit-out period.

The combined monthly rent is NPR ______, deposit is NPR ______, escalation is ______, lease term is ______ and rent begins on ______ after a fit-out period of ______ days. The landlord will provide ______. K&S may complete the alterations listed in the attached schedule. At exit, only ______ must be restored. Utilities, toilet access, signage, operating hours, maintenance and break rights are recorded as follows: ______. This remains subject to measurement, technical inspection, finance approval and the signed lease. Please confirm these points in writing so our lawyer can prepare or review the final document.

## Negotiation fallback ladder

If the landlord will not reduce headline rent, trade for value in this order:

1. Longer rent-free fit-out period
2. Lower/refundable deposit and no unnecessary advance
3. Slower or less frequent escalation
4. Landlord-funded structural, electrical, drainage or base-building work
5. Contribution/rent credit for permanent improvements
6. Strong break and renewal options
7. Clear limits on restoration and deposit deductions
8. Included common charge, parking/loading and signage rights

Do not trade away safety, lawful use, five-unit continuity, written alteration rights, toilet access, utility adequacy or deposit protection merely to obtain a lower first-year rent.
