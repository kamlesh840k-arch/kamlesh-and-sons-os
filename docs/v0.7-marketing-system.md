## Outcome

Build trusted awareness, qualified demand, launch momentum, retention and referrals through brand-consistent, measurable and financially controlled marketing.

## Strategy Framework

Every plan defines business outcome, priority audience and insight, proposition, journey stage, channel role, offer, proof, call to action, budget, owner, dates, risks, measurement and stop/scale rule. Nepalgunj flagship launch planning must integrate local trust, store readiness and after-sales capacity; it must not promise unavailable capability.

## Channel Roles

| Channel | Primary role | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Store/local | Trust, discovery, events and conversion | Capacity and safety review |
| Website/search | Authoritative information and intent capture | Accuracy, accessibility and consent |
| Social/content | Education, proof and community | Brand/claim moderation |
| Direct CRM | Lifecycle service, retention and referral | Consent, preference and frequency |
| Paid media | Qualified reach and demand testing | Budget, attribution and fraud controls |
| Partners/PR | Credibility and extended reach | Due diligence and approved claims |

## Campaign Workflow

Brief → audience/offer evidence → creative and claim review → privacy/legal/brand/finance approval → readiness check → controlled launch → monitoring → optimize/stop → reconciliation → learning record. Promotions must state eligibility, duration, stock/availability, exclusions, warranty and financing terms clearly.

## Content and Reputation

Maintain a source for approved brand assets, claims, product facts, prices and offer terms. Obtain usage rights and consent for customer/creator content. Correct material errors promptly, preserve an approval trail, route complaints into the v0.4 recovery system, and escalate safety, legal, fraud or reputation events.

## Measures

Measure reach quality, engagement intent, qualified leads, store visits, conversion contribution, acquisition cost, retention, referrals, incremental margin contribution, budget variance, complaint/unsubscribe rate and experiments learned. Do not equate vanity metrics with business value; attribution assumptions must be visible.

## Activation and Closure Evidence

Activation requires a named owner, approved strategy/audiences, launch calendar, content capacity, store/service readiness, budget authority, data/privacy/legal review and measurement baseline. Operational closure requires executed campaigns, reconciled spend, reliable performance evidence, brand/compliance review, retained learning and leadership acceptance.
