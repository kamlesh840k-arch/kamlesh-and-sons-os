# K&S OS — v0.3 Signage & Wayfinding Standards

**Milestone:** v0.3.0 — Store Design  
**Status:** Draft standard — concept-level

## Purpose

Ensure customers can orient, browse, seek help, and complete a visit without confusion.

## Wayfinding Hierarchy

1. Store identity and arrival orientation
2. Category and zone identification
3. Product discovery and comparison cues
4. Consultation, service, and checkout direction
5. Mandatory safety, accessibility, and regulatory notices

## Standards

- Use the approved v0.2 identity, typography, color, and layout principles.
- Make key zones understandable from arrival and at decision points.
- Use plain, concise language and consistent terminology.
- Avoid excessive signage; every sign must clarify an action, location, or decision.
- Product information and directional signage must not compete visually.
- Safety and accessibility signs must remain visible and compliant with applicable requirements.
- Temporary or promotional signs must be governed, dated, and removed when no longer current.

## Accessibility

Detailed design must validate readability, contrast, placement, tactile or other required accessibility measures, language requirements, and local code compliance.
