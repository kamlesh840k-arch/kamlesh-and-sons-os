# K&S OS — v0.2.0 Brand Standards

**Milestone:** v0.2.0 — Brand Identity  
**Status:** Working brand guide — final approval required

## Purpose

Provide one practical standard for applying the K&S brand consistently across approved customer-facing materials. This guide operationalizes the v0.2 strategy and identity specification without inventing unapproved assets.

## 1. Brand Foundation

All brand expression should reinforce the approved strategic direction:

- Trustworthy
- Premium
- Knowledgeable
- Human
- Modern

The brand should feel confident and refined without becoming distant, exaggerated or discount-led.

## 2. Logo Standards

Use only approved logo artwork once the final identity package is signed off.

Required standards:

- Maintain defined clear space.
- Respect minimum size.
- Use only approved color/background variants.
- Never stretch, distort, rotate or redraw the mark.
- Do not add unauthorized effects, outlines or decorative treatments.
- Maintain consistent placement across recurring materials.

## 3. Color Standards

The final approved palette must define:

- Primary brand colors
- Supporting colors
- Neutral palette
- Functional interface colors where applicable

Until final approval, no specific color values are treated as canonical in this guide.

## 4. Typography Standards

The final approved typography system must define:

- Display hierarchy
- Heading hierarchy
- Body text
- Supporting text
- Numeric/data treatment where needed

Typography should prioritize legibility, hierarchy and consistency across digital and print applications.

## 5. Imagery Standards

Preferred imagery should be:

- Authentic rather than staged-looking
- Clean and well composed
- Human and service-oriented
- Technically credible
- Product-aware
- Premium without artificial luxury cues

Avoid imagery that is misleading, excessively generic, visually noisy or inconsistent with the brand personality.

## 6. Graphic Language

Supporting shapes, icons, patterns and graphic treatments should remain simple and subordinate to the core identity.

Use repetition and consistency to create recognition rather than adding decoration for its own sake.

## 7. Layout & Spacing

Layouts should provide:

- Clear hierarchy
- Adequate whitespace
- Predictable alignment
- Consistent margins and spacing
- Strong readability

The final design system should define reusable spacing and layout tokens where digital systems require them.

## 8. Voice & Messaging

Use language that is:

- Clear
- Confident
- Warm
- Premium
- Helpful

Avoid exaggerated claims, unnecessary jargon, aggressive sales language and artificial luxury phrasing.

## 9. Application Governance

Every recurring customer-facing template should use the approved brand assets and standards rather than creating local variations.

Detailed store environment standards remain part of **v0.3 — Store Design**.
Marketing campaign execution remains part of **v0.7 — Marketing**.

## 10. Approval & Version Control

Before v0.2 is closed, leadership must approve the final brand identity package and identify its canonical asset/version location.

Any material future change to the approved identity should be recorded as a new decision or controlled change, preserving the previous version for traceability.

## Completion Standard

v0.2 is ready for closure when the brand strategy, identity system and practical standards are approved, internally consistent and usable by later roadmap milestones without ambiguity.
