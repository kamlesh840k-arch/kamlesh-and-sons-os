## Decision-relevant conclusion

Nepal has multiple current financing routes relevant to K&S, but “EMI” is not one interchangeable product. Public evidence shows four different lanes:

1. merchant-sponsored credit-card EMI at participating retailers;
2. customer-paid post-purchase credit-card conversion;
3. pre-approved bank-app or QR-based digital lending/BNPL;
4. product- or ecosystem-specific schemes with their own down payment and documentation.

K&S should not advertise a generic “0% EMI” promise. A zero fee or zero interest rate for the customer can still require a merchant subsidy, handling charge, ordinary card-acquiring fee or promotion contribution. No bank, rate, merchant fee, subsidy, eligibility rule or launch date is approved by this study.

The recommended research direction is to obtain at least four comparable written proposals, then pilot a deliberately small set of complementary routes: one locally relevant merchant programme, one customer-controlled conversion route and one verified QR/app BNPL route. This is a hypothesis for founder approval, not a partner selection.

## Evidence and classification rule

- **Verified fact (V):** current official regulator, bank or programme-owner source.
- **Public claim (P):** current provider proposition; availability and execution still require confirmation.
- **Calculated estimate (E):** arithmetic from stated inputs; not an observed K&S result.
- **Hypothesis (H):** proposed partner priority, pilot structure or operating threshold requiring validation.
- **Founder decision (D):** accepted only when separately approved and recorded; none is created here.

Public retail pages and fee schedules do not disclose a complete K&S merchant agreement. “Not publicly stated” means a written proposal is required; it does not mean the fee or obligation is zero.

## What the matrix does and does not prove

### Verified/publicly established

- Credit-card EMI is already deployed with multiple electronics/mobile merchants in Nepalgunj.
- Some bank products let eligible cardholders convert purchases after the sale, potentially at any merchant.
- Some app-based products use pre-approved bank analytics and can pay a Fonepay QR merchant immediately.
- A “no customer fee” route can charge the merchant instead.
- Minimum amount, credit limit, tenure, application timing and product/merchant participation vary materially.

### Not established

- Number or share of eligible Nepalgunj customers.
- Approval rate, decline reasons or average financed ticket.
- Any K&S merchant fee, card MDR, tax, subsidy, reserve, holdback or settlement time.
- Refund, cancellation, return, chargeback and failed-conversion economics for K&S.
- Whether a public bank product is available through a Nepalgunj branch/POS and K&S legal entity on launch day.
- Whether any advertised 0% programme is funded by the bank, brand, distributor, merchant or a combination.

## Customer eligibility taxonomy

Staff and customer materials must distinguish these routes:

| Route | What the customer needs | What K&S may say | What K&S must not say |
|---|---|---|---|
| Existing credit-card merchant EMI | Correct issuing-bank card, sufficient available limit, eligible merchant/product and bank approval | “This written offer is available to eligible holders of this card, subject to bank approval.” | “Any card works,” “everyone is approved,” or “the card limit does not matter” |
| Post-purchase card conversion | Eligible issuing-bank card, qualifying transaction and request within the bank's conversion window | Point to the bank's current process and let the customer submit it | Promise conversion before the bank confirms it or release goods against an unverified approval |
| New credit-card/application route | Bank account and identity/income documents set by the bank; processing time varies | Give the bank's official checklist and contact | Complete declarations, alter documents or imply approval is instant |
| Pre-approved app/QR BNPL | Eligible bank/app customer with a visible pre-approved offer and sufficient limit | “If your bank app shows the offer, review its total cost before confirming.” | Claim all mobile-banking or salary-account customers are eligible |
| Product-specific scheme | Exact eligible model, authorised merchant, quotation, down payment, tenure and offer dates | Quote the current approved product sheet only | Extend an Apple/brand offer to other products or dates |

K&S should collect only the minimum sale and partner-reference information. Customer KYC, bank statements, credentials, OTPs, card PIN/CVV and finance application data should go directly to the partner's approved channel unless a signed agreement and professional review require a narrowly controlled exception.

## Merchant-economics model

For every financed sale, calculate:

`Net financed-sale contribution = selling price − landed product cost − ordinary card/QR acquiring cost − EMI/BNPL merchant fee or subsidy − promotion/gift cost − financing-specific refund/chargeback cost − incremental operating cost`

Do not compare a partner fee only with revenue. Compare it with gross profit and contribution by SKU.

### Illustrative sensitivity — not a partner quotation

Assume a NPR 100,000 handset and 5.5% product gross margin before payment/financing cost. Initial gross profit is NPR 5,500.

| Incremental financing cost | Cost | Gross profit left before ordinary MDR/operations | Product margin left | Gross-profit erosion |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.75% | NPR 750 | NPR 4,750 | 4.75% | 13.6% |
| 2.5% | NPR 2,500 | NPR 3,000 | 3.0% | 45.5% |
| 5.0% | NPR 5,000 | NPR 500 | 0.5% | 90.9% |
| 10.0% | NPR 10,000 | **NPR -4,500** | **-4.5%** | 181.8% |

**Calculated estimate:** even a modest-looking percentage can consume a large share of handset gross profit. The table excludes ordinary MDR, VAT/tax treatment, accessories, incentives, returns and staff cost. It is a sensitivity test, not a K&S price or approved subsidy.

Merchant-funded 0% should therefore be SKU- and campaign-specific, ideally supported by bank, brand or distributor funding. K&S should not fund a flat percentage across thin-margin and high-margin categories without a contribution-floor decision.

## Written proposal schedule

Request the same dated schedule from at least four providers:

### Legal, licensing and scope

- Exact licensed bank/financial institution and any PSO/PSP roles
- Contracting entity, merchant category, Nepalgunj coverage and authorised sales channels
- Eligible products/SKUs, minimum/maximum ticket, tenure and offer validity
- Prohibited categories or cross-border use

### Customer terms

- Existing-card/account requirement and new-customer documents
- Credit-limit/down-payment rules and application/approval timing
- Interest, processing, annual/card, insurance or other fees
- Instalment, total payable, prepayment, late/penal charge and complaint route
- Decline/expiry/failure handling without revealing unnecessary personal decision data to K&S

### Merchant economics

- Ordinary POS/QR MDR and EMI-specific merchant fee/subsidy, separately stated
- Who funds 0%: bank, merchant, brand, distributor or shared pool
- VAT/withholding/tax treatment and invoice/credit-note evidence
- Gross or net settlement, settlement account and T+ timing
- Reserve/holdback, minimum volume, target, clawback and campaign obligations
- POS/portal/setup, training, maintenance and marketing-material cost

### Exceptions and risk

- Authorisation condition required before product release
- Failed/duplicate conversion, reversal and reconciliation workflow
- Cancellation, partial/full return, exchange, refund and prepayment treatment
- Fraud, stolen card/account, chargeback and merchant-liability allocation
- Product dispute versus finance dispute ownership
- Data controller/processor roles, access, retention, breach and audit obligations
- Named support contacts, escalation, complaint SLA and exit/termination handling

No verbal “zero cost,” same-day settlement or refund promise counts as proposal evidence.

1. **Kumari and Machhapuchchhre:** obtain proposals first because official merchant lists prove current Nepalgunj mobile/electronics deployment.
2. **Foneloan/Fonepay with the acquiring bank:** verify whether K&S's ordinary Fonepay QR can receive BNPL proceeds, the merchant receives normal immediate settlement, and no special category/contract restriction applies.
3. **NMB:** test the customer-controlled any-merchant conversion route as a lower-subsidy alternative; K&S should not advertise conversion as guaranteed.
4. **Nabil:** request general electronics and Apple-specific terms for premium customers, subject to K&S authorised-channel status.
5. **NIC ASIA, Global IME and Laxmi Sunrise:** compare explicit/flexible cost structures and selected-merchant programmes against the local candidates.

## Recommended launch sequence — hypothesis requiring approval

### Phase 0 — payment and control readiness

- Establish business bank, POS and QR acquiring arrangements.
- Finalise daily reconciliation, refund, product-release and exception controls.
- Obtain current licensing, legal, tax/accounting, data/privacy and consumer-disclosure review.
- Train staff to show cash price and total financing obligation side by side.

K&S may accept ordinary approved payments in this phase but should not advertise a K&S EMI programme.

### Phase 1 — proposals and controlled demonstrations

- Obtain four complete written proposals using the common schedule.
- Run test transactions/refunds in a non-customer or bank-supervised environment where permitted.
- Build an offer-version register and one True Cost Sheet per proposed offer.
- Founder approves partner, category, maximum merchant cost, contribution floor, pilot cap and communication.

### Phase 2 — narrow pilot

Pilot for a founder-approved transaction or time cap with no more than two K&S-sponsored offers plus one customer-controlled route. Suggested complementarity:

- one local participating-merchant card programme;
- one QR/app pre-approved BNPL route;
- one post-purchase/customer-funded conversion route.

This cap is a research recommendation, not an accepted operating limit.

### Phase 3 — scale or stop

Review conversion, contribution, settlement, refund, complaint and control evidence. Expand only if the route adds eligible customers without hiding cost, destroying category contribution or creating unresolved reconciliation/customer harm.

## Pilot evidence and stop conditions

Record for each offer and transaction:

- customer route and issuing partner, without storing unnecessary KYC data;
- cash price, financed amount, down payment, customer fee/interest and total payable;
- merchant fee, MDR, co-funding and net contribution;
- application started, approved/declined/abandoned and elapsed time;
- authorisation before release, settlement due/received and mismatch;
- cancellation/return/refund time and residual customer balance confirmation;
- complaint category, owner, next update and closure;
- staff disclosure acknowledgement and any prohibited claim.

Stop the affected offer immediately if product is released without required funding authorisation, settlement cannot be reconciled, the merchant cost breaches the approved contribution floor, customer total cost cannot be reproduced, refunds leave unexplained finance balances, staff mis-sell eligibility/cost, or the partner does not resolve a material security/privacy/control incident.

## Regulatory and complaint boundary

Partner status must be checked against the current [NRB licensed BFI list](https://www.nrb.org.np/category/list-of-bfis/?department=bfr) and, where a payment operator/provider is involved, the [NRB licensed PSO/PSP list](https://www.nrb.org.np/departments/psd/). A technology vendor or payment brand is not automatically the lender.

The financing partner owns underwriting, approval, repayment account and credit complaints. K&S owns accurate offer explanation, sale/fulfilment evidence, merchant-side settlement/refund coordination and escalation. Customers should first receive the partner's complaint route; unresolved licensed-provider complaints can be escalated through the [NRB Grievance Management System](https://gunaso.nrb.org.np/).

Qualified Nepal legal, banking, tax/accounting, privacy/data-security and consumer-protection review remains mandatory before launch.

## Batch synthesis

### What we learned

- Nepalgunj already has participating electronics/mobile EMI merchants; EMI itself is table stakes.
- Card-based EMI is substantially narrower in potential reach than mobile/QR access.
- Customer-controlled conversion and pre-approved QR BNPL can complement merchant-sponsored card offers.
- Public product pages rarely disclose complete merchant economics, settlement and refund liability.

### What changed in prior assumptions

- “Launch EMI” must become a portfolio of clearly labelled routes, not one generic feature.
- “0%” is not evidence of zero K&S cost.
- Cardless does not mean universal: digital BNPL is pre-approved and often concentrated among salary/regular-income customers.
- A partner's broad national product does not establish K&S merchant participation or Nepalgunj support.

### K&S opportunities

- Offer a transparent cash-versus-finance True Cost Sheet instead of promotional ambiguity.
- Combine one local card route with a verified QR/app option rather than signing many overlapping banks.
- Use premium/product-specific financing only where distributor/brand funding protects contribution.
- Make settlement/refund case ownership a service advantage.

### Risks

- Merchant subsidy can erase thin handset margin.
- Staff may promise approval, zero cost or instant conversion beyond written terms.
- Returns and finance reversals can leave the customer paying after K&S has taken the product back.
- Manual forms, offer expiries and multiple partners can create reconciliation and privacy failures.

### Unresolved questions

- Exact K&S merchant fee, MDR, settlement, reserve, refund and chargeback terms by provider.
- Current Nepalgunj approval rate, financed ticket and preferred tenure.
- Which banks will approve K&S's entity/category and co-fund offers.
- Whether Fonepay QR BNPL requires special merchant enrolment or category controls for K&S.

### Recommended next evidence

1. Send the common proposal schedule to four priority providers.
2. Add EMI questions and approval-route observation to the local customer and mystery-shop work.
3. Run supervised test authorisation, settlement and refund flows before public launch.
4. Continue the next desk-research batch on official-versus-grey-versus-India-border buying and price gaps while proposals are pending.
