## Decision question

Which consumer segments, device jobs, price bands and geographic origins should K&S test before choosing an opening assortment—and what can current official evidence genuinely establish?

## Evidence labels

- **V — verified fact:** an official/primary source or directly collected auditable evidence.
- **E — estimate:** a transparent calculation from stated inputs.
- **H — hypothesis:** a proposition requiring local behavioral, commercial or operating evidence.
- **D — founder decision:** an accepted choice in `decisions/`; this study creates none.

## Executive findings

1. **V — Digital access is already broad, while computer access is much narrower.** Nepal MICS 2024–25 reports a smartphone in 91.1% and home internet in 90.2% of Lumbini Urban households, but a computer in only 19.8%. Among Lumbini Urban people aged 15–49, 78.6% owned a smartphone and 16.7% had used a computer in the prior three months. Household access is not individual ownership, a unique-device count, replacement demand or sales.
2. **V — The urban/rural computing divide is larger than the smartphone divide.** Lumbini Rural households reported 85.7% smartphone access and 11.8% computer access; people aged 15–49 reported 71.0% smartphone ownership and 7.2% recent computer use. This supports separate phone and computing research—not an assumption that one category's demand predicts the other.
3. **V — Nepalgunj contains identifiable education, professional, trade, migrant-linked and accessibility research frames.** Census counts can help recruit and interpret segments, but occupation, education, enterprise or migration status does not establish ability or willingness to buy a device.
4. **V — Affordability is heterogeneous.** The NSO reported 24.08% poverty incidence in Lumbini Urban in 2022/23 and a cost-of-basic-needs poverty line of NPR 75,468 per person per year. This is a welfare threshold, not average income, household disposable cash or a device budget.
5. **Evidence gap — No authoritative public source located reports Nepalgunj or Western Nepal 2026 phone/laptop units by retail price band, current brand share, replacement interval, premium-buyer count, retailer share or origin-linked catchment.** Customs, MRP lists and installed-base statistics cannot fill those gaps.
6. **H — K&S should initially test a phone-led value/replacement market, selective step-up and premium demand, a narrower job-led laptop market and assisted ownership needs.** The proposed segments below are research strata, not accepted customer personas or stocking allocations.
7. **Method boundary — “Underserved” requires evidence of an important unmet outcome, sufficient reachable demand, positive contribution and reliable delivery.** A demographic group, low device-access rate or enthusiastic interview alone is insufficient.

## Affordability boundary

The [Nepal Living Standards Survey IV](https://data.nsonepal.gov.np/dataset/b6c3c19b-4b15-44bf-8653-1571e76dad14/resource/46e9357a-143c-4a70-98c9-c7a29aba7b70/download/nepal-living-standard-survey.pdf) reports Lumbini Urban poverty incidence of 24.08% and an annual per-person basic-needs poverty line of NPR 75,468 in 2022/23 (NPR 34,419 food and NPR 41,049 non-food). The poverty line is a welfare threshold. It must not be used as an average income, savings estimate, EMI capacity, device budget or premium-market denominator.

Implication: fieldwork must capture exact executable budget, payment route, timing, repair-versus-replace choice and household decision role. A single “middle class” or “premium customer” label is not analytically safe.

## Testable consumer-demand architecture

These are **H** until local evidence satisfies the stated test.

| Hypothesis segment | Device job to test | Candidate K&S treatment | Minimum evidence before assortment action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential/value replacement | Reliable communication, battery, common apps and repairability under a hard cash limit | Small set of explainable value options, durable accessories and clear ownership cost | Recent purchases/lost sales by exact SKU and price; contribution and service outcomes |
| Step-up phone/productivity | Better camera, storage, performance, battery and longer useful life | Needs-led comparison across exact alternatives; finance only when eligible and economic | Observed switching from lower/higher bands, actual quotes, conversion and use-case fit |
| Premium/halo | Ecosystem, camera, status, performance, long use, gift or business-critical continuity | Live demonstration and fast advance-order fulfilment; shallow speculative stock | Repeat executable demand, deposits/orders, positive downside contribution and low ageing |
| Student/first laptop | Study, office work, online learning and portability | Comparable configured demos, genuine software/warranty and rapid order route | Exact budget/configuration, course/job, financing, lead-time and service evidence |
| Work/creator/business laptop | Reliability, invoicing, professional software, performance and downtime reduction | Job-led configuration, business invoice, setup and support SLA | Recent purchases, workload proof, authorized availability, margin and support cost |
| Rural/wider-catchment buyer | Travel-efficient purchase, battery/power resilience, remote support and fewer repeat trips | Appointment/stock confirmation, bundled compatibility check, delivery/pickup where economic | Origin-linked visits/sales, travel burden, route cost, serviceability and repeat use |
| Assisted/accessibility buyer | Readability, hearing/motor support, caregiver handover, privacy-safe setup | Accessible demo, patient comparison, documented setup and support contact | Co-designed interviews, actual use, consent/privacy controls and delivery reliability |
| Migrant-linked household/gift buyer | Remote product decision, funds coordination, verified handover and recipient support | Remote quote/proof, payer-recipient consent separation and supported handover | Verified cases, payment/legal/privacy controls, conversion and support cost |

The segment names describe device jobs and buying constraints. They are not stereotypes; one customer can occupy several segments.

## Price-band evidence protocol

The bands below are **field coding strata**, chosen to expose discontinuities and keep previous evidence comparable. They are not estimates of demand share or approved merchandising tiers.

### Phones

- below NPR 15,000;
- NPR 15,000–24,999;
- NPR 25,000–34,999;
- NPR 35,000–49,999;
- NPR 50,000–79,999;
- NPR 80,000–99,999;
- NPR 100,000 and above.

### Laptops

- below NPR 60,000;
- NPR 60,000–79,999;
- NPR 80,000–129,999;
- NPR 130,000 and above.

For every recent purchase, serious alternative, quote and lost sale, record exact configuration, final executable price, payment route, seller/channel, purchase timing, device job, current/previous device, replacement trigger and outcome. Analysts may combine adjacent cells after collection; they must not infer a bestseller band from a quota designed to fill cells.

The canonical 100-interview customer study uses broader quota strata to maintain feasible cell sizes. Exact budgets and prices should be retained so this more granular coding can be applied during analysis.

## Brand-preference evidence protocol

Public MRP availability, competitor shelf presence, distributor enthusiasm and national import totals do not establish local brand preference. The customer instrument should measure, in order:

1. last device brand/model and evidenced purchase channel;
2. first unaided brand considered for the next stated job;
3. complete considered set and executable quoted alternatives;
4. chosen and rejected brands with unprompted reasons;
5. prompted trade-offs only afterward: price, camera, battery, ecosystem, design/status, service, warranty, resale, finance and trusted recommendation;
6. actual choice, cancellation, return, repair and repeat-purchase outcome.

Report brand recall, consideration and purchase separately. A directional purposive sample cannot be labelled Nepalgunj brand share. Assortment treatment requires corroborating sell-through, lost-sale, distributor and contribution evidence.

## Catchment definition and measurement

Use mutually exclusive origin zones for every consenting competitor, funnel and completed-purchase record:

| Zone | Definition | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|
| A | Nepalgunj resident | Resident funnel and sales analysis |
| B1 | Kohalpur resident | Named Banke comparison |
| B2 | Other Banke resident | Wider-district catchment |
| C | Karnali or other Nepal resident | Wider-Western-Nepal catchment; preserve district/locality internally where safe |
| D | India resident or other origin | Cross-border/other origin; keep separate from Nepal catchment |

Worker/student and frequent-shopper relationship should be recorded separately from residence. Count catchment only from origin-linked interviews, visits, quotes or transactions after duplicate control. Airport passengers, road traffic, district population, social followers and institutional account counts can support sampling and logistics; none is a retail-demand multiplier.

Four weeks of evidence is the minimum pilot window. Report qualified visits, quotes, completed sales, returns/cancellations, contribution and service cases by origin. Do not extrapolate an annual catchment uplift until seasonality and channel coverage are understood.

## Underserved-segment test

A segment becomes a K&S opportunity candidate only when all four dimensions are evidenced:

1. **Need:** repeated experienced burden, lost sale or unresolved ownership outcome—not only stated importance.
2. **Reach:** enough origin-linked qualified demand to justify the offer and operating coverage.
3. **Economics:** positive contribution after stock, discount, finance, delivery, labor, warranty, failure and capital cost.
4. **Delivery:** K&S and approved partners meet the service promise safely and consistently.

Classify the result as table stakes, experiment, differentiator candidate or reject. Founder approval is required before a permanent assortment, public premium, subsidized route or service promise.

## Evidence plan and stopping rules

1. Complete the canonical 12 cognitive interviews and 100 valid directional customer interviews; preserve exact device, budget, origin and brand fields.
2. Obtain eight to ten comparable last-30/90-day sell-through records with price band, brand, model family and evidence quality.
3. Run the same-day 12-phone/3-laptop basket across named competitor geographies.
4. Record four weeks of K&S or participating-retailer funnel, origin, quote, conversion, loss reason and contribution evidence.
5. Keep the twelve-account institutional lane separate under RESEARCH-INSTITUTION-001.
6. If a population price-band, brand-share or catchment estimate is required for a high-capital decision, commission a probability-oriented design with an explicit sampling frame; do not stretch the purposive pilot into that purpose.
7. Stop stocking expansion when evidence is missing, cells conflict materially, delivery/service fails, or downside contribution and ageing exceed the approved limits.

## Major-batch synthesis

### What we learned

Official evidence supports a digitally connected, phone-led market context and a meaningfully narrower computing category. Nepalgunj also has credible education, work, small-business, migrant-linked and accessibility recruitment frames. None establishes price-band sales, brand shares or premium demand.

### What changed in assumptions

The earlier NPR 15,000–35,000 “strongest band,” sub-NPR-30,000 centre of gravity and brand allocation percentages are reclassified as hypotheses and field coding inputs. High smartphone access does not by itself prove a large annual replacement market; low computer access does not by itself prove unmet laptop demand.

### K&S opportunities

Test a value-led phone core with selective step-up/premium fulfilment, a job-led laptop range, and assistance that reduces travel, configuration, accessibility and ownership burden. A catchment advantage should be built from reliable fulfilment and service evidence, not a gateway narrative.

### Risks and disconfirming evidence

Wide smartphone access may coexist with long replacement cycles, low margins or price-only choice. A smaller active computer-user base may make laptop breadth uneconomic. Segment labels can create false confidence if they are not tied to actual quotes, purchases and contribution.

### Unresolved questions

What are current local units and contribution by exact price band and brand? Which origins create qualified visits and completed sales? What triggers replacement or upgrade? Which customer jobs justify premium/order-led stock, finance or support?

### Recommended next research

Activate the supervised field pilot after founder/privacy approval. Freeze the reviewed instrument, collect origin-linked exact-SKU behavior, obtain comparable seller sell-through, and use the resulting evidence to replace—not merely decorate—the current assortment hypotheses.
