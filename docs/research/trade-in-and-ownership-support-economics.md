## Decision question and evidence labels

Which ownership-support services can KAMLESH & SONS launch without taking unpriced used-device, custody, privacy, warranty, tax or future-value risk?

- **V — verified fact:** supported by a current official or primary source.
- **P — public claim:** a company describes its own program; execution quality is not independently established.
- **E — estimate:** calculated from disclosed inputs.
- **H — hypothesis:** requires partner, customer or operating validation.
- **D — founder decision:** requires an explicit decision record; this study creates none.

## Executive findings

1. **P/V:** Structured trade-in is not hypothetical in Nepal. IMS Group described a 2024 SabKo Phone exchange facility for Samsung-authorised IMS Smart outlets and authorised resellers in Kathmandu and the “west region,” using a web-based valuation. Current exact Nepalgunj outlet coverage, eligible brands, commercial split and dispute ownership remain unverified.
2. **P:** SabKo Phone currently advertises automated valuation, certified data erasure, refurbishment, warranty and take-back. These claims make it a high-priority partner lead, not proof of K&S terms or outcomes.
3. **V:** NTA's MDMS portal supports registration/status and lost/stolen reporting. MDMS evidence is one control; it does not alone prove lawful ownership, absence of finance/account locks, device condition, parts originality or the right to resell.
4. **V:** Nepal's Consumer Protection Act gives consumers rights to information on price/quality and addresses warranty/repair disclosure for durable electronic goods. A refurbished/open-box resale therefore needs explicit condition, testing, prior-repair/parts, warranty and remedy terms—not “like new” as an unsupported label.
5. **V:** Nepal's Privacy Act protects information and communications on electronic media and restricts unauthorized collection/use. NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2, issued September 2025, treats sanitization as a controlled program with validation, not a casual factory-reset promise.
6. **V:** The Nepal VAT Act treats barter/exchanged goods at market value and separately contemplates prescribed input-tax treatment for dealers in used goods. This does not resolve K&S invoicing or input-credit treatment; a written accountant/tax position is required before principal trading or guaranteed buyback.
7. **P/V:** Apple, Google and Samsung trade-in guidance demonstrates recurring controls: estimated versus final inspection value, activation/account lock removal, factory reset/data preparation, lost/stolen exclusion, exact model/IMEI and physical/functional inspection.
8. **H:** K&S's safest launch route is a disclosed **partner pass-through exchange** with K&S owning customer explanation and case status while the qualified partner owns final valuation, title purchase, payment and used-stock risk. K&S should not initially buy used devices onto its own balance sheet or promise future values.
9. **H:** Repair concierge and pickup/drop can be earlier pilots than own-inventory trade-in if partner SLAs, chain of custody, privacy, insurance and per-case economics pass. A loaner pool is a narrow later pilot, not a default promise.

## Trade-in structure taxonomy

| Structure | Who buys/owns the used device? | Customer value timing | K&S exposure | Launch treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Referral only | Partner contracts directly with customer | Partner pays/credits customer | Reputation and referral disclosure | Lowest-risk discovery route |
| Partner pass-through at K&S | Partner is principal buyer; K&S facilitates inspection/handoff | Final partner value offsets new sale or is paid by partner | Intake, explanation, custody and dispute handoff | Preferred controlled pilot |
| K&S agent for disclosed partner | Partner is principal; K&S acts under written agency | According to partner terms | Agency authority, customer disclosure, funds/custody | Legal/tax review first |
| K&S exchange credit with immediate partner transfer | K&S grants credit then transfers device/receivable to partner | Immediate final or conditional credit | Repricing/chargeback, settlement and cash timing | Pilot only after supervised settlement test |
| K&S principal buyback / used inventory | K&S purchases and resells/refurbishes | Cash/store credit at acquisition | Full title, tax, fraud, price, repair, warranty and stock risk | Do not launch initially |
| Assured future buyback | K&S/partner promises a future formula or percentage at new sale | Redemption months later if conditions pass | Contingent liability, condition disputes and funding | Partner-backed pilot only after reserve/legal review |
| Recycling/take-back | Approved downstream handler receives no-resale stock | Usually no or scrap value | Data, environmental and chain-of-custody claims | Separate documented route |

**Control:** “exchange bonus” and used-device value must be separate. A temporary brand subsidy cannot be presented as the phone's intrinsic valuation.

## Legal, tax, privacy and accounting boundary

Before any principal purchase, resale, refurbishment, assured buyback or cash payout, obtain a written Nepal adviser schedule covering:

1. legal form of exchange, agency and principal purchase;
2. seller identity/age/authority, ownership declaration and evidence proportional to risk;
3. stolen/lost, counterfeit, altered IMEI, lien/finance lock and dispute escalation;
4. purchase record, VAT invoice, barter/market-value rule and used-goods input-tax treatment;
5. inventory cost, impairment, warranty/refund reserve and buyback contingent liability;
6. required disclosures for used/refurbished/open-box condition and any replaced/non-original parts;
7. consumer remedies, warranty wording, price/grade dispute and unfair-practice risk;
8. personal-information collection, purpose, consent, access, sharing, retention and deletion;
9. data-sanitization method, validation, failed-wipe/quarantine and certificate wording;
10. e-waste/recycling route and any environmental or local permits;
11. insurance for customer devices, used stock, transit, fraud, theft, employee dishonesty and cyber/privacy events.

Do not copy an Indian partner's identity collection or waiver language into Nepal policy. Collect the minimum data a qualified Nepal review supports, store identity separately from diagnostic content and restrict access.

## Transparent valuation system

### Quote states

Every customer sees one of four states:

1. **Indicative estimate:** based on declared model/condition; not final.
2. **Provisional inspected value:** deductions itemized; account/title/data checks still open.
3. **Final accepted value:** customer and principal buyer accept the same signed condition/price record.
4. **Settled:** new-sale credit or partner payment reconciled; device custody transferred.

The customer must be able to reject a reduced final value and recover the unchanged device unless the disclosed instant-exchange structure makes handover simultaneous and the customer freely accepts the final result.

### Inspection and grade record

Capture exact model/storage/colour, IMEI/serial, MDMS result, ownership evidence type, lost/stolen check, finance/network/account/activation locks, power/charge, battery health and safety, display/touch/pixels, body/frame/back, cameras, speakers/microphones, ports/buttons, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/cellular, biometrics, sensors, water/repair indicators where reliably testable, parts/service warnings, accessories and photographs with consent.

Suggested labels are descriptive rather than promotional:

| Grade | Minimum description | Customer proof |
|---|---|---|
| A | Fully functional; very light wear; no disqualifying lock/safety issue | Checklist plus photos/diagnostic output |
| B | Fully functional; visible ordinary wear; stated battery/cosmetic deductions | Same evidence with itemized deductions |
| C | Functional but material disclosed wear or repair need within accepted scope | Repair estimate and resale/warranty route |
| Reject/recycle | Lost/stolen/ownership concern, unsafe/swollen battery, prohibited lock, altered identity, counterfeit or uneconomic/unsupported repair | Reason code; no unsupported accusation |

Grade alone never sets price. Model liquidity, storage, age, local demand, channel/warranty provenance, parts history and current downstream bids also matter.

### Maximum offer economics

For a partner pass-through route:

`K&S case contribution = disclosed partner commission + incremental new-sale contribution − staff/diagnostic time − pickup/packaging − payment cost − expected dispute/chargeback cost`

For any future K&S principal-buying route:

`Maximum final offer = conservative net resale proceeds − diagnostics/sanitization − refurb parts/labour − logistics/marketplace/payment fees − tax/accounting cost − warranty/return reserve − fraud/title reserve − holding/price-decline reserve − target contribution`

`Expected holding loss = probability-weighted price reduction × expected days held`

Use the lower of model value and a verified executable downstream bid. A social-media asking price is not net realizable value.

### Assured-buyback liability

`Expected cohort liability = eligible devices sold × redemption probability × expected(max(contract payout − downstream recovery − customer/partner fees, 0))`

Stress test higher redemption, lower recovery, adverse model mix, more condition disputes and delayed partner settlement. Record the responsible legal obligor, funding/reserve, cancellation/return treatment and whether the promise survives partner failure. Do not sell a K&S-guaranteed percentage before the founder approves the maximum liability.

## Data preparation and sanitization

The customer—not staff—should authenticate accounts and initiate backup/sign-out/erase wherever possible. K&S must never ask the customer to disclose Apple/Google passwords, banking credentials, wallet PINs or OTPs.

Sequence:

1. explain that valuation diagnostics can occur before customer data is surrendered;
2. let the customer back up and transfer data, with consent-based K&S assistance if selected;
3. remove SIM/eSIM according to approved instructions and remove memory cards;
4. customer disables Find My/Activation Lock or Android/OEM account protection without sharing passwords;
5. customer initiates the approved device-specific erase/factory reset;
6. trained staff validate reset/account-lock state without opening personal content;
7. record sanitization method, operator, date, validation result, exception and custody ID;
8. quarantine any device that cannot be reliably sanitized; do not resell it;
9. issue a narrowly worded sanitization record only for the validated procedure actually performed.

NIST SP 800-88r2 is a program benchmark, not a Nepal legal safe harbor and not proof that every phone's factory reset meets a higher “purge” outcome. K&S must approve methods by device/media type and validate its partner's tools and evidence.

## Repair-concierge operating model

K&S can own the **case** without pretending to make the brand's warranty decision or perform work it has outsourced.

### Case states

`Booked → received/condition captured → diagnostic route confirmed → estimate/brand submission → customer approval or warranty review → partner custody → repair/parts wait → return test → customer handover → financial/warranty reconciliation → closed/reopened`

Every state has an owner, timestamp, next-update date and customer-facing description. “Sent outside” is not a status.

### Service economics

`Case contribution = diagnostic/coordination fee + disclosed partner commission/parts-labour margin − staff and communication time − courier/pickup − payment cost − rework/refund reserve − custody/privacy incident reserve`

Track first-time resolution, estimate variance, promised-versus-actual days, no-fault-found, customer approvals, reopen/repeat failure, partner rejection, unrecovered cost, status-update compliance and contribution by route. A fast-looking repair with high rework or custody risk is not a successful service.

### Partner SLA schedule

Require legal identity, authorisations and technician competence; accepted brands/models/jobs; genuine/alternative-parts terminology; diagnostic fee; estimate approval; turnaround by job class; parts availability; warranty and repeat-fault remedy; data-access method; subcontracting; custody/transport; additional damage/loss; old-parts return; status API/message; invoice/VAT; dispute, indemnity, insurance, audit and termination.

Warranty jobs follow the authorised brand/distributor route. Independent repair must never be described as brand-authorised without proof.

## Pickup/drop economics and custody

`Fully loaded trip cost = runner time + fuel/vehicle + insurance + secure packaging/seals + communications + failed-attempt/re-delivery + allocated supervision`

`Cost per completed stop = route cost ÷ completed handovers`

Measure zone, distance, route density, first-attempt success, minutes/stop, late/lost/damaged events and customer willingness to pay. “Free” pickup/drop must have a named funding source: service contribution, membership allowance, brand/partner support or capped promotion.

Minimum custody controls:

- unique case and tamper-evident pouch/seal number;
- exact device/serial, visible condition, accessories and consented photos at each handoff;
- named runner/partner, timestamps and status events;
- no undocumented subcontracting or overnight vehicle storage;
- customer identity plus non-banking handover code at return;
- restricted secure storage and daily exception reconciliation;
- insurer-approved loss/damage/liability limits and customer disclosure;
- incident freeze, escalation, evidence preservation and notification process.

## Loaner/standby-device model

Loaners reduce downtime but create capital, data, SIM, loss, damage, late-return and support risk. Begin only with a small serialized pool for narrowly eligible cases after insurer/legal/privacy approval.

`Loaner cost per completed case = (annual depreciation + maintenance + setup/wipe + insurance + expected loss/damage + administration − collected fees) ÷ completed loaner cases`

Record acquisition cost, residual value, useful life, available/issued/quarantine/repair state, battery, accessories, issue/return photos, borrower, linked service case, due date, extensions, fees/deposit/deductible, wipe/validation and next-ready approval.

Eligibility could be limited to selected warranty/paid repairs with estimated downtime above a founder-approved number of days. The loaner is not a like-for-like model guarantee. Prohibit customer banking/wallet setup where practical, provide a clean setup guide and never retain customer credentials. A returned unit remains quarantined until condition, accounts, SIM/storage and sanitization validation pass.

Do not launch when replacement cost, pool availability, late return, customer damage, data isolation, insurer cover or cross-customer sanitization is unresolved.

## Launch ladder and approval gates

### Recommended ladder

1. Warranty/repair information and appointment booking with no custody.
2. Numbered in-store intake and retailer-owned status updates for an authorised/qualified partner.
3. Paid or tightly zoned pickup/drop after supervised route tests and insurance.
4. Partner-principal trade-in quote/referral with no K&S custody or credit.
5. Partner pass-through exchange at K&S after valuation, settlement, return and dispute tests.
6. Small loaner pool for narrowly eligible cases.
7. K&S-owned used inventory or assured buyback only after sufficient cohort, resale, tax, warranty and reserve evidence.

### Non-negotiable gates

No service progresses when:

- the legal principal, warranty decision-maker, data processor or custody owner is unclear;
- the customer cannot see estimate versus final price, deductions, fees, warranty and recourse;
- activation/account/finance/lost-stolen/ownership checks fail or remain unresolved;
- a partner requires customer passwords or uncontrolled access to personal content;
- device wipe/sanitization cannot be validated;
- tax/invoice/accounting treatment is unsigned;
- downstream payment/credit, return, dispute and chargeback behavior is untested;
- insurance excludes customer devices, used stock or transit needed by the model;
- downside contribution or assured-buyback reserve is unacceptable;
- the operating team cannot reconcile every serialized device daily.

## Field and partner evidence plan

1. Obtain at least three current partner proposals: SabKo Phone/IMS route plus two credible Nepal alternatives, using one common schedule.
2. Mystery-shop eight Nepal trade-ins (two budget Android, two mid-range Android, two premium Android and two iPhones) at the same declared condition; record estimate, final/deductions, proof requests, data steps, quote validity, settlement, warranty and recourse.
3. Re-inspect four devices independently to measure inter-rater grading consistency; never sell the devices as part of research without owner approval.
4. Run supervised test exchanges for one low-, one mid- and one premium-value device after legal/tax approval; reconcile device, customer credit, partner settlement and any re-grade.
5. Complete ten real service-case interviews from the customer-friction protocol and at least three full partner workflow simulations.
6. Obtain two insurer quotations covering in-store custody, transit, used stock, employee dishonesty and privacy/cyber incidents; record deductibles and exclusions.
7. Obtain an accountant memo on exchange/barter VAT, used-goods input credit, purchase records, resale invoicing, warranty reserve and contingent buyback liability.
8. Obtain legal review of seller declaration, privacy/consent, partner agency, custody, liability, deductions, loaner and buyback wording.
9. Pilot pickup/drop by zone and route, logging full cost and failures; do not call it free until funding is approved.
10. After 30–50 completed partner cases, compare conversion, realized contribution, disputes, settlement days, customer effort and repeat intent before expanding scope.

## Founder decisions still required

This research does **not** approve trade-in, cash buyback, K&S-owned used stock, guaranteed future value, a partner, pickup/drop, a loaner pool, customer-device insurance limits, grading rules, fees, deposits, reserves or refurbished-device warranty. Each requires its documented gate and explicit approval.

## Major-batch synthesis

### What we learned

Nepal already has organized trade-in/refurbishment signals, including a relevant IMS/SabKo Phone route, but public pages do not disclose the economics or execution needed for K&S approval. Ownership-support value comes from transparent handoffs and case control; the largest risks arise when the retailer becomes the undisclosed buyer, guarantor, custodian or data processor.

### What changed in assumptions

“Trade-in” is no longer one feature: it is separated into referral, partner-principal, conditional credit, K&S principal inventory and assured future buyback. A factory reset is no longer treated as sufficient proof of sanitization, and “free pickup”/“loaner” now require full-cost and incident reserves.

### K&S opportunity

K&S can offer a lower-risk integrated ownership path—documented valuation, customer-controlled data preparation, retailer-owned status updates and partner-backed exchange—before taking used inventory or future-value risk.

### Risks and disconfirming evidence

Condition re-grades, stolen/encumbered devices, activation/finance locks, unsafe batteries, partner settlement delays, data incidents, used-stock warranty and rapid price decline can erase both trade-in contribution and customer trust. Public “up to” buyback values are not evidence of expected payout.

### Unresolved questions

Does an approved partner cover Nepalgunj, who is principal buyer, what does K&S earn/pay, how often are quotes reduced, how fast does settlement occur, and who bears data, tax, warranty and customer-dispute liability?

### Recommended next research

Execute the common partner proposal and eight-device mystery-shop before choosing a route. In parallel, obtain accountant, lawyer and insurer memos. The next desk batch should integrate demand, assortment and flagship differentiation only after these commercial evidence requests are actively underway.
