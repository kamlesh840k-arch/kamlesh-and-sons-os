## Purpose

This file is the canonical index, coverage map and backlog for substantial KAMLESH & SONS market research. It prevents duplicate studies, makes uncertainty visible and connects research to decisions without silently turning hypotheses into policy.

The active flagship evidence package remains at [Nepalgunj Mobile Flagship Evidence](../evidence/nepalgunj-mobile-flagship/README.md). Cross-market and reusable studies live in this folder.

## Evidence labels

Every material claim should use one of these labels, either explicitly or through a clearly defined document-specific equivalent:

- **Verified fact (V):** supported by an identified primary/official source or directly collected evidence.
- **Public claim (P):** stated by a retailer, brand or other interested party; confirms what is advertised, not necessarily execution quality.
- **Secondary signal (S):** directory, established media, review or other non-primary evidence; useful for discovery, not final proof.
- **Estimate (E):** a calculation from stated inputs; method and limitations must be shown.
- **Hypothesis (H):** a proposition requiring customer, competitor, commercial or operational validation.
- **Founder decision (D):** an accepted decision recorded in `decisions/`; research cannot create one by implication.

Historical conversation notes and planning files can shape research questions, but unstable or factual claims from them must be independently verified before being promoted to V.

## Founder-decision boundary

This index does not approve pricing, a price premium, a competitor claim, a bank, a distributor, an inventory amount, trade-in, pickup/drop, a loaner pool, a protection plan or a launch date. Any such commitment requires its relevant evidence and an explicit founder decision where the decision architecture requires one.

## Major-batch synthesis template

Each completed batch should record:

1. What we learned.
2. What changed in prior assumptions.
3. K&S opportunities.
4. Risks and disconfirming evidence.
5. Unresolved questions.
6. Recommended next research.
