## Decision question

Can an institutional-device offer become a commercially defensible demand lane for the proposed K&S flagship without mistaking institution counts, budgets, tender notices or verbal interest for device orders?

## Evidence labels

- **V — verified fact:** supported by an official/primary source or directly collected documentary evidence.
- **P — public claim:** made by an interested organization; it does not prove execution.
- **S — secondary signal:** useful for discovery but insufficient for a material decision.
- **E — estimate:** a transparent calculation from stated inputs.
- **H — hypothesis:** must be tested locally.
- **D — founder decision:** accepted only through the decision system; this study creates none.

## Executive conclusion

1. **V:** Official 2024–25 education records show a substantial addressable institution base in Banke: 569 schools, including 377 community and 192 institutional schools. Nepalgunj accounts for 154 schools and Kohalpur for 80. These are organization counts, not device demand.
2. **V:** Nepalgunj's 80 public schools report uneven digital infrastructure: 67 have electricity, 26 have computers, 42 have internet and 14 have ICT labs. The gap is a discovery signal; it is not proof that the remaining schools have approved budgets or intend to buy from a retailer.
3. **V:** The official National Health Facility Registry returned 86 facilities in Nepalgunj on 2026-08-15: 31 government and 55 non-government. It returned 184 facilities across Banke and 37 in Kohalpur. Registry records are target-account discovery, not annual replacement units.
4. **V:** Formal local technology procurement exists. Lumbini Technological University issued an April 2024 electronic tender in Nepalgunj for supply, delivery and setup of computer-lab equipment, with bid security and e-GP submission requirements.
5. **V:** Nepalgunj's FY 2082/83 approved budget documents contain communications/IT and science/technology sector envelopes, but the published summary does not isolate device quantities, exact specifications, awards or actual expenditure. A budget line therefore cannot be booked as pipeline.
6. **H:** K&S may have a credible institutional lane if it can combine authorized products, exact specifications, VAT documentation, configuration, asset records, staged delivery and accountable warranty handling. Price alone is unlikely to be defensible in formal bids.
7. **E:** No annual institutional phone, laptop, tablet or accessory volume is estimated in this version. Public evidence is insufficient. The twelve-account protocol below is the minimum replacement evidence.

## Procurement evidence

### Local proof that formal technology buying occurs

The [Lumbini Technological University computer-lab tender](https://www.bolpatra.gov.np/egp/download?alfId=cm%3ABidding_Documentsnull%2Fcm%3AY2024%2Fcm%3AM3%40%40%40%40%40982542c0-3593-4d19-be41-04346f6a0dad&docId=79318767) was published on 2024-04-11 for supply, delivery and setup of equipment in Nepalgunj. The notice required electronic bidding, a NPR 3,000 bid-document fee, NPR 96,000 bid security and a 90-day bid-validity period.

This establishes a formal local procurement event and illustrates cash/security requirements. It does not establish the award value, actual quantities delivered, supplier margin, payment days or recurring annual demand.

### Current public-procurement route

- **V:** The Public Procurement Monitoring Office maintains the [e-GP system and bidder resources](https://ppmo.gov.np/content/7556/7556-e-gp-handsout-resources/) and publishes [annual reports](https://ppmo.gov.np/category/1112/).
- **V:** Nepalgunj Sub-Metropolitan City publishes a [public-procurement notice page](https://nepalgunjmun.gov.np/ne/public-procurement).
- **Boundary:** No procurement threshold, bidder-eligibility rule or tender method is stated here without checking the current Act, regulations and the specific solicitation. Requirements can differ by procuring entity, amount, funding source and document.

### Budget is not purchase evidence

The municipality's [FY 2082/83 approved budget](https://nepalgunjmun.gov.np/sites/nepalgunjmun.gov.np/files/%E0%A4%AC%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%9F%20%E0%A4%AA%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9F.pdf) presents approximately NPR 1.647 billion in total expenditure, including NPR 5.318 million under communications/information technology and NPR 0.932 million under science/technology. It also presents a NPR 2.280 million IT-branch ceiling.

These envelopes may include services, connectivity, software, maintenance or other activities. They do not prove device quantities, timing, award, supplier or spend. K&S should record a public budget only as **documented plan/context** unless a specification, invitation, award, purchase order, contract or payment record provides a stronger state.

### National specification benchmark, not local demand

A recent [Nepal Bank laptop bidding document](https://bolpatra.gov.np/egp/download?alfId=88b97d4d-0b2d-4170-a4c7-7f9a68ea65db&docId=87444778) required three-year OEM warranty, OEM-verifiable configuration, manufacturer authorization, pickup/delivery of faulty equipment and repair or replacement within two weeks. This is a national, non-Banke example. It shows the service and documentary standard K&S may need to meet in formal procurement; it does not prove local demand or that every tender uses the same terms.

## Institutional demand architecture

Institutional demand should be collected by use case rather than by organization count.

| Use case | Likely category | Evidence needed before sizing | K&S capability hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Administration and mobile workforce | Phones, laptops, accessories | Staff/user count, issue policy, last purchase, refresh rule, approved plan | Standard bundles, asset record and setup |
| Shared teaching/ICT lab | Desktops/laptops, displays, networking, UPS and peripherals | Lab seats, existing condition, power/network readiness, specification and funding | Authorized configuration plus staged installation |
| Health/field data collection | Phones/tablets/laptops, rugged cases and power | Program scope, security policy, offline/online needs, donor/government rules | Configuration, custody labels and rapid swap process |
| Executive/professional mobility | Premium phone/laptop and accessories | Role eligibility, replacement cycle, approval and warranty requirements | Exact-SKU sourcing, migration and ownership support |
| Replacement and repair | Parts/accessories, replacement devices, warranty coordination | Failure rate, downtime cost, service route and SLA | Case ownership and documented turnaround |
| Event/project purchase | Mixed devices and accessories | Funded project, quantity, delivery locations, deadline and acceptance test | Staged delivery and acceptance pack |

## Procurement-state taxonomy

Every opportunity must have exactly one state. Values in different states must never be added as if equally probable.

| State | Minimum evidence | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Completed historical purchase | Invoice, award, purchase order or system record | Use for past-volume and supplier-performance analysis only |
| Confirmed order/award | Signed purchase order, contract or award with quantity | Pipeline; still subject to delivery, acceptance and payment risk |
| Approved budget and specification | Approved plan plus identifiable quantity/specification | Documented plan; not booked revenue |
| Documented plan without approval | Dated official plan/minute/request | Early pipeline, separately reported |
| Verbal expectation | Named-role interview with period and quantity | Hypothesis; never mixed with documented pipeline |
| Exploratory interest | General interest without quantity/timing/authority | Qualitative discovery only |

## Minimum twelve-account field protocol

The existing twelve-interview commitment is retained. Use the canonical `Institutions` sheet: one organization × category row, a non-identifying organization code, evidence quality and separate last-12-month, confirmed, documented-plan and verbal quantities.

### Sector and geography quotas

- Five education accounts: at least one community school, two institutional schools, one campus and one technical/training institution.
- Three healthcare accounts: at least one government and one non-government facility.
- Two NGO/INGO or donor-funded project accounts.
- Two SME/public/professional-service accounts with recurring device use.
- At least eight interviews in Nepalgunj, two in Kohalpur and two in other Banke/wider catchment; geography and sector quotas can overlap.

### Required interview fields

1. Organization type, operating sites, approximate workforce/users and device-owning model.
2. Past 12-month quantity by phone, laptop, tablet, desktop and accessory family; record proof state.
3. Existing installed base, age bands, failure/repair backlog and normal replacement trigger.
4. Next 12-month confirmed, documented-plan, verbal and exploratory quantities kept separate.
5. Approved budget state, funding source, procurement calendar and decision/approval roles.
6. Procurement route: direct quotation, sealed quotation, e-GP/tender, framework, donor procedure or other.
7. Required bidder documents, VAT invoice, manufacturer authorization, bid/performance security and acceptance tests.
8. Exact configuration, operating system/licensing, software, security, MDM, network and power requirements.
9. Delivery sites, deadline, partial/staged delivery and installation/training expectations.
10. Contractual versus actual payment timing, retention, advance, credit need and dispute history.
11. Warranty term, local service route, pickup/drop, repair SLA, standby expectation and downtime cost.
12. Current supplier strengths/failures, switching triggers and willingness to use a new local authorized supplier.

### Evidence and confidentiality controls

- Ask for permission before copying a specification, invoice, award, quotation or contact detail.
- Keep personal contacts and unredacted commercial documents out of GitHub; register only a coded evidence reference and approved aggregate.
- Record “not known,” “refused” and “not applicable” explicitly; blank is not zero.
- Count one organization only once in the minimum through `first_record_for_organization`.
- Do not infer annual demand from schools, students, facilities, staff or beds without a documented purchasing rule.
- Do not publish named supplier failures or confidential prices without written permission.

## Commercial hypothesis for K&S

### Candidate offer

**H:** A small institutional procurement desk could differentiate K&S through:

1. authorized exact-SKU sourcing and VAT documentation;
2. compliance-ready quotation/specification response;
3. standard device/accessory bundles by role;
4. asset register with serial, warranty, user/site and acceptance fields;
5. staged delivery, setup, software/licensing and basic user handover;
6. one accountable warranty/repair case owner; and
7. transparent lead time, substitutions and exception approval.

### What K&S should not promise yet

- lowest-price guarantees;
- open credit or payment after sale;
- bid/performance security without a funded exposure limit;
- manufacturer authorization that has not been obtained;
- two-week repair, loaner devices or on-site support without partner capacity and cost proof;
- software, data migration, MDM or security services beyond trained/legal capability; or
- tender success, budget approval or future quantities.

## Account prioritization hypothesis

| Priority hypothesis | Initial targets | Reason to test | Principal risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Institutional schools, private campuses/training providers, non-government health facilities, established NGOs and SMEs | Potentially shorter route and need for local setup/service | Fragmented budgets, relationship-led buying, credit requests |
| B | Community schools, local-government health facilities and municipal bodies | Visible scale and formal documentation need | Tender rules, budget timing, price pressure and payment delay |
| C | Large public/externally funded procurements | Larger lots and reference value | Bid security, authorization, working capital, compliance and execution exposure |

This prioritization is H. Interview evidence, not organization type alone, determines the target list.

## Commercial gates before launch

1. Twelve valid interviews with the required sector/geography coverage.
2. At least six documentary past-purchase or plan records across at least three sectors.
3. A six-month procurement calendar with each item assigned a state and evidence reference.
4. Two authorized distributor responses confirming institutional quotation, warranty, lead-time and manufacturer-authorization support.
5. One accountant/legal review of tax, tender, credit, software/licensing, privacy and liability boundaries relevant to the intended offer.
6. Approved maximum open receivable, bid/performance-security and special-order exposure.
7. Three supervised small orders or simulations with actual quote time, gross contribution, fulfillment, acceptance, support load and payment days.
8. Explicit founder decision on scope, owner, exposure and public promise.

## Risks and controls

| Risk | Control before commitment |
|---|---|
| Tender price pressure destroys contribution | Exact landed-cost and service-cost floor; no below-floor bid without founder approval |
| Payment delay creates a cash gap | Contractual and observed payment days; receivable cap and downside liquidity model |
| Wrong or substituted specification | Written exact-SKU equivalence and purchaser approval before order |
| Bid/performance security consumes headroom | Separate exposure register linked to bank/distributor facilities |
| Warranty obligation exceeds partner support | Written principal, SLA, parts, pickup and escalation responsibilities |
| Large order becomes obsolete stock | Back-to-back/special-order structure, staged delivery and cancellation terms |
| Donor/public compliance failure | Solicitation-specific checklist and qualified review where needed |
| Device/user data exposure | Minimal setup scope, consent, access log and no retained credentials/data |
| False pipeline inflation | Procurement-state taxonomy and documentary evidence requirement |

## Founder decisions still required

No founder decision is created by this study. Later decisions are required for:

- whether K&S launches an institutional lane at all;
- eligible sectors/geographies and named owner;
- maximum receivable, special-order, tender-security and warranty exposure;
- any credit, loaner, on-site support, software or managed-service promise; and
- public institutional pricing or service claims.

## Major-batch synthesis

### What we learned

Banke contains a substantial and diverse education and healthcare account base. Public records also prove at least one formal local computer-lab procurement. The market is real enough to research, but public sources cannot support an annual device-volume or revenue claim.

### What changed in assumptions

“Institutional demand” is no longer a single undifferentiated line in the market model. It is now separated by sector, geography, category, procurement state and evidence quality. School/student/facility counts are discovery denominators, not unit multipliers.

### K&S opportunity

The strongest candidate is a compliance-and-ownership service around authorized devices: exact specifications, documentation, configuration, asset records, staged delivery and accountable warranty handling. The offer may be more defensible than a generic bulk discount if it can be delivered profitably.

### Risks

Tender security, price pressure, payment delay, special-order exposure, manufacturer authorization, specification mismatch and after-sales liability can make apparent volume economically harmful.

### Unresolved questions

Current institution-level device bases, refresh cycles, funded 12-month quantities, procurement calendars, incumbent performance, payment days and willingness to switch remain unknown.

### Recommended next research

Execute the twelve-account protocol, build the six-month evidence-backed procurement calendar, obtain two distributor institutional-support responses and run three small supervised order/simulation cases before any public institutional offer or inventory commitment.
