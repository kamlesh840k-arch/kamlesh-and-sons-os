# v0.9 — Expansion Blueprint

**Status:** Documentation-ready — proof, capital and market approvals pending
**Master:** [Expansion Master](../../decisions/DEC-EXPANSION-v0.9-master.md)
**Canonical system:** [Expansion System](../v0.9-expansion-system.md)
**Gate:** [Documentation and Activation Checklist](../v0.9-expansion-checklist.md)

## Objective

Create a disciplined model for multi-store growth that protects brand, customer experience, operating quality, and financial control.

## Required Outcomes

- Expansion thesis, readiness criteria, and investment gates defined.
- Market, location, and format evaluation methods documented.
- Replication standards, rollout governance, and post-opening review established.
- Partnership, franchise, and geographic-growth options assessed deliberately.

## Deliverables

1. Expansion strategy and readiness scorecard.
2. Market, location, and store-format evaluation framework.
3. New-store rollout and post-opening review process.
4. Partnership/franchise decision framework.
5. Expansion risk, capital, and governance model.

## Gate Before Closure

No market entry, lease, franchise, partner agreement, or expansion investment is approved by this blueprint; each requires evidence and separate authority.
## Governance

- The named master decision governs this milestone.
- Each implementation decision must name an owner, evidence, dependencies, and approval authority.
- No milestone is considered operationally complete merely because its blueprint exists.
