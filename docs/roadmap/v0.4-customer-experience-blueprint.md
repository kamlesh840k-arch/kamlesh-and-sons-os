# v0.4 — Customer Experience Blueprint

**Status:** Documentation-ready — validation and activation pending
**Master:** [Customer & Commercial Master](../../decisions/DEC-CUSTOMER-COMMERCIAL-v0.4-master.md)
**Canonical system:** [Customer Experience System](../v0.4-customer-experience-system.md)
**Gate:** [Documentation and Activation Checklist](../v0.4-customer-experience-checklist.md)

## Objective

Design one coherent customer journey and service system that expresses the approved brand across discovery, consultation, purchase, support, and recovery.

## Required Outcomes

- Customer segments, priority needs, and journey assumptions documented.
- End-to-end journey map and service standards defined.
- Consultation, handover, complaint, warranty, and service-recovery principles defined.
- Customer-feedback loop, ownership, and measures established.
- CRM and channel requirements documented for later technology work.

## Deliverables

1. Customer research brief and insight register.
2. Priority customer journeys and service blueprints.
3. Service standards and escalation model.
4. Feedback, complaint, and recovery framework.
5. Customer-experience measurement definition.

## Gate Before Closure

Leadership must approve the journey and service standards using real customer evidence. Any systems, staffing, or channel implementation requires separate decisions.
## Governance

- The named master decision governs this milestone.
- Each implementation decision must name an owner, evidence, dependencies, and approval authority.
- No milestone is considered operationally complete merely because its blueprint exists.
