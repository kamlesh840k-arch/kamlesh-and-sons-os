# v1.0 — Flagship Ready Blueprint

**Status:** Documentation-ready release candidate — real-world evidence pending
**Basis:** v0.1 through v0.9 roadmap masters and DEC-0090
**Canonical integration:** [Integrated Flagship Operating Model](../v1.0-integrated-flagship-operating-model.md)
**Gate:** [Flagship Readiness Gate](../v1.0-flagship-readiness-gate.md)

## Objective

Demonstrate that the K&S operating model can support a flagship store with integrated brand, customer, technology, operations, marketing, finance, and governance.

## Required Outcomes

- Cross-milestone dependencies and ownership reconciled.
- Flagship readiness criteria, evidence, and decision gates defined.
- Customer, operational, technology, financial, safety, and risk requirements integrated.
- Launch, review, and continuous-improvement governance established.

## Deliverables

1. Integrated flagship operating-model map.
2. Readiness scorecard and evidence register.
3. Cross-functional launch governance and escalation plan.
4. Risk, contingency, and post-launch review framework.
5. v1.0 release decision and handoff plan.

## Gate Before Closure

A flagship is ready only after real-world validation of all required functions, safety, financial, legal, staffing, technology, and launch criteria.
## Governance

- The named master decision governs this milestone.
- Each implementation decision must name an owner, evidence, dependencies, and approval authority.
- No milestone is considered operationally complete merely because its blueprint exists.
