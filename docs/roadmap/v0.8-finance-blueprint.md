# v0.8 — Finance Blueprint

**Status:** Planned blueprint — execution pending  
**Master:** [Finance Master](../../decisions/DEC-FINANCE-v0.8-master.md)

## Objective

Establish trustworthy financial information, disciplined controls, useful planning, and decision-ready economics.

## Required Outcomes

- Financial governance, accounting, cash, spending, and approval controls documented.
- Budgeting, forecasting, and management-reporting cycles defined.
- Revenue, margin, pricing, and capital-expenditure economics made visible.
- Tax, statutory, and financial-risk obligations assigned and monitored.

## Deliverables

1. Financial-control and authority matrix.
2. Chart/reporting design and management-reporting pack.
3. Budgeting, forecasting, cash, and treasury process.
4. Pricing, margin, and investment-evaluation framework.
5. Compliance and financial-risk register.

## Gate Before Closure

Accounting, tax, legal, banking, and audit requirements require qualified professional review; this blueprint does not substitute for that advice.
## Governance

- The named master decision governs this milestone.
- Each implementation decision must name an owner, evidence, dependencies, and approval authority.
- No milestone is considered operationally complete merely because its blueprint exists.
