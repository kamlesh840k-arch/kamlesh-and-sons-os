# v0.6 — Operations & People Blueprint

**Status:** Documentation-ready — pilot and workforce approval pending
**Masters:** [Operations](../../decisions/DEC-OPERATIONS-v0.6-master.md) and [People & Workforce](../../decisions/DEC-PEOPLE-v0.6-master.md)
**Canonical system:** [Operations & People System](../v0.6-operations-and-people-system.md)
**Gate:** [Documentation and Activation Checklist](../v0.6-operations-and-people-checklist.md)

## Objective

Create repeatable, measurable store and supporting operations with a capable workforce system.

## Required Outcomes

- Core operating processes, control points, and exception paths defined.
- Supply, inventory, fulfillment, service, quality, and store routines standardized.
- Workforce roles, planning, training, performance, and escalation systems aligned to demand.
- Operating measures and continuous-improvement discipline established.

## Deliverables

1. Process architecture and operating-SOP library.
2. Inventory, fulfillment, quality, and store-control standards.
3. Workforce role, training, scheduling, and performance framework.
4. Operational dashboard and improvement backlog.
5. Operational risk and incident-management procedures.

## Gate Before Closure

Standards must be piloted in real operations with owners, training evidence, and measured results before they are declared operationally effective.
## Governance

- The named master decision governs this milestone.
- Each implementation decision must name an owner, evidence, dependencies, and approval authority.
- No milestone is considered operationally complete merely because its blueprint exists.
