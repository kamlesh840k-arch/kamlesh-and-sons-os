# v3.0 — AI-Native Retail Platform Blueprint

**Status:** Long-term directional blueprint — execution pending  
**Basis:** v2.0 maturity, v0.5 technology system, and DEC-0090

## Objective

Evolve proven digital and operational capabilities into an AI-native retail platform that creates measurable value while preserving trust and control.

## Required Outcomes

- AI platform strategy, capability boundaries, data governance, and operating model defined.
- Human oversight, customer protection, reliability, and value measurement embedded.
- Cross-store intelligence, automation, and learning loops governed responsibly.

## Deliverables

1. AI-platform target architecture.
2. Data-governance and model-risk framework.
3. Prioritized, measurable AI capability portfolio.
4. Human-oversight and incident-response model.
5. Platform reliability, security, and lifecycle standard.

## Gate Before Closure

No long-term AI platform may be activated without demonstrated value, lawful data use, robust security, human accountability, and leadership approval.
## Governance

- The named master decision governs this milestone.
- Each implementation decision must name an owner, evidence, dependencies, and approval authority.
- No milestone is considered operationally complete merely because its blueprint exists.
