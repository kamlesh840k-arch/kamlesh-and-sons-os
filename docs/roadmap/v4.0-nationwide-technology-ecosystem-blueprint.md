# v4.0 — Nationwide Technology Ecosystem Blueprint

**Status:** Long-term directional blueprint — execution pending  
**Basis:** proven multi-store and AI-platform maturity

## Objective

Provide a controlled strategic framework for a nationwide technology ecosystem without committing K&S to unvalidated scale or business models.

## Required Outcomes

- Ecosystem thesis, partner roles, platform boundaries, and national-governance model defined.
- Trust, interoperability, data protection, resilience, and competition considerations embedded.
- Expansion stages, investment gates, and measurable public/customer value identified.

## Deliverables

1. Ecosystem strategy and value proposition.
2. Partner, platform, and interoperability governance.
3. National expansion and investment-gate model.
4. Data, trust, resilience, and regulatory risk framework.
5. Outcome and impact measurement plan.

## Gate Before Closure

This direction must be revalidated against market conditions, law, capital, capability, customer value, and leadership priorities before any commitment is made.
## Governance

- The named master decision governs this milestone.
- Each implementation decision must name an owner, evidence, dependencies, and approval authority.
- No milestone is considered operationally complete merely because its blueprint exists.
