# K&S OS — Roadmap Blueprint Approval

**Record ID:** DOC-ROADMAP-APPROVAL-001  
**Date:** 2026-08-09  
**Status:** Approved — documentation and sequencing  
**Approval scope:** Roadmap blueprints v0.4 through v4.0

## Decision

The K&S OS roadmap structure and its complete set of planning blueprints are approved as the governing documentation for future work.

This approval confirms:

- The sequence from v0.4 Customer Experience through v1.0 Flagship Ready.
- The long-term directions v2.0 Multi-Store Operations, v3.0 AI-Native Retail Platform, and v4.0 Nationwide Technology Ecosystem.
- The scope, intended deliverables, dependencies, and closure gates documented in the roadmap blueprints.
- The rule that activation and closure occur one milestone at a time with traceable evidence.

## What Is Approved Now

- Roadmap sequencing and the current boundaries between milestones.
- The blueprint documents as the official starting point for future work.
- The use of the roadmap blueprint index as the navigation and governance reference.

## What Remains Pending

This approval does **not** approve or assert:

- Real-site accessibility, safety, building, engineering, or construction validation.
- Vendor selection, contracts, budgets, procurement, or capital spending.
- Customer, employee, financial, technology, legal, tax, privacy, or regulatory implementation.
- Activation or closure of v0.4 through v4.0.
- Any commitment to a specific number of stores, markets, technologies, partners, or investments.

## Immediate Pending Item — v0.3

Before v0.3 Store Design may close:

1. A design-governance owner must be identified.
2. The concept must be validated against the actual site and applicable requirements.
3. Detailed drawings, budgets, vendors, procurement, and construction must be separately reviewed and approved.

## Future Activation Rule

For each future milestone, before activation:

1. Name the accountable owner.
2. Confirm relevant evidence and dependencies.
3. Create implementation decisions where needed.
4. Obtain the appropriate business and specialist approvals.
5. Record the activation and eventual release-gate outcome.

## Result

K&S now has an approved, complete documentation roadmap. Real-world implementation remains deliberately staged, evidence-based, and controlled.
