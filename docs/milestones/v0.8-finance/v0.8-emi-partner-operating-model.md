## Principle

At launch, K&S should **not lend money itself**. EMI is offered through approved, appropriately authorised finance/bank/card partners under an executed commercial agreement. The partner decides eligibility and credit terms; K&S sells the product and explains the approved offer accurately.

This avoids informal store credit, unclear collection responsibility and unapproved financial risk. It does not replace banking, legal, tax, consumer-protection or regulatory review.

## Offer lanes

Keep customer communication and reconciliation separate for:

1. participating-merchant credit-card EMI;
2. customer-paid post-purchase card conversion;
3. pre-approved app/QR BNPL;
4. product-specific financing programmes.

An ordinary card or QR payment is not a K&S EMI programme. A customer-controlled bank conversion should not be advertised as guaranteed. A product-specific offer must not be extended to other SKUs, customers, dates or channels.

## Customer Journey

| Step | K&S role | Partner role | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Choose product | Confirm stock, cash price and eligibility of the SKU | Publish approved financing products/rules | Product and offer version |
| 2. Explain offer | Show cash price, down payment, instalment count, instalment amount, total payable, fees, interest/zero-interest basis, late-payment consequences, cancellation/refund route and material exclusions | Provide current approved terms | Customer order/offer acknowledgement |
| 3. Apply | Direct customer to the partner's secure process; provide process help only | Collect application and make eligibility decision | Partner reference, not K&S copies of sensitive application data |
| 4. Approve/decline | Never influence or promise the decision; offer cash/other approved method if declined | Make and communicate decision | Approval/reference or decline state |
| 5. Fulfil | Release product only when the agreed partner funding/authorisation condition is satisfied | Settle K&S under the agreement | Order, serial, payment/settlement match |
| 6. Support | Triage explanation, order or fulfilment issues; keep a complaint case | Handle credit account, repayment, eligibility and account disputes | Case history and escalation record |

## Customer Communication Rules

- Quote only the partner's current, written and approved offer. Do not call an offer “zero cost,” “no interest,” “guaranteed approval,” or “instant” unless the approved terms support every part of that statement.
- “No fee” or “0%” for the customer does not mean no merchant cost. Staff must use the approved customer language; Finance must separately record ordinary MDR, merchant EMI fee/subsidy, co-funding and tax treatment.
- Present the cash price beside the **total amount payable** under EMI. Do not hide fees, conditions, tenure, late-payment consequences, required card/account conditions or partner restrictions.
- Give the customer time to read terms and complete their own partner application. K&S staff must not make credit decisions, falsify eligibility, complete customer declarations or pressure a customer to borrow.
- Collect the minimum data needed for the K&S sale. Sensitive finance/KYC data should go directly to the partner through its approved channel; K&S does not retain copies unless the agreement and applicable rules specifically require it.

## Store Controls

1. Maintain a founder-approved list of partners, offers, eligible products, staff permissions and current marketing materials.
2. Keep a daily EMI register: order ID, partner reference, SKU/serial, cash price, approved offer version, status, settlement due/received, refund/cancellation state and case ID. Do not include unnecessary sensitive data.
3. Reconcile partner authorisations, product release, settlement and refunds daily; investigate every mismatch before close.
4. Keep partner-related funds separate from personal/staff cash. Refunds, cancellations, returns and failed fulfilment follow the written partner agreement and customer terms—never an improvised cash workaround.
5. Train staff on transparent explanation, privacy, prohibited conduct, handover timing, exception escalation and complaint handling. Test competence before access.

## Merchant economics and offer register

For every offer version, Finance must retain the signed source and record:

- eligible partner, card/account, products, ticket, down payment, tenure and dates;
- customer interest, processing/card/other fees, instalment and total payable;
- ordinary acquiring fee and incremental merchant EMI/BNPL fee or subsidy;
- bank, brand and distributor co-funding and claim timing;
- gross/net settlement, due date, reserve/holdback and credit-note evidence;
- cancellation, return, refund, prepayment, fraud and chargeback treatment;
- category/SKU contribution after every financing cost.

No offer may be activated if its full merchant cost cannot be reproduced or if it breaches the founder-approved contribution floor.

## Partner Selection and Launch Gate

Before launch, leadership must approve partner due diligence, current NRB licence/permission evidence, commercial terms, settlement timing/fees, refund/return handling, data-sharing and privacy roles, customer complaint route, staff training, offer disclosures, accounting/tax treatment, reconciliation workflow and pilot results. Qualified finance, legal, tax and banking advice is required.

No public EMI advertisement or customer financing offer is authorised by this document alone.
