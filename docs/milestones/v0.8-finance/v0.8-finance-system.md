# K&S OS — v0.8 Finance System

## Outcome

Protect cash, produce reliable records, expose unit/store economics, and make budgets, pricing, EMI and investment decisions through traceable authority.

## Core Controls

| Cycle | Minimum control | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue/receivables | Complete order-to-receipt reconciliation and refund authority | Sales, payment and settlement match |
| Purchase/payables | Approved need/vendor/order, receipt and invoice match | Three-way evidence or approved exception |
| Cash/bank | Custody, daily reconciliation, deposit and independent review | Reconciliation and variance sign-off |
| Inventory/cost | Movement completeness, count, valuation and shrink review | Stock-to-ledger reconciliation |
| Payroll/people | Approved roster/change, calculation and payment review | Payroll approval trail |
| Close/reporting | Cut-off, reconciliations, review and locked period | Close checklist and management pack |
| Tax/statutory | Obligation calendar and qualified review | Filing/payment evidence |

## Authority and Segregation

An authority matrix must set monetary and risk thresholds for budgets, purchases, payments, refunds, discounts, credit/EMI, write-offs, contracts and capital expenditure. Request, approval, custody, recording and reconciliation should be separated where practical; unavoidable founder-stage conflicts require documented compensating review.

## Planning and Economics

Maintain assumptions separately from actuals. The budget/forecast covers revenue, gross margin, operating cost, cash, inventory, working capital and capital expenditure with base/downside/upside cases. Product/store economics include landed cost, discount, financing cost, warranty/service exposure, returns, acquisition cost, occupancy, staffing and tax effects. EMI offerings require approved partners, transparent total obligations, compliance review, reconciliation and complaint handling.

## Management Pack

At an approved cadence report profit and loss, cash/runway, balance-sheet risks, sales/margin, inventory aging/turn, receivables/payables, budget/forecast variance, capital commitments and control exceptions. Each material variance names cause, action, owner and date.

## Activation and Closure Evidence

Activation requires a Finance Owner, qualified accounting/tax/legal input, chart of accounts, authority matrix, bank/payment controls, calendar, opening balances and approved budget. Operational closure requires completed periods, reconciliations, statutory evidence, tested controls, reliable management packs, resolved exceptions and leadership acceptance.
