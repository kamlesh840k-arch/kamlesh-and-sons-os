## Customer Promise

> **K&S Care — Clear advice. Genuine products. Support after purchase.**

K&S Care is the service promise for every customer. It is a standard of clear, recorded and respectful help; it is not a promise of a repair outcome, warranty approval, lender approval, data-recovery result or turnaround time that K&S cannot control.

## What the Customer Receives

| Moment | K&S Care standard | Customer evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consultation | Need, budget and use case understood before recommendation | Consultation/order record where used |
| Purchase | Cash price, finance terms, warranty source, product status and material exclusions explained before payment | Order recap and consent |
| Handover | Identity/order/serial check, product condition check, basic setup and care explanation | Handover checklist |
| First-use help | Reasonable setup questions routed through the approved support channel | Case or interaction record |
| Service/warranty | Case ID, condition/serial record, custody receipt, updates and escalation path | Case history and closure confirmation |

## Data-Transfer Service

### Scope at Launch

Offer data transfer as an **assisted setup service**, not as a data-recovery service. The standard service may help a customer transfer contacts, photos, videos, calendar data and supported apps/settings using the device maker's approved, customer-visible method. Its precise free/paid scope, eligible device types and booking availability require founder approval before public launch.

Do not promise that every app, chat history, password, banking application, eSIM, game save, licence, damaged-device file or cloud item will transfer. Some content is controlled by the device maker, application provider, account settings, storage capacity, internet connection or the customer's own backup.

### Required Procedure

1. **Explain and obtain consent.** Confirm the source and destination device, intended categories of data, known limitations, expected time and whether the customer wants to proceed. Record the consent and case/order ID.
2. **Verify ownership and readiness.** Check that the customer has both devices, sufficient power, usable connectivity and enough storage. Refuse/route escalation if theft, fraud, account-control or unsafe-device concerns arise.
3. **Customer controls credentials.** The customer enters all passwords, PINs, security codes and one-time passwords directly. Staff must not write down, photograph, ask the customer to disclose, retain or reuse credentials.
4. **Use a customer-visible method.** Prefer direct device-to-device or approved cloud transfer while the customer can see the devices. Avoid copying data to K&S equipment. Any exception needs approved tooling, explicit written consent, a trained authorised employee and a documented deletion procedure.
5. **Transfer only the approved scope.** Do not browse unrelated personal content. Pause for customer decisions, account prompts, payments, banking-app activation or any unexpected request for sensitive permissions.
6. **Verify with the customer.** Ask the customer to confirm selected categories, basic device operation and account access. Record completion, exception or decline; never certify that every item transferred unless verified.
7. **Close safely.** Return both devices, remove any temporary K&S-held material under the approved deletion process, give after-care guidance and offer a case ID for follow-up.

### Non-Negotiable Privacy Rules

- Never retain a copy of customer data, passwords, PINs, OTPs, recovery codes or account credentials.
- Never handle banking, wallet, government-ID, health, private-business or other sensitive applications beyond directing the customer to complete their own sign-in/activation.
- Never factory-reset, erase, trade in, recycle or dispose of a source device unless the customer separately authorises it after confirming the transfer; these are separate services with separate evidence.
- Stop and escalate an incident, loss, suspected compromise or customer complaint immediately; do not attempt to conceal or self-remedy it.

## Staff and Operating Controls

Only trained and certified staff may provide the service. The store needs an approved consent/checklist, private but customer-visible setup space, queue/booking method, case register, incident route and quality review. Measure completion, time, customer effort, repeat contact, complaint and privacy/security exceptions. Targets are set only after a controlled pilot and founder approval.

## Launch Gate

K&S Care and data transfer can be marketed only after a named owner, privacy/compliance review, approved customer wording, staff competency evidence, system/forms, incident process and real-customer pilot results are accepted by leadership.
