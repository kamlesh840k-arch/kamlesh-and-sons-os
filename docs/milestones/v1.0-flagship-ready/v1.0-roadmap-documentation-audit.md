## Repository Reconciliation

- `VERSION` is `1.0.0-rc.2`, making the incomplete real-world release state explicit.
- `ROADMAP.md`, `README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, blueprints and indexes use consistent milestone status language.
- All v0.1–v1.0 milestone records are organized in `docs/milestones/` by version, with repository-level and folder-level navigation records.
- Existing historical decisions are preserved; roadmap masters point to canonical systems rather than duplicating decision history.
- Every newly documented milestone has an auditable checklist with documentation items separated from activation/real-world items.
- Final `1.0.0` is prohibited until the flagship readiness gate contains real evidence and accepted attestations.

## Founder Approval Needed

The Founder should approve or revise owners, delegated authority, risk appetite, service promise, AI/data boundaries, operating-control thresholds, marketing strategy, finance authority matrix, expansion criteria and the eventual flagship go/no-go decision. Specialist approvals remain necessary where competence or regulation requires them.

Current flagship demand inputs must be read through [RESEARCH-SEGMENT-001](../../research/western-nepal-device-demand-segments-and-catchment.md): public access, poverty and demographic context does not approve a price-band mix, brand allocation, premium-demand claim or regional-catchment uplift. Those remain v1.0 field-evidence dependencies.

[DEC-0103](../../../decisions/DEC-0103-staged-field-research-activation-boundary.md) authorised zero-cost preparation and synthetic rehearsal; [RESEARCH-FIELD-002](../../research/field-evidence-stage-a-synthetic-rehearsal.md) records the passed Stage A controls. This does not close a v1.0 evidence dependency. Live pilot and production remain gated by named accountability, qualified privacy/legal review, reviewed instruments, tested technology/custody, safety, budget and pilot evidence.
