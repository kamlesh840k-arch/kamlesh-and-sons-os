## Integration Rule

K&S OS is documentation-ready when every required operating domain has a canonical design, owner role, controls, evidence requirements and approval path. The flagship is ready only when the actual site, people, systems, finances and launch controls produce accepted evidence. These states must never be conflated.

## Flagship Release Authorities

The Founder is final business release authority. Named functional owners attest only within their competence. Qualified professionals approve regulated or specialist matters such as legal, tax/accounting, safety, accessibility, building/engineering, privacy/security and employment requirements. No silence or unchecked box counts as approval.

## Launch Governance

- Maintain one readiness register containing criterion, evidence link, owner, reviewer, status, exception and expiry/recheck date.
- Classify issues as release-blocking, conditionally accepted with owner/date, or post-launch improvement.
- Run cross-functional readiness reviews, a full operating rehearsal, contingency exercise and final go/no-go meeting.
- Freeze material launch changes unless risk-assessed and approved; preserve rollback/manual fallback for critical systems.
- Establish launch-day command, safety/incident escalation, customer recovery authority and daily stabilization review.
- Conduct 7/30/60/90-day reviews and convert accepted learning into controlled document/process changes.

## Non-Negotiable No-Go Conditions

Do not release if there is unresolved material danger; missing required permit/approval; unaccepted structural, fire, electrical or accessibility risk; inadequate cash/funding authority; uncontrolled payment/inventory/customer-data handling; unavailable critical roles; failed critical-system fallback; materially misleading public promise; or any other unaccepted high risk.

## Release Decision

`1.0.0-rc.2` records a complete documentation architecture and approval-ready integration gate. A final `1.0.0` Flagship Ready release requires the completed evidence register, resolved blockers, functional attestations and a signed founder go/no-go decision. Opening the flagship and declaring post-launch operating effectiveness are later, evidence-based events.
