## Outcome

Run safe, reliable and measurable store operations through clear standard work, controlled inventory and cash, capable people, and disciplined exception handling.

## Process and Control Architecture

| Domain | Standard work | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Daily store | Opening, readiness, shift handover, closing, key/alarm control | Signed checklist and exception log |
| Sales | Needs discovery, quote/order, payment, handover, cancellation/return | Transaction and handover trail |
| Inventory | Receiving, identification, storage, movement, cycle count, reconciliation | Stock movement and variance approval |
| Cash/payment | Till assignment, receipt, reconciliation, deposit, refund and exception | Independent reconciliation |
| Service/warranty | Intake, condition/serial capture, custody, diagnosis routing, updates, closure | Chain of custody and case history |
| Procurement | Need, approved supplier, order, receipt, invoice match, payment | Segregated approval trail |
| Safety/security | Hazard, incident, emergency, access, loss and escalation | Incident/corrective-action record |
| Management | Daily review, KPI review, root cause, corrective action | Action owner and due date |

## SOP Standard

Every SOP names its owner, purpose, trigger, inputs, roles, ordered steps, controls, exception path, evidence retained, measure, training requirement, review frequency and version. Staff must be able to stop and escalate unsafe, unlawful, fraudulent or privacy-compromising work.

## People System

- Maintain role charters with accountability, authority, competence and segregation-of-duty requirements.
- Recruit consistently against job-related criteria and applicable employment requirements.
- Complete documented onboarding, policy acknowledgement, access provisioning and supervised practice.
- Certify competence for cash, inventory, customer data, setup, warranty custody and safety-sensitive tasks.
- Schedule against customer/operating demand with fair rest, attendance and escalation rules.
- Use evidence-based coaching and performance review with an appeal/escalation route.
- Remove access, recover assets and preserve records promptly during role change or exit.

## Measures and Cadence

Daily: readiness, sales/service exceptions, cash variance, stock variance and incidents. Weekly: service levels, aging cases, replenishment, staffing/skills gaps and corrective actions. Monthly: customer outcomes, margin inputs, loss, productivity, training, safety and recurring root causes. Targets require baseline and approval.

## Activation and Closure Evidence

Activation requires named Operations and People Owners, approved role charters, prioritized SOPs, training materials, systems/forms, compliance review and pilot scope. Operational closure requires trained/observed competence, pilot records, inventory and cash reconciliations, safety evidence, incident testing, measured service levels, corrected gaps and leadership acceptance.
