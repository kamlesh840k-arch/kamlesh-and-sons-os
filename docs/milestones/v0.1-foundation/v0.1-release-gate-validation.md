# K&S OS v0.1.0 — Release Gate Validation

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Validation complete — leadership closure required

## 1. DEC-0100 Validation

DEC-0100 is the integration/readiness checkpoint. Its criteria require defined ownership, representation of major capability areas, documented standards and controls, management reporting, preserved organizational knowledge, material risk/continuity coverage, operating-model integration and visible outstanding gaps.

**Finding:** DEC-0100 is consistent with the consolidated foundation architecture, provided that its references to major business systems are interpreted as governance/representation requirements rather than fully implemented operating systems.

## 2. DEC-0101 Validation

DEC-0101 is the formal v0.1.0 completion/release gate. It requires the repository and its governance system to support purpose/structure, founder and leadership principles, repository/documentation governance, repeatable decision records, reusable templates, innovation/knowledge capture, roadmap progression, version/change discipline, a coherent foundation decision set and an identified v0.2.0 next milestone.

It explicitly states that v0.1.0 does not require all operational, technology, customer, financial, brand or expansion capabilities to be fully implemented.

**Finding:** DEC-0101 is consistent with the new consolidation architecture and correctly preserves later roadmap work as out of scope for v0.1 completion.

## 4. Scope Boundary

The later domain masters are planned/deferred. Their existence does not mean those roadmap milestones are complete or that their implementation is required for v0.1.

## 5. Release Decision

The technical/documentation validation supports **v0.1.0 readiness**. The repository is not to be marked as formally closed solely by automation: DEC-0101 explicitly reserves the final completion declaration for leadership acceptance.

Therefore:

**Recommended status:** READY FOR LEADERSHIP CLOSURE

**Next milestone after approval:** v0.2.0 — Brand Identity

## 6. Remaining Human Action

Leadership must explicitly accept DEC-0101 as the release gate. Once accepted, the repository status can be changed from “Release Gate Pending” to “v0.1.0 Complete / v0.2.0 Active.”
