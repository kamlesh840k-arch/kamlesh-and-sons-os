# K&S OS v0.1.0 — Canonical Foundation Decision Set

**Document ID:** DOC-DEC-FOUNDATION-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Foundation Audit — Part 2

## Purpose

This document defines the decision architecture that should remain in the v0.1.0 foundation after the repository's DEC series is cleaned up.

## Roadmap Basis

`ROADMAP.md` defines v0.1 as **Foundation**, focused on repository, governance, founding principles and the documentation system. v0.2 onward is reserved for brand identity, store design, customer experience, AI/technology, operations, marketing, finance and expansion.

## Canonical Foundation Domains

### 1. Founding & Strategic Principles
Keep the original founding decisions that establish mission, vision, founder principles, company identity, strategic intent and documentation-first philosophy.

### 2. Governance & Decision Rights
Keep decisions establishing governance structure, authority, accountability, decision rights, delegation, escalation and material decision records.

### 3. Documentation & Institutional Knowledge
Keep decisions establishing documentation standards, decision-log rules, knowledge retention, templates, institutional memory and innovation documentation.

### 4. Repository & Technology Governance
Keep only foundation-level decisions establishing the repository as source of truth, architecture principles, security/privacy principles, technology governance and change/version discipline. Detailed AI, digital-product, API and technology implementation belongs in v0.5.

### 5. Risk, Legal & Internal Control Foundation
Keep foundation-level decisions for legal/compliance governance, enterprise risk principles, internal controls, business continuity principles and privacy/security governance. Detailed operating controls may mature in later releases.

### 6. Management System Foundation
Keep decisions establishing strategic planning principles, project/portfolio governance, management reporting principles, continuous improvement and operating-model integration. Detailed department systems belong to their roadmap milestones.

### 7. Release Gates
Keep DEC-0100 — v0.1 Foundation Integration & Completion Readiness and DEC-0101 — v0.1 Foundation Completion & Release Gate. These are release controls, not business-domain systems.

## Explicitly Deferred From v0.1

Brand identity implementation; store design; customer-experience implementation; CRM implementation; pricing/revenue operating systems; AI concierge; internal AI implementation; API/platform ecosystem; detailed supply chain; procurement operations; inventory operations; store operating SOPs; workforce scheduling; marketing campaigns; detailed financial systems; franchise/partnership expansion; and international expansion.

These map to v0.2–v0.9 in the roadmap.

## Audit Rule

Existing decisions are not deleted merely because they are deferred. They should be consolidated or relocated to the appropriate roadmap domain while preserving decision history and traceability.

## Part 2 Result

The v0.1 foundation should be judged by whether it establishes the governance and knowledge infrastructure required to build the later operating systems, not by the number of DEC files present.
