# v0.5 — AI & Technology Blueprint

**Status:** Documentation-ready — architecture approval and implementation pending
**Master:** [AI & Technology Master](../../../decisions/DEC-AI-TECHNOLOGY-v0.5-master.md)
**Canonical system:** [AI & Technology System](v0.5-ai-and-technology-system.md)
**Gate:** [Documentation and Activation Checklist](v0.5-ai-and-technology-checklist.md)

## Objective

Build a secure, maintainable technology foundation that improves customer and internal outcomes without premature platform commitments.

## Required Outcomes

- Target architecture, data ownership, integration, and security principles ratified.
- Prioritized AI concierge and internal-AI use cases evaluated for value and risk.
- Digital-product and API requirements defined.
- Technology operating model, vendor controls, reliability measures, and change controls established.

## Deliverables

1. Technology and data architecture baseline.
2. AI use-case portfolio, risk assessment, and governance policy.
3. Security and privacy implementation plan.
4. Integration/API and digital-product roadmap.
5. Vendor, reliability, and lifecycle-management standard.

## Gate Before Closure

Implementation requires security, privacy, financial, operational, and leadership approvals; no AI system may use protected data without documented controls.
## Governance

- The named master decision governs this milestone.
- Each implementation decision must name an owner, evidence, dependencies, and approval authority.
- No milestone is considered operationally complete merely because its blueprint exists.
