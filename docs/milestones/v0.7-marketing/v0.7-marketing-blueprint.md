# v0.7 — Marketing Blueprint

**Status:** Documentation-ready — strategy approval and execution pending
**Master:** [Marketing Master](../../../decisions/DEC-MARKETING-v0.7-master.md)
**Canonical system:** [Marketing System](v0.7-marketing-system.md)
**Gate:** [Documentation and Activation Checklist](v0.7-marketing-checklist.md)

## Objective

Activate the approved brand and customer proposition through measurable, trusted communications and growth systems.

## Required Outcomes

- Marketing strategy, target audiences, and channel roles defined.
- Campaign-planning, brand-governance, and content workflows established.
- Acquisition, retention, local/store, partnership, and reputation approaches integrated.
- Measurement, attribution, budget controls, and learning cycles defined.

## Deliverables

1. Marketing strategy and audience plan.
2. Communications and content standards.
3. Campaign-planning and approval process.
4. Channel, acquisition, and retention playbooks.
5. Measurement, attribution, and marketing-budget framework.

## Gate Before Closure

Every public claim, promotion, customer-data use, and spend commitment requires the relevant legal, brand, privacy, and financial approvals.
## Governance

- The named master decision governs this milestone.
- Each implementation decision must name an owner, evidence, dependencies, and approval authority.
- No milestone is considered operationally complete merely because its blueprint exists.
