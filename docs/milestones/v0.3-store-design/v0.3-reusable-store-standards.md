# K&S OS — v0.3 Reusable Store Standards

**Milestone:** v0.3.0 — Store Design  
**Status:** Draft replication standard

## Purpose

Capture the elements of the flagship design that future K&S locations must preserve, while allowing responsible adaptation to site and market conditions.

## Mandatory Elements

Future stores must preserve:

- The approved K&S brand identity and visual hierarchy
- Clear arrival, orientation, browsing, consultation, service, and checkout logic
- Product presentation focused on discovery, comparison, and trust
- Accessible, safe, and maintainable customer circulation
- Consistent signage and wayfinding principles
- Controlled visual-merchandising and display governance
- The distinction between customer-facing and operational/support space

## Adaptable Elements

The following may vary with documented rationale:

- Footprint and zone scale
- Category emphasis and product capacity
- Fixture configuration
- Location-specific circulation and entrance response
- Site-appropriate material and lighting choices
- Technology touchpoints and local service capacity

## Change Control

- Every new or materially changed store applies the mandatory elements.
- Exceptions require documented rationale, brand review, and appropriate safety/technical validation.
- Reusable standards evolve through versioned updates; past store decisions remain traceable.
- Store design does not independently redefine the approved brand system.

## Handoff

This document guides later v0.6 operations and v0.9 expansion work, but it does not replace those future masters.
