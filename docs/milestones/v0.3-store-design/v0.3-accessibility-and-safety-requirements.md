# K&S OS — v0.3 Accessibility & Safety Requirements

**Milestone:** v0.3.0 — Store Design  
**Status:** Draft requirement set — professional validation required

## Purpose

Make accessibility, safety, and practical usability first-class store-design requirements.

## Core Requirements

- Customer routes must be clear, legible, and free of avoidable obstacles.
- Entrances, circulation, consultation, service, checkout, and exits must be assessed for accessibility.
- Fixtures, displays, cables, and temporary materials must not create trip, collision, or egress hazards.
- Product-security measures must preserve safe access and emergency movement.
- Safety notices, emergency equipment, and exits must remain visible and unobstructed.
- Customer seating and consultation environments should accommodate a range of mobility and comfort needs.
- Staff work areas must support safe handling, replenishment, and service activity.

## Design Validation

Before construction or fit-out approval, qualified local professionals must validate:

- Applicable building, fire, electrical, accessibility, and occupational-safety requirements
- Egress and occupancy assumptions
- Accessible clearances, reach ranges, and service-counter provisions
- Emergency lighting, alarms, signage, and equipment
- Structural, electrical, mechanical, and network implications

## Governance

No concept document overrides applicable law, site-specific risk assessment, supplier guidance, or professional technical advice.
