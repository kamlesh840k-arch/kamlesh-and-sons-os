# K&S OS — v0.3 Visual Merchandising Standards

**Milestone:** v0.3.0 — Store Design  
**Status:** Draft standard — concept-level

## Purpose

Set consistent product-presentation rules that translate the approved K&S brand into clear, premium, and useful in-store discovery.

## Principles

- Present products for understanding and comparison, not maximum density.
- Give hero products visible priority without obscuring relevant alternatives.
- Maintain clear category hierarchy and product information.
- Use whitespace, alignment, and repeatable display logic to create calm.
- Keep promotional material controlled, timely, and subordinate to the core brand.
- Balance hands-on access with product security and care.

## Display Hierarchy

1. Brand and category orientation
2. Hero or featured products
3. Comparison and supporting products
4. Product information and service prompts
5. Approved promotional communication

## Standards

- Each category zone has a visible purpose and consistent information hierarchy.
- Demo products are functional, clean, secured appropriately, and kept current.
- Product information is readable, accurate, and consistent with approved typography and visual standards.
- Display density supports browsing, staff replenishment, cleaning, and safe circulation.
- Seasonal or promotional changes must preserve category clarity.
- Damaged, incomplete, or outdated displays are removed or corrected promptly.

## Governance

Merchandising changes must preserve the v0.2 brand system and be traceable through the relevant store-change process. Product-range, pricing, campaign, and operational decisions remain subject to their roadmap milestones.
