# K&S OS — v0.3 Fixture & Display Principles

**Milestone:** v0.3.0 — Store Design  
**Status:** Draft standard — concept-level

## Purpose

Define the principles for fixtures and displays that support premium product discovery, maintainability, safety, and future reuse.

## Principles

- Fixtures must support the brand through clarity and quality, not unnecessary ornament.
- Product access, comparison, replenishment, cleaning, and maintenance are required design considerations.
- Materials and finishes should be durable, repairable where practical, and suitable for a high-touch retail environment.
- Fixture systems should distinguish reusable core elements from site-specific adaptations.
- Security must be integrated discreetly and proportionately.

## Fixture Categories

- Category display fixtures
- Feature/hero presentation fixtures
- Demonstration surfaces
- Consultation furniture
- Service and support counters
- Checkout equipment surrounds
- Signage supports
- Back-of-house storage and replenishment support

## Requirements

- Display heights, reach ranges, and clearance must be validated against accessibility requirements during detailed design.
- Fixtures must not create blind corners, unsafe edges, blocked routes, or obstructed exits.
- Power, charging, and device-security needs must be identified before final engineering.
- Surfaces must be compatible with routine cleaning and maintenance.
- Final material, supplier, cost, and construction choices require separate approval.
