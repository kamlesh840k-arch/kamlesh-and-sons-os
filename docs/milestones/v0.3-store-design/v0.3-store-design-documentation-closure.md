## Open Real-World Gates

1. **Governance:** Founder appoints an accountable design-governance owner and records their authority.
2. **Site validation:** The actual flagship site is measured and checked for circulation, accessibility, safety, building constraints, utilities, service operations, and applicable requirements by qualified parties.
3. **Detailed design and commercial approval:** Drawings, specifications, bills of quantity, budget, and schedule are reviewed and approved separately.
4. **Delivery approval:** Vendors, procurement, engineering, permits, contracts, construction, commissioning, and handover each follow applicable authority and evidence requirements.

## Evidence Required to Close Operationally

- Named design-governance owner and acceptance date.
- Approved site-validation report and resolved exception log.
- Approved drawing/specification issue and professional sign-offs where required.
- Approved budget, procurement route, vendors/contracts, and delivery plan.
- Construction/installation completion, safety evidence, defects closeout, and handover record.

## Handoff

v0.4 documentation may proceed because its work defines the customer and service system at the operating-model level. Physical implementation of v0.3 remains gated and must be rechecked against v0.4 customer journeys before final design approval.
