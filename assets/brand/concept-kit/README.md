# K&S Visual Concept Kit

## Status

**Sample concept set — not production-approved.** These files turn the approved brand direction into tangible review material. They are not an authority to order uniforms, print IDs, fabricate signs, or construct the store.

## Contents

- `logo/`: editable SVG directions. The original wordmark/monogram is retained for comparison; four distinctly different routes are in `logo/variations/`. No direction is yet recommended or final.
- `logo/minimal/`: typography-led variations with no decorative icon system; created after the first visual directions were rejected as too elaborate.
- `logo/symbol-first/`: a reset after the typography-only directions were rejected; these use non-letter symbols that can become a recognisable storefront/device badge.
- `id-card/`: editable staff identity-card sample with intentionally fictitious data.
- `uniform/`: visual reference for a graphite uniform with precise cobalt detailing and black lanyard.
- `store/`: a conceptual zoning diagram, general flagship references, and three Nepalgunj-specific five-shutter concept views covering the overall interior, storefront and axonometric layout.
- `deck/`: the earlier `KNS_Brand_and_Store_Direction.pptx` presentation, also opened in Canva for editable presentation work.

## Founder decisions required before production

1. Select, refine, and legally clear the final wordmark/monogram; set trademark ownership and final typography.
2. Approve uniform cut, fabric, size range, supplier sample, cost, care guidance, and staff comfort testing.
3. Approve ID-card security rules, employee-data fields, photo policy, issuer, replacement process, and access-control integration.
4. Validate all store drawings, dimensions, site circulation, accessibility, safety, finishes, lighting, local permits, vendor quotes, and construction budget against the actual flagship site.
