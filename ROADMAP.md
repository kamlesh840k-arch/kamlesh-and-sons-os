# KAMLESH & SONS OS — Roadmap

> The strategic development roadmap for the KAMLESH & SONS operating system.

## Purpose

This roadmap defines the planned evolution of K&S OS from its initial documentation foundation into a complete operating system for KAMLESH & SONS.

The roadmap is directional. Major changes to company strategy or sequencing should be captured through a decision record and reflected in the changelog.

## Release Roadmap

| Version | Milestone | Primary Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **v0.1** | Foundation | Repository, governance, founding principles, documentation system |
| **v0.2** | Brand Identity | Brand strategy, identity, typography, visual standards |
| **v0.3** | Store Design | Flagship concept, layout, visual merchandising, store standards |
| **v0.4** | Customer Experience | Customer journey, service standards, premium services |
| **v0.5** | AI & Technology | AI Concierge, internal AI, technology architecture |
| **v0.6** | Operations | SOPs, inventory, service, training, operational systems |
| **v0.7** | Marketing | Marketing strategy, campaigns, communications, growth systems |
| **v0.8** | Finance | Financial systems, KPIs, financing, controls, planning |
| **v0.9** | Expansion | Multi-store model, expansion framework, national growth |
| **v1.0** | Flagship Ready | Integrated operating model ready for flagship execution |

## Long-Term Direction

After v1.0, the operating system is expected to evolve with the company:

- **v2.0** — Multi-Store Operations
- **v3.0** — AI-Native Retail Platform
- **v4.0** — Nationwide Technology Ecosystem

These future milestones are strategic directions rather than fixed commitments.

## Roadmap Principles

- Build systematically rather than all at once.
- Preserve existing work before extending it.
- Prefer documented decisions over undocumented assumptions.
- Keep the repository as the single source of truth.
- Use semantic versioning for meaningful releases.
- Cross-reference related documents, decisions, and innovations.
- Treat the roadmap as a living document.

## Change Management

A significant roadmap change should be evaluated for its impact on:

- Company strategy
- Customer experience
- Brand
- Technology
- Operations
- Finance
- Expansion
- Existing decisions
- Innovation initiatives

Where a change represents a major strategic decision, create a corresponding `DEC-*` record and update the relevant documentation.
