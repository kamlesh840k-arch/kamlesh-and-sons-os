# DEC-0081 — Strategic Planning & Annual Operating Plan System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0081  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will translate long-term strategy into a practical annual operating plan with priorities, targets, budgets, owners and measurable outcomes.

> **Turn strategy into a small number of funded, owned and measurable priorities.**

## Core Controls

- Strategic objectives
- Annual priorities
- Operating targets
- Budget alignment
- Initiative portfolio
- Ownership
- Quarterly reviews
- Assumption tracking
- Scenario planning
- Year-end evaluation

## Planning

Annual plans should connect strategy, customer needs, operations, people, technology and finance.

## Priorities

The plan should explicitly identify what will not be prioritized when capacity is constrained.

## Targets

Targets should be measurable and grounded in available evidence, with assumptions documented where uncertainty is high.

## Budget Alignment

Major priorities should have corresponding resource and funding implications under DEC-0057.

## Review

Quarterly reviews should assess progress, assumptions, risks and whether priorities should change.

## Success Standard

Leadership can explain the few priorities that matter most, who owns them, what resources they require and how progress will be judged.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Establish strategic objectives.
2. Select annual priorities.
3. Translate priorities into initiatives and KPIs.
4. Align budget and capacity.
5. Review progress quarterly.
6. Evaluate outcomes and improve the next planning cycle.
