# DEC-0084 — Partner, Franchise & Business Development Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0084  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will evaluate and manage strategic partnerships, franchise opportunities and business-development relationships through structured commercial, operational, legal and risk review.

> **Expand through partners only when the model remains controllable, profitable and trustworthy.**

## Core Controls

- Opportunity qualification
- Partner due diligence
- Business-case evaluation
- Commercial terms
- Operating standards
- Brand protection
- Training requirements
- Performance monitoring
- Exit conditions
- Relationship governance

## Qualification

Potential partners should be assessed for strategic fit, economics, capability, reputation, compliance and operational compatibility.

## Agreements

Material partnerships should use appropriate contracts reviewed under DEC-0058.

## Brand

Partners representing K&S should follow approved brand and customer standards under DEC-0059.

## Performance

Partner performance should be measured against agreed commercial, customer, quality and compliance expectations.

## Risk

Material partner dependencies should be included in enterprise risk and continuity planning.

## Success Standard

Partnerships create measurable value without introducing unmanaged legal, brand, financial, operational or customer risk.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Define partnership qualification criteria.
2. Establish due-diligence and business-case reviews.
3. Standardize material agreements and operating standards.
4. Monitor partner performance.
5. Escalate or exit underperforming or high-risk relationships.
