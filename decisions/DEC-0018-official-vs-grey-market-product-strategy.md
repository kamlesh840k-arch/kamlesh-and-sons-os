# DEC-0018 — Official vs. Grey-Market Product Strategy

**Decision ID:** DEC-0018  
**Title:** Establish the Official-Distribution Product Strategy  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08  
**Owner:** KAMLESH & SONS  
**Related Documents:** [Founding Charter](../docs/founders-playbook/FOUNDING-CHARTER.md), [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md)  
**Related Decisions:** DEC-0002, DEC-0006, DEC-0009, DEC-0013, DEC-0017

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will position **officially distributed, properly documented products and transparent customer protection** as the preferred foundation of its technology retail model.

The company will compete against grey-market alternatives primarily through **trust, authenticity, warranty clarity, service, experience, and long-term customer value**, rather than attempting to win only through price.

## Context

The market may contain products entering through different distribution channels. Customers can therefore encounter meaningful differences in:

- Authenticity and provenance
- Warranty coverage
- After-sales support
- Regional compatibility
- Documentation
- Tax and invoicing requirements
- Software or network compatibility
- Resale and ownership confidence
- Service availability

KAMLESH & SONS should make these differences understandable to customers without misleading or disparaging competitors.

## Customer Promise

Where KAMLESH & SONS offers an officially distributed product, the customer should receive clear information about:

- Product authenticity
- Applicable warranty
- Seller or distributor relationship where relevant
- Invoice and tax documentation
- Included accessories and specifications
- Service and support pathway
- Any important regional limitations

Customers should never need to guess what protection they are receiving.

## Pricing Philosophy

KAMLESH & SONS will not make "cheapest price" the primary definition of value.

The value proposition should consider the total ownership experience:

**Product + Authenticity + Warranty + Service + Expertise + Convenience + Trust**

A higher price may be justified when it corresponds to meaningful additional value, but pricing claims must remain transparent and evidence-based.

## Grey-Market Products

The existence of grey-market products should be treated as a market reality requiring research and responsible commercial strategy.

KAMLESH & SONS should not make unsupported claims about the legality, quality, or authenticity of a competitor's product merely because it is sourced through a different channel.

Where the company discusses channel differences, statements should be factual, verifiable, and relevant to the customer's purchase decision.

## Sales and Staff Principles

Staff should be trained to explain channel differences clearly and professionally.

They should focus on:

- What the customer receives
- What warranty applies
- What service is available
- What documentation is provided
- What limitations may exist
- Why KAMLESH & SONS recommends a particular option

Staff should not use fear, misinformation, or pressure to discourage customers from alternatives.

## Product Acceptance Standards

Before stocking a product, KAMLESH & SONS should eventually define documented requirements covering, as applicable:

- Supplier legitimacy
- Product authenticity
- Distribution channel
- Warranty support
- Documentation
- Tax and invoicing requirements
- Regional compatibility
- Return and replacement conditions
- Serviceability
- Commercial viability

These standards should be formalized in future Operations and Legal documentation.

## Strategic Advantage

The official-distribution strategy supports the broader brand position:

> **We are not building a phone store. We are building Nepal's most trusted premium technology brand.**

Trust becomes a competitive asset when customers know where products came from, what support they receive, and who will help them after purchase.

## Research Requirement

DEC-0017 establishes research as a core capability. The company should conduct local research before making strong commercial claims about official versus grey-market market share, customer preferences, price gaps, or regulatory treatment.

Research should distinguish verified facts from assumptions and should be dated because market conditions can change.

## Guardrails

KAMLESH & SONS must:

- Avoid deceptive claims about competitors.
- Avoid presenting unverified channel information as fact.
- Maintain accurate invoices and documentation.
- Follow applicable tax, consumer, distribution, and regulatory requirements.
- Clearly communicate applicable warranty terms.
- Escalate uncertain legal or regulatory questions for professional review.

## Implementation Status

This decision establishes the **strategic preference for trustworthy, properly documented distribution and transparent customer protection**.

It does not yet approve specific suppliers, distributors, brands, import arrangements, product eligibility rules, pricing policies, or legal interpretations.

Those matters require separate commercial, research, financial, and legal decisions.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded. Specific product categories or brands may require different commercial arrangements where legally and commercially appropriate.

Any material change to this strategy should document the rationale, evidence, customer impact, financial implications, legal considerations, alternatives considered, and affected repository documents.
