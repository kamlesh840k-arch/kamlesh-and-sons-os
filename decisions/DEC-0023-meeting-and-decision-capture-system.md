## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will maintain a structured **Meeting and Decision Capture System** so that important discussions, decisions, responsibilities, and follow-up actions remain traceable in K&S OS.

Meetings are a source of organizational knowledge. They should not become the only place where important decisions exist.

## Meeting Record Standard

Important meetings should use a consistent record containing, where applicable:

- Meeting ID
- Title
- Date and time
- Participants
- Facilitator
- Agenda
- Context
- Discussion summary
- Decisions made
- Open questions
- Action items
- Owner for each action
- Due date
- Dependencies
- Follow-up meeting
- Related decisions
- Related research
- Related innovations
- Meeting status

A meeting record should capture outcomes rather than attempting to reproduce every spoken sentence.

## Meeting IDs

Formal meeting records should receive unique IDs, for example:

`MTG-0001`, `MTG-0002`, `MTG-0003` …

IDs should remain stable and should not be reused.

## Decision Capture

When a meeting produces a material strategic or operating decision, the outcome should be transferred into the Decision Log when appropriate.

The intended flow is:

**Meeting → Discussion → Proposed Decision → Decision Record → Action → Review**

A meeting note may record a proposed decision, but a proposal should not be presented as an approved company decision unless the appropriate approval has occurred.

## Action Items

Every meaningful action item should have:

- A clear description
- One accountable owner
- A target date where appropriate
- Relevant dependencies
- A link to the related meeting or decision

Actions should be closed, updated, or explicitly deferred rather than silently disappearing from the record.

## Meeting Types

The system may support different meeting types, including:

- Founder meetings
- Leadership meetings
- Operations reviews
- Store reviews
- Product reviews
- Technology reviews
- AI reviews
- Customer experience reviews
- Research reviews
- Project meetings
- Partner meetings
- Retrospectives

The template may be adapted to the meeting's purpose while retaining enough structure for traceability.

## Before the Meeting

Where practical, meeting owners should define:

- Objective
- Required participants
- Agenda
- Pre-reading
- Decisions required
- Expected outputs

Meetings should have a clear reason to exist. Not every conversation needs to become a formal meeting record.

## During the Meeting

The facilitator or designated note owner should capture:

- Important facts
- Alternatives discussed
- Key concerns
- Decisions or proposals
- Action items
- Unresolved questions

Discussion should be summarized objectively rather than rewritten to favor one participant after the fact.

## After the Meeting

Meeting records should be completed promptly enough that participants can verify the outcomes while context remains fresh.

Where applicable:

1. Decisions are linked or created.
2. Action owners are notified.
3. Due dates are recorded.
4. Research gaps are added to the research queue.
5. Innovations are added to the Innovation Vault.
6. Roadmap impacts are documented.

## Confidentiality

Some meetings may contain sensitive commercial, financial, personnel, customer, partner, or legal information.

Access to such records should be appropriately restricted. Sensitive information should not be copied into public-facing documentation merely because it was discussed in a meeting.

## AI-Assisted Meeting Capture

Future K&S OS systems may use AI to assist with meeting summaries, action extraction, and cross-referencing.

AI-generated meeting records must be reviewed by an accountable human before being treated as authoritative.

AI must not silently convert discussion into decisions or assign responsibilities without human verification.

## Meeting Quality

Meetings should be evaluated by outcomes rather than volume.

A good meeting should create clarity such as:

- What was decided?
- What remains uncertain?
- Who does what?
- By when?
- What evidence is still required?
- What changes in the roadmap or documentation?

## Cross-Reference Standard

Meeting records should link to relevant:

- Decision records
- Innovation records
- Research records
- Roadmap items
- Project documents
- Action tracking

This creates a traceable organizational history.

## Implementation Status

This decision establishes the meeting and decision-capture framework. It does not require every informal conversation to be documented as a formal meeting.

Templates and workflows should be added progressively under the repository's `meetings/` and `templates/` areas.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded. The meeting system may evolve as K&S OS introduces project management, CRM, collaboration, and AI capabilities.

Any material change to the governance model should document the rationale and affected processes.
