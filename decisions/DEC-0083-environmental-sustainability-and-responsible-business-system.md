# DEC-0083 — Environmental Sustainability & Responsible Business System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0083  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will progressively manage environmental and responsible-business impacts in ways that improve efficiency, resilience, compliance and stakeholder trust without compromising core commercial viability.

> **Use resources responsibly, reduce avoidable waste, and build a business that can last.**

## Core Areas

- Energy efficiency
- Waste reduction
- Packaging management
- Responsible sourcing
- Resource efficiency
- Environmental compliance
- Supplier expectations
- Measurement
- Improvement initiatives

## Operations

Stores, warehouses and offices should identify practical opportunities to reduce unnecessary energy, materials and waste.

## Packaging

Packaging decisions should consider customer needs, product protection, cost and avoidable environmental impact.

## Suppliers

Relevant environmental or responsible-sourcing expectations may be incorporated into supplier selection and review where material.

## Compliance

Environmental obligations should be tracked under DEC-0058 where applicable.

## Measurement

The company should prioritize measurable initiatives with meaningful operational or environmental value rather than unsupported claims.

## Success Standard

K&S reduces avoidable resource use and waste, meets applicable obligations and can demonstrate responsible practices without overstating its impact.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Identify material environmental impacts.
2. Establish baseline measurements where useful.
3. Prioritize practical efficiency and waste-reduction initiatives.
4. Add relevant supplier expectations.
5. Track progress and compliance.
6. Communicate results truthfully.
