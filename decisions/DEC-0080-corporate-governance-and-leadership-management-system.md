# DEC-0080 — Corporate Governance & Leadership Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0080  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will use clear governance structures that define authority, accountability, decision rights, reporting and oversight as the organization grows.

> **Make ownership clear, decisions deliberate, and accountability visible.**

## Core Controls

- Governance roles
- Decision rights
- Delegation of authority
- Leadership cadence
- Management reporting
- Conflict management
- Strategic oversight
- Policy approval
- Escalation paths
- Governance records

## Decision Rights

Material decisions should have clear owners and approval thresholds appropriate to financial, legal, operational and strategic impact.

## Leadership Cadence

Leadership should use consistent forums for operational, financial, risk and strategic review.

## Delegation

Authority may be delegated while accountability remains explicit.

## Oversight

Leadership should challenge material assumptions, risks and outcomes rather than relying solely on status reporting.

## Success Standard

Employees know who can decide what, leadership sees material issues early, and important decisions are documented and accountable.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Define governance roles and decision rights.
2. Establish delegation and approval thresholds.
3. Standardize leadership review cadence.
4. Improve management reporting.
5. Document material decisions and actions.
6. Review governance as organizational complexity increases.
