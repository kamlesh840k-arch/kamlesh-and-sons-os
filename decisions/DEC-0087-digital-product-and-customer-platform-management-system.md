# DEC-0087 — Digital Product & Customer Platform Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0087  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will manage customer-facing digital products as business capabilities with clear product ownership, customer outcomes, release discipline, measurement and lifecycle governance.

> **Build digital products around customer outcomes, not feature volume.**

## Core Controls

- Product ownership
- Customer problems
- Product roadmap
- Prioritization
- UX standards
- Release management
- Product analytics
- Reliability
- Customer feedback
- Lifecycle decisions

## Product Discovery

Digital product work should begin with a clear customer or business problem and evidence supporting its importance.

## Prioritization

Features should be evaluated using customer value, strategic value, effort, risk and measurable outcomes.

## Release Management

Material releases should have appropriate testing, rollback or recovery plans and communication where customer impact is significant.

## Analytics

Products should measure meaningful outcomes such as task completion, conversion, retention, service quality and customer satisfaction where appropriate.

## Feedback

Customer feedback and service signals should feed the product backlog.

## Accessibility

Customer-facing digital experiences should consider accessibility and applicable requirements.

## Success Standard

Digital products solve important customer problems reliably, improve measurable outcomes and remain maintainable as K&S scales.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Establish digital product ownership.
2. Define customer problems and success measures.
3. Build evidence-based roadmaps.
4. Standardize release and quality practices.
5. Connect product analytics and customer feedback.
6. Review product value and lifecycle continuously.
