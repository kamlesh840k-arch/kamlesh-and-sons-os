## Decision

The official company name is:

# KAMLESH & SONS

This name will be used as the primary corporate and brand identity unless a future formal decision changes it.

## Context

The company is intended to become a premium technology house rather than remain positioned as a conventional mobile shop. The name therefore needs to support a long-term, multi-business, family-owned identity and remain suitable as the company expands across Nepal and potentially beyond.

## Rationale

**KAMLESH & SONS** was selected because it communicates:

- Timelessness
- Premium positioning
- Heritage
- Trust
- Family ownership
- Long-term continuity
- Scalability across multiple businesses
- Suitability for future expansion

The name provides a foundation that can extend beyond the company's initial technology-retail business.

## Alternatives Considered

The following names were considered and rejected:

- KAMLESH
- KAMLESH by Narayan
- KAMLESH by Sons
- KAMLESH & Co.

The selected name was considered the strongest fit for the intended heritage, family, and long-term business identity.

## Implications

This decision establishes the company-name baseline for future work involving:

- Brand identity
- Store signage
- Packaging
- Digital properties
- Legal and corporate documentation
- Customer communications
- Marketing
- Technology platforms
- Expansion planning

Future brand work may develop visual identities, sub-brands, or product/service names, but those should remain consistent with the official company identity unless separately approved.

## Decision Principle

The company should build recognition around **KAMLESH & SONS** as an enduring technology-house identity rather than defining itself only by a single product category.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded by a new decision record. Any proposal to change the official company name must document the rationale, implications, alternatives, and affected repository documents.
