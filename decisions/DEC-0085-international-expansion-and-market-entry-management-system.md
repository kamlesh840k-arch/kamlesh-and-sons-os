# DEC-0085 — International Expansion & Market Entry Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0085  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will approach expansion into new countries or jurisdictions through staged market-entry assessment covering demand, economics, regulation, supply chain, people, technology and risk.

> **Enter new markets deliberately, validate locally, and scale only after the model proves itself.**

## Core Controls

- Market research
- Regulatory assessment
- Entry-mode analysis
- Business case
- Pilot launch
- Supply-chain readiness
- Local partnerships
- Tax and legal review
- Currency and cash planning
- Scale gates

## Market Assessment

New markets should be assessed for customer demand, competitive conditions, pricing, category fit, operating costs and strategic relevance.

## Regulatory Review

Market entry requires appropriate legal, tax, employment, consumer, privacy, product and import/export review under DEC-0058.

## Entry Mode

Options may include direct operation, local partnership, distributor, marketplace or franchise structures, selected according to risk and control needs.

## Pilot

Where uncertainty is high, K&S should validate the model through a controlled pilot before significant capital commitment.

## Scale Gates

Expansion should define measurable gates for demand, margin, service quality, operational readiness and risk before scaling.

## Success Standard

Leadership can explain why a market should be entered, how it will be operated compliantly, what capital and capability it requires, and what evidence would justify scaling or exiting.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Establish market-entry criteria.
2. Conduct demand and competitive research.
3. Complete legal, tax and regulatory assessment.
4. Build a market-entry business case.
5. Run a controlled pilot where appropriate.
6. Scale only after defined evidence and approval gates are met.
