# DEC-0068 — Customer Data, CRM & Lifecycle Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0068  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will maintain a trusted customer-management capability that connects customer identity, interactions, purchases, service history and lifecycle engagement while respecting privacy and access controls.

> **Know the customer without exploiting the customer.**

## Principles

- One trusted customer view where appropriate
- Data minimization and privacy
- Accurate customer records
- Clear ownership of customer relationships
- Useful lifecycle engagement
- Service and commercial coordination
- Measurable retention and value

## Core Capabilities

1. Customer profiles and identity resolution
2. Consent and communication preferences
3. Purchase and interaction history
4. Customer segmentation
5. Lifecycle journeys
6. Service-history integration
7. Retention and win-back workflows
8. Customer-value analytics
9. Duplicate and data-quality management
10. Role-based access and auditability

## Lifecycle

The system should support **Prospect → First Purchase → Onboarding → Repeat Purchase → Loyalty → Win-Back** while coordinating with marketing under DEC-0059 and customer service under DEC-0054.

## Governance

Customer data must follow DEC-0055 privacy and security controls. Access should be limited to legitimate business needs, and material customer-data processing should be documented.

## Success Standard

Management can identify important customer needs, interactions and lifecycle opportunities without losing control of privacy, data quality or customer trust.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Define customer data ownership and core fields.
2. Establish privacy and preference controls.
3. Consolidate customer interactions where practical.
4. Build lifecycle segmentation.
5. Connect service and purchase history.
6. Introduce retention analytics and controlled automation.
