## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will be positioned as a:

> **Premium Technology House**

It will **not** be positioned as a conventional mobile phone shop.

The initial technology portfolio includes:

- Smartphones
- Laptops
- Tablets
- Accessories
- Smart Home
- Wearables
- Future Technology

Retail is the company's starting point rather than the limit of its long-term identity.

## Context

The long-term ambition is to build a trusted premium technology brand that can expand from a flagship retail experience in Nepalgunj into Western Nepal, major cities, and eventually a nationwide network.

A mobile-shop positioning would narrowly associate the company with one product category. A technology-house positioning provides room for multiple product categories, services, experiences, and future technology initiatives.

## Rationale

The Premium Technology House position is intended to:

- Create a broader and more durable brand identity.
- Support multi-category retail.
- Enable premium customer experiences.
- Provide a foundation for future services and technology platforms.
- Support authorized relationships with multiple global brands.
- Make national expansion easier to communicate and standardize.
- Align the physical store, AI Concierge, CRM, customer portal, and future technology ecosystem under one identity.

## Brand Implications

The positioning should influence:

### Store

Stores should feel like technology experience centers rather than crowded product shops.

### Customer Experience

The company should compete through trust, knowledge, service, experience, and convenience rather than price alone.

### Product Presentation

Customers should be encouraged to experience products before purchasing. Live and interactive displays should replace a locked-showcase mentality where practical.

### Services

The brand may extend beyond product sales into services such as:

- Device setup
- Data transfer
- Screen protector installation
- Warranty assistance
- Trade-in
- Corporate sales
- Workshops
- Personal consultation

### Technology

The positioning supports future systems including the AI Concierge, customer portal, CRM, digital warranty services, trade-in technology, and other technology-enabled experiences.

## Customer Promise

The intended customer experience is that customers should feel they **visited KAMLESH & SONS**, not simply that they bought a phone.

Customers should leave feeling:

- Respected
- Informed
- Comfortable
- Confident
- Proud of their purchase
- Interested in returning

## Strategic Principle

> **Experience > Discounts**

KAMLESH & SONS should not build its primary competitive advantage around discounting. Price remains important, but the long-term differentiation should come from the complete experience surrounding the product.

## Scope

This decision establishes the strategic positioning. Detailed brand identity, visual language, store design, customer-experience standards, product-category strategy, and marketing execution should be documented separately and linked back to this decision.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded by a new decision record. Any proposal to reposition KAMLESH & SONS should document the strategic rationale, customer impact, brand implications, alternatives considered, and affected repository documents.
