# K&S OS — v0.1.0 Foundation Master Decision

**Decision ID:** DEC-FOUNDATION-v0.1.0-MASTER  
**Status:** Active Foundation Master  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will operate v0.1.0 as a governed foundation layer: founding principles, decision rights, documentation and institutional memory, repository and technology governance, security/privacy principles, legal/compliance and risk principles, core financial controls, and management-system foundations.

The foundation is intentionally smaller than the full future operating model. Domain-specific implementation is deferred to the roadmap milestones where it belongs.

## Foundation Domains

### 1. Founding & Strategic Principles
Mission, vision, founder principles, strategic intent and the company's documentation-first operating philosophy.

### 2. Governance & Decision Rights
Authority, accountability, delegation, escalation, governance forums and material decision recording.

### 3. Documentation & Institutional Memory
Documentation standards, decision logs, templates, knowledge retention, institutional memory and innovation records.

### 4. Repository & Technology Governance
Repository-as-source-of-truth, architecture principles, technology governance, change/version discipline and baseline security/privacy principles.

### 5. Legal, Compliance, Risk & Continuity
Legal/compliance governance, enterprise risk principles, internal controls, privacy/security governance and business-continuity principles.

### 6. Financial Control Foundation
Core financial-control principles, accountability, approval discipline and management visibility. Detailed finance operations are deferred to v0.8.

### 7. Management System Foundation
Strategic planning, project/portfolio governance, management reporting, continuous improvement and operating-model integration.

## Explicitly Out of Scope for v0.1

Detailed brand identity, store design, customer-experience implementation, CRM operations, pricing systems, AI products, API ecosystem, supply-chain operations, procurement operations, inventory operations, store SOPs, workforce scheduling, marketing execution, detailed financial systems, franchise/partnership expansion and international expansion.

These remain valid business needs but belong to later roadmap milestones.

## Historical Source Records

This master consolidates the foundation intent represented by the DEC history. Historical DEC files remain authoritative source records and are not deleted by this decision.

The complete mapping is maintained in `docs/decision-consolidation-registry.md` and `docs/v0.1-decision-inventory-classification.md`.

## Release Gate

Completion of this master does not itself close v0.1. Closure occurs only when the criteria in DEC-0100 are satisfied and leadership formally accepts DEC-0101.

## Success Standard

The K&S repository has a coherent, documented and governed foundation from which the v0.2–v0.9 operating systems can be built without losing decision history or accountability.
