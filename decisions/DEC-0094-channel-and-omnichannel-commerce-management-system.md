# DEC-0094 — Channel & Omnichannel Commerce Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0094  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will coordinate physical stores, digital channels, delivery and other customer touchpoints through common commercial, inventory, service and experience standards.

> **Let customers choose the channel without making the business behave like separate companies.**

## Core Areas

- Channel roles
- Inventory visibility
- Order orchestration
- Customer identity
- Pricing consistency
- Fulfillment
- Returns
- Service continuity
- Channel performance

## Success Standard

Customers receive a coherent K&S experience across channels while leadership retains visibility into channel economics and operational dependencies.
