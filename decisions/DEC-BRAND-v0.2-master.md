# K&S OS — v0.2.0 Brand Identity Master Decision

**Decision ID:** DEC-BRAND-v0.2-MASTER  
**Status:** Active — v0.2.0  
**Target Version:** v0.2.0 — Brand Identity

## Purpose

Define the governing brand-identity system for KAMLESH & SONS so that future store, customer, marketing and digital work can build from one coherent brand foundation.

## Roadmap Scope

The v0.2 roadmap defines the primary focus as:

- Brand strategy
- Brand identity
- Typography
- Visual standards

This master is limited to that scope. Store design belongs to v0.3, customer experience to v0.4, technology to v0.5, and marketing activation to v0.7.

## Brand Strategy

Establish the approved strategic foundation for the K&S brand, including its purpose, positioning, intended audience, differentiation, brand promise and strategic personality.

## Brand Identity

Define the recognizable identity system required for consistent use across company touchpoints, including the approved naming, identity architecture, logo/mark direction and core visual identity components.

## Typography

Define the approved typographic system, hierarchy, usage principles and accessibility/readability expectations for K&S communications and interfaces.

## Visual Standards

Define the visual standards that keep future materials coherent, including color, imagery, layout, graphic treatment, composition and basic application rules.

## Governance

- The v0.2 brand master is the source of truth for approved brand-identity rules.
- Brand decisions should be recorded through the DEC system.
- Changes to approved identity standards require explicit rationale and traceability.
- v0.3+ work must consume the approved brand system rather than independently redefining it.

## Explicitly Out of Scope

The following remain later roadmap work:

- Store layout and visual merchandising systems — v0.3
- Detailed customer journey and service standards — v0.4
- AI and technology implementation — v0.5
- Operational SOPs and workforce systems — v0.6
- Marketing campaigns and growth systems — v0.7
- Detailed financial systems — v0.8
- Expansion systems — v0.9

## Success Standard

K&S has one approved, documented and governed brand identity system that can be applied consistently across future store, customer, technology and marketing work without repeated foundational redefinition.
