## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will maintain the Founding Charter and the focused documents indexed in the Founder's Playbook README as the canonical, usable expression of its founding direction.

## Rationale

The accepted foundation already records the vision, mission, long-term goal, customer philosophy, and founder operating principles. Focused playbook documents make those decisions easier to discover and apply without changing their authority or wording.

## Authority and Precedence

- Existing accepted DEC records remain authoritative.
- The Founding Charter remains the integrated founding record.
- Focused playbook documents interpret and operationalize those records.
- A conflict must be resolved in favor of the accepted decision record until a formal superseding decision is approved.
- Decision IDs 0027 and 0028 remain assigned to their existing records and are not reused.

## Scope

The canonical set covers vision, mission, brand philosophy, core values, customer promise, founder manifesto, long-term vision, decision-making principles, and company culture.

This decision does not reopen v0.1, alter later roadmap approvals, approve spending or implementation, or replace detailed brand, people, customer-experience, legal, security, or operating standards.

## Implementation

1. Maintain a complete index in the playbook README.
2. Link each focused document to its controlling decisions.
3. Review the set when a controlling decision is superseded.
4. Preserve material changes through versioned, traceable history.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded. Material changes to the set or its authority model require a new decision record.
