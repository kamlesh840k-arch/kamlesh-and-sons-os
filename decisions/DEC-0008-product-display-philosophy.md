## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will present technology products as **experiences to discover and use**, rather than primarily as inventory displayed for sale.

Where practical, customers should be able to interact directly with live products before purchasing.

## Principles

### 1. Live Products

Demonstration devices should be functional and ready for meaningful customer interaction where commercially and operationally appropriate.

### 2. Hands-On Discovery

Customers should be encouraged to touch, test, compare, and understand products before making a purchase decision.

### 3. Clear Comparison

The display environment should make it easy to understand meaningful differences between products without overwhelming customers with technical information.

### 4. Minimal Presentation

Products, signage, pricing, and information should be presented cleanly and intentionally. Display density should not create visual clutter.

### 5. Knowledgeable Assistance

Product displays should work together with trained staff and future digital tools such as the AI Concierge. Technology should support the human experience, not replace it.

### 6. Accessory Discovery

Accessories should be easy to discover and understand while remaining integrated into the overall premium environment.

## Customer Journey

The intended product-discovery flow is:

**Discover → Touch → Explore → Compare → Understand → Decide**

The display system should support this journey without creating unnecessary sales pressure.

## Strategic Implications

This decision should inform future work on:

- Visual merchandising
- Store fixtures
- Demonstration tables
- Product information systems
- Pricing presentation
- Security systems
- Accessibility
- Staff training
- AI Concierge integration
- Accessory merchandising

Detailed standards will be defined in the Store Design and Operations documentation.

## Guardrails

Open product interaction must still account for:

- Product security
- Device availability
- Hygiene
- Maintenance
- Loss prevention
- Accessibility
- Manufacturer requirements
- Commercial practicality

The experience principle does not require every product to be permanently available for unrestricted use.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded. Detailed implementation standards may evolve as the flagship store is designed and tested.

Any material change to the product display philosophy should document the rationale, customer impact, operational implications, alternatives considered, and affected repository documents.
