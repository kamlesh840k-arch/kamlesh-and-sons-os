# DEC-0097 — Workforce Planning & Labor Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0097  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will plan workforce capacity against customer demand, operating hours, service standards and labor requirements while maintaining lawful and fair employment practices.

> **Put the right capability in the right place at the right time.**

## Core Areas

- Workforce demand planning
- Staffing models
- Scheduling
- Attendance
- Capacity monitoring
- Labor productivity
- Overtime controls
- Skills coverage
- Workforce cost
- Compliance

## Success Standard

Stores and operations have sufficient capability to serve customers effectively without persistent understaffing, unnecessary overcapacity or uncontrolled labor cost.
