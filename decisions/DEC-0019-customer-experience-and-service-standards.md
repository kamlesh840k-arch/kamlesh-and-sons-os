## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will treat **customer experience and service quality as core operating capabilities**, not as optional additions to product sales.

The company will compete through trust, knowledge, hospitality, convenience, service, and consistency rather than relying primarily on discounts.

## Customer Experience Promise

Customers should leave KAMLESH & SONS feeling:

- Respected
- Well informed
- Comfortable asking questions
- Confident about their purchase
- Supported after purchase
- Proud of their decision
- Interested in returning

The intended experience is:

> **A customer should remember the visit, not merely the product purchased.**

## Experience Principles

### 1. Welcome Without Pressure

Customers should be welcomed professionally and given enough space to explore products without aggressive selling.

### 2. Knowledge Before Persuasion

Staff should understand the customer's needs and explain meaningful differences before recommending a product.

### 3. Hands-On Experience

Where practical, customers should be able to interact with live products and understand how they work before purchase.

### 4. Clear Recommendations

Recommendations should be based on customer requirements, compatibility, budget, and intended use—not solely on inventory pressure or sales incentives.

### 5. Transparent Information

Prices, product condition, warranty, financing terms, accessories, service commitments, and important limitations should be communicated clearly.

### 6. Calm Problem Solving

Complaints and service problems should be handled calmly, respectfully, and with ownership of the customer's experience.

### 7. Continuity After Purchase

The relationship should continue beyond the transaction through setup, warranty assistance, service, trade-in, support, and future recommendations where relevant.

## Service Standards

Future operational SOPs should define measurable standards for:

- Greeting
- Product consultation
- Demonstration
- Recommendation
- Billing
- Device setup
- Data transfer
- Screen protector installation
- Warranty assistance
- Repair intake and updates
- Trade-in consultation
- Complaint handling
- Follow-up

Specific response times, service fees, escalation levels, and staff responsibilities require separate operational decisions.

## Human + Technology Experience

Customer experience should combine human hospitality with technology.

The intended model is:

**AI and digital systems provide information, speed, continuity, and convenience.**  
**People provide empathy, judgment, trust, and relationship.**

The AI Concierge described in DEC-0010 should therefore support staff rather than create a barrier between the customer and the company.

## Service Recovery

When the company makes a mistake or a customer has a legitimate problem, the response should prioritize:

1. Listening
2. Understanding the issue
3. Acknowledging the customer's experience
4. Explaining the available resolution
5. Taking ownership of the next step
6. Following through
7. Learning from recurring failures

Service recovery should feed operational improvement rather than treating each complaint as an isolated event.

## Measurement

The company should eventually measure customer experience through a combination of quantitative and qualitative indicators, such as:

- Customer satisfaction
- Repeat visits
- Repeat purchases
- Referrals
- Complaint frequency
- Complaint resolution time
- Service completion time
- Warranty experience
- Customer feedback
- Staff knowledge assessments

Metrics should be interpreted in context and should not encourage staff to optimize numbers at the expense of genuine customer care.

## Training Requirement

Customer experience standards require trained people.

Future training systems should cover:

- Product knowledge
- Needs discovery
- Communication
- Demonstration skills
- Financing explanation
- Warranty explanation
- Service processes
- Complaint handling
- Privacy
- Responsible AI use
- Brand standards

## Strategic Principle

> **Experience > Discounts.**

Price remains important, but KAMLESH & SONS should build defensible customer value through the complete ownership experience.

## Implementation Status

This decision establishes the **customer experience and service philosophy**. It does not yet define every SOP, KPI, service-level agreement, staffing model, compensation structure, or training curriculum.

Those operational standards should be documented separately and cross-referenced to this decision.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded. Operational standards may become more specific over time while remaining consistent with the principles established here.

Any material change to the customer experience philosophy should document the rationale, customer impact, business implications, alternatives considered, and affected repository documents.
