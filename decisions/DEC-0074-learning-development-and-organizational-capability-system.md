# DEC-0074 — Learning, Development & Organizational Capability System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0074  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will build organizational capability through structured onboarding, role training, leadership development, knowledge sharing and measurable skill progression.

> **Build capability deliberately so the business gets stronger as it grows.**

## Core Capabilities

- Role-based onboarding
- Skills matrices
- Product knowledge
- Customer-service training
- Manager development
- Compliance training
- Technology training
- Knowledge management
- Performance coaching
- Succession readiness

## Learning Needs

Training priorities should be based on role requirements, performance gaps, operational changes, compliance obligations and strategic capability needs.

## Onboarding

New employees should receive role-specific orientation, systems access guidance, policies, safety information and customer standards.

## Skills Matrix

Critical roles should have documented capabilities and proficiency expectations where useful.

## Knowledge Management

Important operational knowledge should be captured in controlled documentation rather than depending on one individual's memory.

## Leadership Development

Managers should develop capability in planning, coaching, decision-making, financial understanding, customer leadership and risk management.

## Training Measurement

Where practical, learning should be evaluated through completion, demonstrated capability, operational performance or other meaningful outcomes rather than attendance alone.

## Success Standard

K&S can identify critical capabilities, develop people against them and reduce dependence on undocumented individual knowledge.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Define critical roles and capability requirements.
2. Build onboarding standards.
3. Create role-based learning paths.
4. Establish skills matrices for priority roles.
5. Develop manager and leadership programs.
6. Measure whether training improves performance.
