# DEC-0077 — Project Portfolio & Strategic Initiative Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0077  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will manage major initiatives as a portfolio with explicit outcomes, owners, dependencies, resources, risks and review gates.

> **Do fewer important things well, and know why each one matters.**

## Core Controls

- Initiative intake
- Strategic alignment
- Business cases
- Prioritization
- Ownership
- Milestones
- Dependencies
- Risk management
- Benefits tracking
- Post-implementation review

## Prioritization

Initiatives should be evaluated using strategic value, customer impact, financial value, urgency, risk, effort and organizational capacity.

## Business Cases

Material initiatives should define expected outcomes, assumptions, costs, dependencies and success measures before significant commitment where practical.

## Portfolio View

Leadership should maintain visibility into active initiatives, capacity constraints, cross-project dependencies and competing priorities.

## Stage Gates

Large or uncertain initiatives should use staged decisions such as **Explore → Validate → Build → Launch → Scale**.

## Benefits

Expected benefits should be measurable where practical and reviewed after implementation.

## Resource Allocation

Portfolio decisions should consider cash, people, technology and operational capacity under DEC-0057 and related operating systems.

## Success Standard

Leadership knows what initiatives are active, why they matter, who owns them, what they consume, what risks they carry and whether they are delivering the promised value.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Create a standard initiative intake.
2. Define prioritization criteria.
3. Establish portfolio reporting.
4. Introduce stage gates for major initiatives.
5. Track benefits and dependencies.
6. Conduct post-implementation reviews.
