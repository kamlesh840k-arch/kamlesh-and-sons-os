## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will treat **research and market intelligence as a core business capability** and will use evidence to guide major commercial, customer, technology, and expansion decisions.

Research should reduce uncertainty before significant commitments are made.

## Priority Research Areas

The initial research agenda includes:

- Smartphone sales in Nepalgunj
- Premium technology segment demand
- EMI adoption
- Customer demographics
- Customer brand preferences
- Competitor analysis
- Official vs. grey-market products
- VAT bill requirements

These topics form the starting research agenda and should be expanded as new business questions emerge.

## Research Principles

### 1. Evidence Before Assumption

Important decisions should distinguish between verified facts, estimates, assumptions, opinions, and hypotheses.

### 2. Local Relevance

Research should prioritize Nepalgunj and Western Nepal where decisions concern the initial market, while broader Nepal data should be used when evaluating national opportunities.

### 3. Multiple Sources

Where practical, significant findings should be checked against multiple credible sources rather than relying on a single data point.

### 4. Time Sensitivity

Market conditions can change. Research should record its date and indicate when information may require revalidation.

### 5. Decision Linkage

Research should answer a business question and identify which decision, assumption, roadmap item, or experiment it informs.

## Research Record Standard

Future research documents should capture, where applicable:

- Research ID
- Question
- Date
- Scope
- Sources
- Method
- Findings
- Assumptions
- Limitations
- Implications
- Recommendation
- Related Decisions
- Related Innovations

This creates traceability between evidence and company decisions.

## Research Areas

### Market

Study market size, demand, growth, customer segments, purchasing behavior, and category trends.

### Customer

Understand customer needs, preferences, willingness to pay, financing behavior, experience expectations, and pain points.

### Competition

Track competitors, positioning, pricing, assortment, services, customer experience, official distribution, and market gaps.

### Technology

Evaluate emerging retail technology, AI, CRM, customer portals, automation, and relevant infrastructure.

### Regulation

Monitor applicable requirements affecting retail, taxation, VAT documentation, consumer protection, financing, privacy, data, and technology operations.

### Expansion

Research potential future locations using demand, competition, economics, infrastructure, staffing, logistics, and strategic fit.

## Research vs. Decision

Research does not automatically become company policy.

The intended flow is:

**Question → Research → Evidence → Analysis → Decision → Documentation → Implementation**

A research finding should not be represented as an approved company decision until the appropriate decision is formally recorded.

## Research Quality Guardrails

Research should clearly identify uncertainty. Where reliable information is unavailable, the repository should say so rather than presenting an unsupported estimate as fact.

Forecasts and projections should identify their assumptions.

Competitive intelligence should be collected and used ethically and lawfully.

Customer research should respect privacy and obtain appropriate consent where personal information is collected.

## Research Repository

The `docs/10-research/` area should become the primary home for structured research, while major conclusions that change company direction should be cross-referenced from the relevant decision records.

Research should remain discoverable through IDs and cross-references so future teams can understand why a decision was made.

## Strategic Principle

> **Measure twice. Decide once. Learn continuously.**

The company should treat research as an ongoing capability rather than a one-time exercise performed only before opening the first store.

## Implementation Status

This decision establishes the **research and market-intelligence operating principle**. It does not approve specific research conclusions, market sizes, forecasts, competitors, locations, or investment assumptions.

Those findings must be documented separately and evaluated before being used as decision inputs.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded. The research agenda should evolve as KAMLESH & SONS moves from flagship validation to Western Nepal expansion and eventually national expansion.

Any material change to the research philosophy should document the rationale, affected decision processes, alternatives considered, and repository documents requiring updates.
