# DEC-0088 — Innovation, Research & Emerging Technology Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0088  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will explore emerging technologies, business models and customer opportunities through disciplined experimentation before committing significant resources.

> **Explore boldly, test cheaply, and scale only what creates durable value.**

## Core Controls

- Opportunity discovery
- Research backlog
- Technology scouting
- Hypothesis definition
- Prototyping
- Pilot testing
- Evidence review
- Intellectual-property awareness
- Risk assessment
- Scale or stop decisions

## Exploration

Emerging opportunities should be connected to real customer, operational or strategic problems rather than technology novelty alone.

## Experiments

Early experiments should minimize cost and commitment while maximizing useful learning.

## Evaluation

Pilots should define success criteria, risks, assumptions and decision gates before meaningful investment.

## Technology

Potential technologies should be evaluated for security, privacy, integration, reliability, total cost and organizational capability under relevant K&S decisions.

## Learning

Failed experiments should produce documented learning when the insight has future value.

## Success Standard

K&S has a repeatable way to discover and validate promising opportunities without allowing speculative projects to consume disproportionate resources.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Establish an innovation opportunity backlog.
2. Define lightweight research and experiment standards.
3. Run small prototypes and pilots.
4. Evaluate evidence and economics.
5. Scale validated opportunities through the project portfolio.
6. Archive unsuccessful experiments with useful lessons.
