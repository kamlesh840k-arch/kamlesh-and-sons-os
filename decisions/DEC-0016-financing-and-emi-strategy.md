## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will treat **EMI and customer financing as a major future sales capability** rather than a secondary payment option.

The company intends to build relationships with suitable banks and licensed finance partners to make premium technology more accessible while maintaining responsible lending practices and the company's premium customer experience.

## Context

Premium technology products can represent a significant upfront expense for customers. Responsible financing can reduce the immediate payment barrier and allow customers to purchase products that better match their needs.

Financing should therefore be considered part of the customer journey, while the company must avoid encouraging unsuitable borrowing or presenting financing as a substitute for affordability.

## Financing Principles

### 1. Customer Choice

Financing should be offered as an option, not forced or used as the primary sales pressure mechanism.

### 2. Transparency

Customers should be able to understand, before committing:

- Total payable amount
- Interest or applicable financing charges
- Fees
- Tenure
- Monthly installment
- Down payment requirements
- Eligibility conditions
- Consequences of missed payments

Actual terms must always come from the relevant licensed financing partner and applicable agreements.

### 3. Responsible Financing

KAMLESH & SONS should not encourage customers to borrow beyond their reasonable ability to repay.

Staff should explain available options accurately and avoid misleading claims such as implying that financing makes a product cheaper when the total cost is higher.

### 4. Premium Experience

The financing process should be simple, professional, private, and respectful. Customers should not feel embarrassed or pressured when discussing payment options.

### 5. Multiple Partners

Over time, KAMLESH & SONS may work with multiple banks or licensed finance companies to provide competitive options, subject to due diligence, commercial terms, regulatory requirements, and customer benefit.

## Customer Journey

The intended experience is:

**Product Selection → Price Understanding → Financing Options → Eligibility → Transparent Terms → Customer Consent → Purchase**

Financing should integrate naturally with the wider customer experience rather than appearing as a separate or confusing process.

## Digital Integration

Future systems may connect financing information with:

- POS
- CRM
- Customer Portal
- Product catalog
- Invoices
- Appointments
- Customer AI

Any integration must respect financial privacy, security, access controls, and applicable regulatory requirements.

The Customer AI should provide only approved and current financing information and should escalate application-specific questions to authorized staff or the financing partner.

## Strategic Benefits

A well-designed financing capability may help KAMLESH & SONS:

- Increase accessibility to premium technology
- Improve conversion among eligible customers
- Support higher-value purchases
- Offer structured payment choices
- Compete through convenience rather than discounting
- Strengthen relationships with financial partners

These benefits must be validated through actual business performance rather than assumed.

## Risk and Compliance Guardrails

Before launch, KAMLESH & SONS must establish appropriate controls for:

- Partner licensing and authorization
- Applicable financial regulations
- Customer eligibility
- Documentation
- Consent
- Privacy
- Data security
- Fraud prevention
- Mis-selling prevention
- Complaint handling
- Record retention

KAMLESH & SONS should not represent itself as a lender unless it is legally authorized to do so.

## Implementation Status

This decision establishes the **strategic direction** to make financing and EMI an important capability.

It does not yet approve:

- A specific bank or finance company
- Interest rates
- EMI products
- Customer eligibility rules
- Financing software
- Commercial agreements
- Credit policies
- Launch date
- Sales targets

Those matters require separate research, financial, legal, compliance, and operational decisions.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded. Individual financing products and partnerships may be added, changed, or discontinued without changing the overall strategic direction.

Any material change to the financing strategy should document the rationale, customer impact, financial implications, regulatory considerations, alternatives considered, and affected repository documents.
