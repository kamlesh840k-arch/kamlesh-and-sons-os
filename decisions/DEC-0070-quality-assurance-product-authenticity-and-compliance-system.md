# DEC-0070 — Quality Assurance, Product Authenticity & Compliance System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0070  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will establish quality controls that protect customers from defective, inaccurate or unauthentic products and make quality performance visible across the supply chain, stores and service operation.

> **Sell what we promise, verify what we receive, and fix quality problems at the source.**

## Core Controls

- Supplier quality standards
- Receiving inspection
- Product authenticity checks
- Product documentation
- Defect classification
- Quarantine and release controls
- Warranty and returns feedback
- Root-cause analysis
- Corrective and preventive action
- Quality reporting

## Product Quality

Products should meet approved specifications and applicable requirements. Higher-risk categories should receive proportionate inspection and documentation controls under DEC-0058.

## Authenticity

Procurement and receiving should use appropriate evidence to reduce counterfeit or unauthorized-product risk.

## Defects

Material defects should be recorded, isolated where necessary and traced to supplier, product, batch or process where possible.

## Continuous Improvement

Recurring quality failures should feed supplier management, merchandising, inventory and service decisions.

## Success Standard

The company can identify significant quality problems quickly, prevent questionable stock from reaching customers and demonstrate corrective action.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Define quality standards by category.
2. Establish receiving and authenticity checks.
3. Create defect and quarantine workflows.
4. Connect returns and warranty data to quality analysis.
5. Establish supplier corrective-action processes.
6. Monitor quality trends and recurring failures.
