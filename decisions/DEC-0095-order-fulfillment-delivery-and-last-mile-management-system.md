# DEC-0095 — Order Fulfillment, Delivery & Last-Mile Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0095  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will manage order fulfillment and delivery through clear service promises, operational ownership, tracking, exception handling and cost-aware execution.

> **Deliver the right order reliably, visibly and economically.**

## Core Areas

- Order capture
- Fulfillment ownership
- Picking and packing
- Delivery planning
- Tracking
- Service-level targets
- Exceptions
- Proof of delivery
- Returns coordination
- Delivery economics

## Success Standard

Orders reach customers accurately and within agreed expectations, while failures are visible and used to improve the fulfillment process.
