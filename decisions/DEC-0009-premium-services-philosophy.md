## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will extend its premium technology-retail experience beyond the product transaction through customer-focused services that improve convenience, confidence, ownership, and long-term relationships.

Planned premium services include:

- Device setup
- Data transfer
- Screen protector installation
- Membership
- Warranty assistance
- Trade-in
- Corporate sales
- Workshops
- Personal consultation

These services are part of the intended customer experience and should be developed as a coherent service ecosystem rather than disconnected add-ons.

## Context

A premium technology house should support customers not only when they purchase a device but also when they begin using, maintaining, upgrading, and replacing it.

Services can therefore become an important source of differentiation and customer trust while supporting the principle established in DEC-0006:

> **Experience > Discounts**

## Service Principles

### 1. Customer Benefit First

A service should solve a genuine customer problem or meaningfully improve the ownership experience.

### 2. Professional Execution

Services should be delivered through clear procedures, trained staff, appropriate tools, and defined quality standards.

### 3. Transparency

Customers should understand what a service includes, its limitations, applicable charges, and expected outcomes before proceeding.

### 4. Convenience

Services should reduce friction across setup, ownership, support, repair, upgrade, and future purchases.

### 5. Continuity

Where appropriate, service interactions should connect with the customer's relationship with KAMLESH & SONS through CRM, warranty records, appointments, and the future customer portal.

### 6. Trust and Data Responsibility

Services involving customer devices or personal information must protect customer privacy, security, and ownership rights.

## Service Categories

### Device Setup

Help customers configure newly purchased devices, accounts, essential settings, and basic functionality.

### Data Transfer

Assist customers with transferring data between devices using safe and appropriate procedures.

### Screen Protector Installation

Provide professional installation where the service is offered, with clear quality standards and responsibility boundaries.

### Membership

Develop future membership offerings that provide meaningful benefits and strengthen long-term customer relationships.

### Warranty Assistance

Help customers understand and navigate applicable manufacturer, distributor, and retailer warranty processes.

### Trade-In

Support customers who want to exchange eligible existing devices toward a new purchase, subject to future valuation, eligibility, and risk-control policies.

### Corporate Sales

Provide tailored technology procurement and support for business and institutional customers.

### Workshops

Offer educational sessions that help customers and communities understand technology and use products more effectively.

### Personal Consultation

Provide one-to-one assistance for customers who require deeper product selection, setup, ecosystem, or technology guidance.

## Future Service Ecosystem

The long-term service model may connect with:

**Customer → CRM → Devices → Warranty → Service → Trade-In → Recommendations → Future Purchase**

The future customer portal should make relevant service information visible to customers while maintaining appropriate privacy and security controls.

## Strategic Implications

This decision should inform future work involving:

- Service SOPs
- Staff training
- Customer portal
- CRM architecture
- Warranty processes
- Trade-in systems
- Appointment systems
- Membership and loyalty
- Corporate sales
- After-sales support
- AI Concierge capabilities

Detailed pricing, eligibility, service-level agreements, staffing requirements, and operating procedures require separate research and decisions.

## Guardrails

No service should be launched solely because it appears premium. Each service should be evaluated for:

- Customer value
- Operational feasibility
- Staff capability
- Financial sustainability
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Data and device security
- Brand impact
- Scalability

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded. Individual services may be introduced, modified, paused, or discontinued through separate operational decisions without changing the overall service philosophy.

Any material change to the premium services philosophy should document the rationale, customer impact, operational implications, alternatives considered, and affected repository documents.
