## Decision

Following the Founder's instruction to choose the field-activation boundary, KAMLESH & SONS adopts a staged, minimum-data approach.

Zero-cost preparation and synthetic rehearsal are authorised immediately. Live participant research, live competitor visits, travel, incentives, paid advice, personal-data collection and commercially confidential collection remain closed until the activation conditions in this decision are evidenced.

This is a field-research governance decision. It is not evidence of demand and does not approve the flagship, an assortment, inventory, pricing, a service, a partner or a launch date.

## Stage A — authorised now

The following work may proceed without another founder decision:

1. Maintain and review the canonical questionnaires, scorecards, codebook and blank operating workbook.
2. Run synthetic role plays using invented records in a disposable copy of the workbook.
3. Test identifiers, required fields, formulas, missing-value codes, exclusion logic, evidence references and the daily QA sequence.
4. Draft—but do not approve—English/Nepali wording, training materials, notices, consent text and incident procedures.
5. Continue public-source desk research and record it under the existing V/P/S/E/H rules.

Stage A has a zero incremental cash budget. It authorises no participant contact, store visit, external travel, incentive, purchase, subscription, adviser engagement or confidential evidence request. Synthetic records must use a `SIM-` prefix, must never enter the canonical blank workbook or an analysis dataset, and must be destroyed after the rehearsal findings are recorded.

**Implementation status — 2026-08-15:** Stage A passed. The disposable synthetic workbook existed only in memory and was destroyed; the canonical workbook remained blank. RESEARCH-FIELD-002 records the results and the governance wording corrected during rehearsal. This implementation note does not activate Stage B.

## Stage B — first live pilot, conditionally authorised

No further strategic choice is required to start Stage B once every condition below is documented. Missing evidence keeps Stage B closed.

1. Name the Research Lead, Data Custodian, Quality Reviewer and enumerator(s). The Quality Reviewer must independently review disputed or excluded records.
2. Obtain qualified Nepal privacy/legal review of notice, consent, lawful basis, access, retention, deletion and incident handling.
3. Freeze the English source instrument; prepare, independently review and adjudicate the Nepali version.
4. Approve and test a company-controlled collection device/tool, restricted storage, access list, backup, sync and incident route.
5. Record dates, daily hours, Nepalgunj locations, researcher safety/check-in plan and a written zero-cost scope or approved capped budget.
6. Complete training and role play without a critical neutrality, safety, consent, privacy or evidence-custody failure.

The first live pilot is limited to:

- twelve adult cognitive interviews in Nepalgunj;
- six standardized Nepalgunj competitor inquiries/observations using the canonical scenarios and basket; and
- up to two supervised live records in each additional workstream that has separately passed every relevant gate.

Kohalpur expansion requires the first Nepalgunj debrief. Rupaidiha or other cross-border activity requires a separate written legal, safety, travel and currency/customs scope. Kathmandu travel is not part of this pilot.

## Minimum-data and conduct rules

For the first live pilot:

- collect no telephone number, email, precise address, citizenship/identity record, banking data, password, OTP, IMEI/serial, private device content or unredacted invoice;
- disable follow-up contact, media use and audio/video recording;
- do not photograph people, screens, documents or devices;
- do not make purchases, finance applications, trade-ins, warranty submissions or false commitments as research;
- keep named-competitor observations internal; external reporting is aggregated or limited to dated public claims with citations;
- use only non-identifying IDs and within-sample reporting; and
- stop immediately for missing consent, unsafe conditions, pressure to provide identity/payment information, unauthorized recording, data loss or material protocol deviation.

A participant may refuse any question or end an interview without consequence. An enumerator may stop an interaction for safety without penalty.

## Stage C — production remains closed

The 100-interview main study, wider competitor programme, confidential sell-through interviews, institutional interviews, purchase audits, service cases, cross-border work and any paid field programme remain closed until:

1. the Stage B pilot report documents disposition, comprehension, duration, missingness, exclusions, safety, custody and deviations;
2. every wording or schema change is recorded and the production instrument is frozen;
3. no unresolved critical privacy, safety, custody or fabrication concern remains; and
4. the Founder accepts the production scope, owners, budget and remaining risk.

Pilot completion cannot approve a market claim or commercial decision by itself.

## Rationale

The research system is ready for controlled rehearsal, but named accountability, local legal interpretation, reviewed Nepali wording and tested storage do not yet exist. A staged boundary preserves momentum without treating founder delegation as permission to invent people, legal advice, evidence or spending authority.

## Review and supersession

This decision remains active until superseded. Any broader geography, sensitive-data collection, contact/media capture, paid action, covert recording, cross-border activity or production launch requires the applicable evidence and explicit approval stated above.
