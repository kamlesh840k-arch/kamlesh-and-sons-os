# DEC-0101 — KAMLESH & SONS Operating System v0.1.0 Foundation Completion & Release Gate

**Decision ID:** DEC-0101  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will formally conclude the **v0.1.0 Foundation** phase once the foundational governance, decision-management, documentation, knowledge-management and operating-system controls are present in the repository and leadership accepts this decision as the release gate for v0.1.0.

> **A foundation is complete when the organization can consistently capture decisions, preserve knowledge, govern change, and use the system to build the next version.**

## Purpose

This decision serves as the final foundation-level DEC in the current 0.1.0 sequence. It does not claim that every future business capability is complete. Instead, it establishes that the foundation is sufficiently defined and governed to begin the next roadmap milestone.

## v0.1.0 Completion Criteria

The foundation is considered complete when the repository contains and maintains:

1. A clear operating-system purpose and structure.
2. Founder and leadership principles sufficient to guide major decisions.
3. Repository and documentation governance.
4. A repeatable decision-record process.
5. Reusable templates for important organizational records.
6. An innovation and knowledge-capture mechanism.
7. A roadmap defining progression beyond the foundation.
8. Version and change-history discipline.
9. A coherent set of foundational operating decisions through this release gate.
10. A clearly identified next milestone: **v0.2.0 — Brand Identity**.

## Release Gate

Before declaring v0.1.0 closed, leadership should confirm that:

- The repository is the authoritative home for the K&S Operating System.
- Material decisions are recorded using the established decision format.
- Current documents have identifiable ownership and review expectations.
- New initiatives can be evaluated against the roadmap and existing decisions.
- Future changes can be versioned without losing organizational history.
- The organization is ready to move from foundation-building into the next defined milestone.

## Scope Boundary

Completion of v0.1.0 does **not** mean that all operational, technology, customer, financial, brand or expansion capabilities are fully implemented. Those capabilities may continue to mature through later roadmap milestones and subsequent versions.

The purpose of this gate is to close the **foundation phase**, not to prematurely declare the entire K&S Operating System finished.

## Next Version

Following acceptance of this decision, work may transition to:

**v0.2.0 — Brand Identity**

The next version should build on the governance and decision foundation established in v0.1.0 rather than replacing it.

## Completion Authority

The final declaration that v0.1.0 is complete remains a leadership decision. This DEC records the proposed completion gate and the criteria against which that decision should be made.

## Success Standard

K&S has a stable, governed foundation from which subsequent versions can be developed without losing decision history, organizational memory, accountability or strategic direction.
