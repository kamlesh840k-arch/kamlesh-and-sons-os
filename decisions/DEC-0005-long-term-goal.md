## Decision

The long-term goal of KAMLESH & SONS is to become:

- An **Authorized Premium Retailer**
- A **Multi-brand Technology House**
- A **National Retail Chain**
- A network of **Technology Experience Centers across Nepal**

This goal represents the intended long-term business model beyond the initial flagship store.

## Context

KAMLESH & SONS is being built with the ambition to create a durable premium technology brand rather than a single-location retail business.

The company will begin by establishing a strong flagship presence in Nepalgunj and then develop the capabilities, systems, customer trust, brand equity, and operating model required for broader expansion.

## Strategic Direction

### 1. Authorized Premium Retailer

Build the brand, operating standards, customer experience, and commercial capabilities required to pursue authorized relationships with leading global technology brands.

### 2. Multi-brand Technology House

Develop a portfolio that can serve customers across multiple technology categories and brands while maintaining a coherent premium experience.

### 3. National Retail Chain

Create repeatable store, service, training, technology, and management systems that can operate consistently across multiple locations.

### 4. Technology Experience Centers

The future physical network should function as technology experience centers rather than conventional product shops, allowing customers to discover, compare, test, purchase, and receive support for technology.

## Intended Expansion Sequence

The current strategic sequence is:

**Phase 1 — Nepalgunj Flagship**  
↓  
**Phase 2 — Western Nepal**  
↓  
**Phase 3 — Major Nepal Cities**  
↓  
**Phase 4 — Nationwide Chain**

Expansion timing, locations, investment requirements, and market-entry criteria require future research and formal decisions.

## Capabilities Required

Achieving this goal will require the company to develop capabilities in:

- Premium retail operations
- Brand management
- Customer experience
- Product expertise
- Employee training
- Supply and inventory management
- Financing and commercial partnerships
- CRM
- AI and technology
- After-sales service
- Store design and development
- Financial management
- Multi-location operations
- Governance and documentation

## Strategic Implications

Major initiatives should be evaluated for their ability to build capabilities that support sustainable expansion.

Short-term growth should not compromise the trust, premium positioning, operational discipline, or customer experience required for the long-term goal.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded by a new decision record. Any material change to the long-term goal should document the strategic rationale, expected consequences, alternatives considered, and affected repository documents.
