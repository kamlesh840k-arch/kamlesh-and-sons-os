# DEC-0093 — Customer Experience & Service Design System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0093  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will design customer journeys deliberately across discovery, purchase, delivery, support, returns and loyalty, with consistent service standards and measurable experience outcomes.

> **Make every important customer interaction clear, useful and trustworthy.**

## Core Areas

- Customer journeys
- Service standards
- Journey ownership
- Friction identification
- Feedback loops
- Service recovery
- Experience measurement
- Omnichannel consistency

## Success Standard

Customers can move through key journeys with minimal avoidable friction, and the company can identify and improve recurring experience failures.
