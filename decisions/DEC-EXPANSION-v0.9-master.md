# DEC-EXPANSION-v0.9 — Expansion Master System

**Decision ID:** DEC-EXPANSION-v0.9  
**Status:** Deferred / Planned  
**Target Version:** v0.9.0 — Expansion

## Purpose

Consolidate expansion, multi-store, partnerships, franchise and geographic-growth decisions into one future KAMLESH & SONS Expansion System.

## Scope

The master covers:

- Multi-store expansion strategy
- Location and market evaluation
- Store rollout sequencing
- Expansion capital and investment gates
- Partnerships and strategic alliances
- Franchise or licensing models, if adopted
- Regional and national growth
- International expansion readiness
- Expansion governance and accountability
- Replication standards
- New-market risk assessment
- Post-opening performance review

## Design Principles

- Expand only when the underlying operating model is repeatable.
- Protect the K&S brand and customer experience during growth.
- Use evidence-based market and location selection.
- Require explicit investment and risk gates for material expansion.
- Replicate proven systems before adding unnecessary complexity.
- Maintain central governance while allowing appropriate local adaptation.
- Measure each expansion against customer, operational and financial outcomes.

## Prerequisites

Expansion depends on the maturity of earlier roadmap systems, especially:

- v0.2 Brand Identity
- v0.3 Store Design
- v0.4 Customer Experience
- v0.5 AI & Technology
- v0.6 Operations
- v0.7 Marketing
- v0.8 Finance

## Relationship to v0.1

v0.1 establishes governance, decision rights, documentation, risk principles and release discipline. It does **not** require a completed expansion strategy or franchise/international operating model.

## Activation

This master becomes active during **v0.9.0 — Expansion**, when K&S is ready to translate the proven operating system into repeatable growth.

## Historical Traceability

Earlier expansion, partnership, franchise and international-growth DEC records remain preserved as historical source material. Future implementation decisions should reference this master rather than creating parallel expansion frameworks.

## Success Standard

K&S can expand deliberately and repeatably while preserving brand trust, customer experience, operating quality, financial discipline and leadership control.
