# DEC-0079 — Enterprise Risk & Insurance Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0079  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will maintain an enterprise risk framework that identifies material threats and opportunities, assigns owners, defines responses and coordinates appropriate insurance protection.

> **Know the risks, protect the downside, and make risk part of every important decision.**

## Core Controls

- Enterprise risk register
- Risk ownership
- Likelihood and impact scoring
- Mitigation plans
- Risk appetite
- Insurance inventory
- Coverage reviews
- Incident and claims tracking
- Emerging-risk monitoring
- Management reporting

## Risk Categories

Risk reviews should consider strategic, financial, operational, legal, technology, cybersecurity, people, supply-chain, customer, reputation and physical risks.

## Risk Appetite

Leadership should define which risks may be accepted, mitigated, transferred or avoided based on materiality and strategic context.

## Insurance

Material assets, liabilities, operations and exposures should be assessed for appropriate insurance coverage with qualified advisors.

## Claims

Insurance incidents and claims should be documented, assigned and reconciled with legal and financial records.

## Review

Risk registers and coverage should be reviewed periodically and after material business changes.

## Success Standard

Leadership can identify the company's most material risks, understand current mitigations and insurance coverage, and see which exposures require action.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Establish enterprise risk categories and ownership.
2. Create the risk register and scoring method.
3. Inventory insurance policies and material exposures.
4. Define mitigation and escalation rules.
5. Review emerging risks and coverage periodically.
6. Integrate risk into strategic and investment decisions.
