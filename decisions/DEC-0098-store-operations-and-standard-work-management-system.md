# DEC-0098 — Store Operations & Standard Work Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0098  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will operate stores using clear standard work, opening and closing routines, merchandising standards, cash controls, service expectations, safety checks and escalation procedures.

> **Make the best way of working easy to repeat, inspect and improve.**

## Core Areas

- Opening and closing
- Store readiness
- Merchandising
- Customer service
- Cash handling
- Inventory routines
- Cleaning and safety
- Issue escalation
- Daily management
- Store performance

## Success Standard

Stores consistently deliver the approved customer and operating standard while local teams retain enough flexibility to respond to legitimate customer and market needs.
