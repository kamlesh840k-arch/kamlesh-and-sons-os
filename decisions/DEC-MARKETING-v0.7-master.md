# DEC-MARKETING-v0.7 — Marketing Master System

**Decision ID:** DEC-MARKETING-v0.7
**Status:** Documentation-ready / strategy approval and execution pending
**Target Version:** v0.7.0 — Marketing

## Purpose

Consolidate marketing, brand activation, communications, campaign, acquisition and retention decisions into one future KAMLESH & SONS Marketing System.

## Scope

The master covers:

- Marketing strategy and positioning activation
- Brand communications
- Campaign planning and execution
- Customer acquisition
- Retention and loyalty
- Content and social channels
- Digital marketing
- Local/store marketing
- Partnerships and promotional activity
- Marketing measurement and attribution
- Marketing budget governance
- Reputation and communications management

## Design Principles

- Marketing must reinforce the approved K&S brand position.
- Customer trust is more important than short-term attention.
- Campaigns should have clear objectives, owners and measures.
- Claims and communications must be accurate and responsible.
- Marketing activity should connect to commercial and customer outcomes.
- Use evidence and learning to improve campaigns.
- Protect customer data and respect privacy requirements.
- Avoid unnecessary channel or campaign complexity.

## Relationship to Earlier Roadmap Stages

v0.2 establishes brand identity. v0.4 establishes the customer-experience foundation. v0.7 activates the marketing system across those foundations rather than redefining them independently.

## Activation

This master is **not part of v0.1.0 completion**. It becomes active during **v0.7.0 — Marketing**, with detailed campaigns and channel plans created only when the underlying brand and customer foundations are ready.

## Historical Traceability

Earlier marketing, communications, positioning and acquisition-related DEC records remain preserved as historical source material. Future implementation decisions should reference this master to avoid parallel marketing frameworks.

## Success Standard

K&S has a measurable, trusted and repeatable marketing system that grows awareness, qualified demand, customer relationships and commercial performance while remaining consistent with the approved brand and customer experience.
