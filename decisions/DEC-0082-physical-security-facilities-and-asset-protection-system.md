# DEC-0082 — Physical Security, Facilities & Asset Protection System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0082  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will protect people, stores, offices, inventory, equipment and facilities through proportionate physical-security and asset-protection controls.

> **Protect people first, then protect the assets that enable the business.**

## Core Controls

- Access control
- Keys and credentials
- CCTV where appropriate
- Alarm procedures
- Opening and closing controls
- Visitor management
- Asset registers
- Incident reporting
- Fire and life safety coordination
- Facility inspections

## Store Security

Store security should address theft, unauthorized access, cash exposure, inventory loss and employee safety while preserving customer experience.

## Asset Management

Material equipment and assets should have owners and appropriate records.

## Facilities

Facilities should be inspected for safety, maintenance, environmental and operational risks.

## Incidents

Security incidents should be documented and escalated according to severity, with coordination across risk, legal, HR and cybersecurity teams as applicable.

## Success Standard

People feel safe, critical assets are appropriately protected, security incidents are visible and facilities remain fit for purpose.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Identify critical facilities and assets.
2. Define access and opening/closing controls.
3. Establish security and incident procedures.
4. Maintain asset and facility records.
5. Conduct periodic inspections and tests.
6. Review recurring incidents and strengthen controls.
