# DEC-0091 — Business Model & Strategic Positioning System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0091  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0
**Related Research:** [Integrated market size, assortment and flagship defensibility](../docs/research/integrated-market-size-assortment-and-flagship-defensibility.md)

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will maintain a clear business model describing target customers, value proposition, revenue logic, capabilities and strategic positioning.

> **Know whom we serve, why they choose us, and how the business creates durable value.**

## Core Areas

- Customer segments
- Value proposition
- Revenue model
- Cost structure
- Competitive positioning
- Key capabilities
- Strategic differentiation
- Business-model assumptions
- Review cadence

## Success Standard

Leadership can clearly explain how K&S creates, delivers and captures value and which assumptions require validation.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Document the current business model.
2. Identify critical assumptions.
3. Connect positioning to customer evidence.
4. Review economics and strategic fit periodically.
5. Update the model when material conditions change.
