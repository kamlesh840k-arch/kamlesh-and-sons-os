# DEC-0099 — Management Reporting & Business Performance System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0099  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will provide leadership with a consistent management view of financial, commercial, operational, customer, people and risk performance using defined metrics, ownership and reporting cadence.

> **Give leaders the few numbers and signals they need to act early.**

## Core Areas

- KPI definitions
- Management dashboard
- Reporting cadence
- Variance analysis
- Exception reporting
- Metric ownership
- Data quality
- Trend analysis
- Action tracking

## Governance

Every material KPI should have a clear definition, owner and source. Significant variances should lead to investigation or action rather than passive reporting.

## Success Standard

Leadership receives timely, trusted and decision-useful information and can distinguish normal variation from conditions requiring intervention.
