# DEC-0092 — Market Research & Competitive Intelligence System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0092  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0
**Related Research:** [Integrated market size, assortment and flagship defensibility](../docs/research/integrated-market-size-assortment-and-flagship-defensibility.md); [Field evidence activation and data-governance protocol](../docs/research/field-evidence-activation-and-data-governance-protocol.md)

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will maintain a disciplined approach to understanding customers, competitors, market trends and external conditions using reliable evidence and clearly separated facts, assumptions and interpretations.

> **Listen to the market continuously, but decide from evidence rather than noise.**

## Core Areas

- Customer research
- Competitor monitoring
- Category trends
- Pricing intelligence
- Market sizing
- Research repository
- Source quality
- Insight synthesis
- Decision linkage

## Governance

Material market claims should identify sources and dates where practical. Sensitive or proprietary information must be handled appropriately.

## Success Standard

Leadership can identify meaningful changes in customer demand, competition and market conditions and connect those insights to decisions.
