## Decision

The official mission of KAMLESH & SONS is:

> **To provide an unmatched technology buying experience through world-class products, knowledgeable people, exceptional service, and innovative technology.**

This mission defines how KAMLESH & SONS intends to serve customers while progressing toward its vision.

## Context

KAMLESH & SONS is being built as a Premium Technology House rather than a conventional mobile shop. The company intends to differentiate itself through the complete customer experience, not through product availability or price alone.

The mission therefore needs to establish the core elements of that experience: products, people, service, and technology.

## Mission Pillars

### 1. World-Class Products

Curate and provide high-quality technology products across the company's current and future categories.

### 2. Knowledgeable People

Build a team capable of understanding products, explaining technology clearly, and helping customers make confident decisions.

### 3. Exceptional Service

Deliver calm, respectful, professional, and convenient service before, during, and after purchase.

### 4. Innovative Technology

Use technology and AI to improve discovery, comparison, purchasing, support, customer relationships, and internal operations.

## Strategic Implications

The mission should guide decisions involving:

- Product selection
- Hiring and training
- Customer service
- Store design
- AI and technology
- CRM
- Customer support
- Premium services
- Marketing
- Operational processes

A proposed initiative should be evaluated not only by its commercial potential but also by whether it strengthens the intended customer experience.

## Relationship to Vision

The **vision** defines the long-term destination:

> To transform retail in Western Nepal and become Nepal's most trusted premium technology brand.

The **mission** defines the operating commitment used to move toward that destination.

## Customer Outcome

The mission is successful when customers leave KAMLESH & SONS feeling informed, respected, confident, and proud of their experience.

The company should aim for customers to remember the experience as much as the product they purchased.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded by a new decision record. Any proposed change to the mission must document the strategic rationale, implications, alternatives considered, and affected repository documents.
