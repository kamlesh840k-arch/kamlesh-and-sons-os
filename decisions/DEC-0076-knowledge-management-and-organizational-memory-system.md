# DEC-0076 — Knowledge Management & Organizational Memory System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0076  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will deliberately capture and maintain important organizational knowledge so decisions, procedures, lessons and operating standards remain accessible as the company grows.

> **If important knowledge exists only in someone's head, the business does not truly own it.**

## Core Capabilities

- Controlled knowledge base
- Standard operating procedures
- Decision records
- Lessons learned
- Product knowledge
- Training materials
- Policy library
- Search and discoverability
- Ownership and review dates
- Version control

## Knowledge Standards

Important knowledge should be written clearly, assigned an owner and reviewed when processes or requirements change.

## Decision Memory

Material decisions should retain rationale, evidence and relevant consequences where practical, building on the decision framework already used by K&S.

## Operational Knowledge

Critical recurring processes should have documented procedures sufficient for trained staff to execute them consistently.

## Searchability

Knowledge should be organized so employees can find the current approved answer without relying on informal message history.

## AI Assistance

Approved AI systems may help employees search, summarize or navigate organizational knowledge, but source documents remain authoritative.

## Governance

Sensitive information must follow DEC-0055 access and privacy controls. Obsolete material should be archived or clearly marked rather than silently competing with current guidance.

## Success Standard

Employees can quickly find the current approved information needed to perform important work, and the organization learns from its own decisions and experience.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Identify critical knowledge domains.
2. Establish a controlled knowledge repository.
3. Standardize document ownership and review.
4. Capture recurring procedures and lessons.
5. Improve search and discoverability.
6. Introduce controlled AI-assisted knowledge retrieval.
