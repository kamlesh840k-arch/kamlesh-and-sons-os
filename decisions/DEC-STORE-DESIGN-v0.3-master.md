# DEC-STORE-DESIGN-v0.3 — Store Design Master System

**Decision ID:** DEC-STORE-DESIGN-v0.3
**Status:** Documentation-ready / real-world validation pending
**Target Version:** v0.3.0 — Store Design

## Purpose

Define the governing framework for translating the approved K&S brand into a physical flagship-store experience and reusable store standards.

## Scope

The v0.3 Store Design system covers:

- Flagship store concept
- Store layout and customer flow
- Zoning and product presentation
- Visual merchandising principles
- Fixtures and display standards
- Signage and wayfinding
- Lighting and environmental principles
- Service and consultation areas
- Technology touchpoints within the store
- Accessibility, safety and practical usability
- Store standards for future replication

## Design Principles

- The physical environment must express the approved v0.2 brand identity.
- Customer flow should be intuitive and comfortable.
- Product presentation should support discovery, comparison and informed decisions.
- Premium quality should come from clarity, consistency and service—not unnecessary extravagance.
- Store design must support staff operations as well as customer experience.
- Safety, accessibility and maintainability are design requirements, not afterthoughts.
- Standards should be documented so successful elements can be replicated later.

## Dependencies

v0.3 depends on the approved v0.2 brand strategy, identity and standards. Detailed customer-journey work belongs to v0.4, while operational systems belong to v0.6.

## Deliverables

1. Flagship concept brief
2. Store zoning and flow plan
3. Visual merchandising standards
4. Fixture/display principles
5. Signage and wayfinding standards
6. Lighting/environment principles
7. Service-zone requirements
8. Store technology-touchpoint requirements
9. Accessibility/safety checklist
10. Reusable store standards

## Boundary

This master does not approve construction drawings, vendor selections, budgets, final dimensions or procurement. Those require subsequent implementation decisions and appropriate technical validation.

## Completion Standard

v0.3 is complete when the flagship concept and store-design standards are documented, internally consistent with the approved brand, operationally usable, and ready to guide detailed design/procurement without ambiguity.

## Current Gate

The documentation standard is met subject to the [v0.3 Documentation Closure](../docs/v0.3-store-design-documentation-closure.md). Site validation, design-governance ownership, drawings, budgets, vendors, procurement, engineering, construction and handover remain separately gated.
