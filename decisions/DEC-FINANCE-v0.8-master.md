# DEC-FINANCE-v0.8 — Finance Master System

**Decision ID:** DEC-FINANCE-v0.8
**Status:** Documentation-ready / professional review and implementation pending
**Target Version:** v0.8.0 — Finance

## Purpose

Consolidate finance, accounting, treasury, budgeting, commercial economics and financial-control decisions into one future KAMLESH & SONS Finance System.

## Scope

The master covers:

- Financial governance and accountability
- Accounting and financial reporting
- Budgeting and forecasting
- Cash and treasury management
- Revenue and margin analysis
- Cost management
- Pricing economics and commercial profitability
- Procurement and payment controls
- Tax and statutory compliance
- Capital expenditure governance
- Financial risk and controls
- Management financial reporting

## Design Principles

- Financial records must be accurate, timely and traceable.
- Cash protection is a core management responsibility.
- Material spending requires appropriate authority.
- Decisions should consider both revenue and economic value.
- Separate financial facts from forecasts and assumptions.
- Maintain appropriate segregation of duties and internal controls.
- Comply with applicable accounting, tax and statutory requirements.
- Use financial reporting to support decisions rather than reporting for its own sake.

## Relationship to Earlier Roadmap Stages

v0.1 establishes the financial-control and governance foundation. Commercial/customer work may create financial assumptions in v0.4 and v0.7, but the integrated finance operating system is intentionally developed in v0.8.

## Activation

This master is **not part of v0.1.0 completion**. It becomes active during **v0.8.0 — Finance**, when K&S is ready to establish the detailed financial operating model and management reporting system.

## Historical Traceability

Earlier finance, pricing, revenue, budgeting, control and reporting-related DEC records remain preserved as historical source material. Future implementation decisions should reference this master to avoid parallel finance frameworks.

## Success Standard

K&S has reliable financial records, disciplined cash and spending controls, useful forecasting, clear unit and business economics, compliant reporting and management information that supports timely decisions.
