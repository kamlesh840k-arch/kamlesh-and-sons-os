# DEC-PEOPLE-v0.6 — People & Workforce Master System

**Decision ID:** DEC-PEOPLE-v0.6  
**Status:** Deferred / Planned  
**Target Version:** v0.6.0 — Operations

## Purpose

Consolidate the people and workforce decisions that should become the KAMLESH & SONS People System as operational scale develops.

## Scope

The master covers:

- Workforce planning and capacity
- Recruitment and onboarding
- Roles, responsibilities and competency standards
- Learning and development
- Performance management
- Attendance and workforce administration
- Employee experience and engagement
- Leadership development
- Workforce productivity and capability measurement
- Employment compliance and fair-work practices

## Design Principles

- Hire for capability and values.
- Make roles and expectations clear.
- Develop people continuously.
- Measure performance fairly and consistently.
- Build leadership capability before scale demands it.
- Protect employee dignity, privacy and lawful rights.
- Connect workforce planning to actual customer and operational demand.

## Relationship to v0.1

v0.1 establishes governance, accountability and documentation principles. Detailed workforce systems are deliberately deferred; they are not required to declare the v0.1 foundation complete.

## Activation

This master becomes active during **v0.6.0 — Operations**, with implementation sequencing determined by workforce maturity, store growth and business needs.

## Historical Traceability

Earlier workforce-related DEC records remain historical source material. Future implementation decisions should reference this master to avoid creating parallel frameworks.

## Success Standard

K&S can recruit, develop, deploy, evaluate and retain capable people through a consistent system that supports customer experience, operational reliability and responsible growth.
