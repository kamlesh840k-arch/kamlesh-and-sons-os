# DEC-0100 — v0.1.0 Foundation Integration & Completion Readiness System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0100  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will use DEC-0100 as the final integration checkpoint for the DEC-0091 through DEC-0100 foundation sequence and as the readiness gate for formally completing the v0.1.0 foundation.

> **A foundation is complete when its parts work together, ownership is clear, and the organization is ready to operate and improve the system.**

## Completion Criteria

- Core business capabilities have defined decision ownership.
- Major operational, customer, financial, people, technology and risk systems are represented.
- Important processes and controls have documented standards.
- Management reporting supports decision-making.
- Organizational knowledge and decision history are preserved.
- Material risks and continuity needs are addressed.
- The operating model is sufficiently integrated to support the next roadmap milestone.
- Outstanding gaps are visible and assigned rather than hidden.

## Release Gate

DEC-0100 does not claim that every process is fully optimized. It establishes that v0.1.0 has a coherent foundation from which K&S can move into the next roadmap milestone after leadership review and formal closure.

## Next Stage

Following formal completion of v0.1.0, the roadmap moves to **v0.2.0 — Brand Identity**.

## Success Standard

K&S has a coherent, documented and governed foundation that leadership can operate, inspect and improve, with a clear transition path to the next version.
