# DEC-0075 — Employee Performance & Incentive Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0075  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will use fair, understandable performance and incentive practices that align individual contribution with customer outcomes, operational standards and sustainable business performance.

> **Reward contribution without creating behavior that damages the business.**

## Core Controls

- Role expectations
- Goal setting
- Regular feedback
- Performance reviews
- Coaching and development
- Incentive eligibility
- Incentive quality checks
- Recognition
- Performance improvement plans
- Documentation

## Performance

Goals should be relevant to role responsibilities and balanced across productivity, quality, customer experience, teamwork and compliance where appropriate.

## Incentives

Incentives should avoid encouraging unsafe, misleading, fraudulent or customer-harming behavior. Measures should be balanced so optimizing one metric does not create unacceptable damage elsewhere.

## Reviews

Performance discussions should be timely, evidence-based and focused on improvement as well as accountability.

## Fairness

Employment and incentive practices must follow applicable requirements and the legal/compliance framework under DEC-0058.

## Manager Accountability

Managers are responsible for clear expectations, regular coaching, consistent application of standards and appropriate escalation of material performance issues.

## Success Standard

Employees understand what good performance means, managers can coach effectively and incentives reinforce sustainable business behavior.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Define role expectations and core measures.
2. Establish review and feedback cadence.
3. Design balanced incentive principles.
4. Add quality and compliance safeguards.
5. Train managers on fair performance practices.
6. Review incentive outcomes for unintended behavior.
