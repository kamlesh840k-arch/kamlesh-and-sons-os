## Decision

The official vision of KAMLESH & SONS is:

> **To transform retail in Western Nepal and become Nepal's most trusted premium technology brand.**

This vision is the long-term strategic direction against which major company initiatives should be evaluated.

## Context

KAMLESH & SONS is intended to grow beyond a conventional mobile shop into a premium technology house. The initial focus is on building a flagship retail experience in Nepalgunj, followed by expansion across Western Nepal, major cities, and ultimately a nationwide network.

The vision therefore needs to express both the immediate geographic opportunity and the long-term national ambition.

## Rationale

The vision establishes four important strategic commitments:

1. **Transform retail** — improve how customers discover, evaluate, purchase, and receive support for technology.
2. **Start in Western Nepal** — build a strong regional foundation before pursuing broader expansion.
3. **Build trust** — make trust a defining characteristic of the brand rather than relying only on product or price.
4. **Become a premium technology brand** — create a durable identity that extends beyond a single product category.

## Strategic Implications

The vision should guide decisions involving:

- Store experience
- Product selection
- Customer service
- Employee capability
- Brand identity
- AI and technology
- CRM and customer systems
- Operational standards
- Marketing
- Financing
- Expansion
- Partnerships

Initiatives that materially support the vision should be prioritized over initiatives that provide only short-term gains but weaken the intended premium and trusted positioning.

## Success Direction

Progress toward the vision should ultimately be evaluated through evidence such as:

- Customer trust and satisfaction
- Strength of the premium brand
- Quality and consistency of the retail experience
- Customer retention and referrals
- Employee knowledge and service quality
- Successful technology-enabled experiences
- Sustainable store economics
- Successful expansion into new markets

Specific KPIs and targets will be established in future operating and finance documentation.

## Relationship to Mission

The vision describes **where KAMLESH & SONS intends to go**.

The mission describes **how the company intends to serve customers while moving toward that vision**.

The approved mission is documented in the Founding Charter and should be developed further in the appropriate strategy documentation.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded by a new decision record. Any proposed change to the company vision must document the strategic rationale, implications, alternatives considered, and affected repository documents.
