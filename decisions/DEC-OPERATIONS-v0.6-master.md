# DEC-OPERATIONS-v0.6 — Operations Master System

**Decision ID:** DEC-OPERATIONS-v0.6
**Status:** Documentation-ready / pilot pending
**Target Version:** v0.6.0 — Operations

## Purpose

Consolidate the operational decisions that should become the KAMLESH & SONS Operations System when v0.6 is activated.

## Source Decision Cluster

This master supersedes the need to maintain overlapping standalone operational frameworks across the historical DEC series, including decisions covering:

- supply chain
- procurement
- quality
- inventory
- fulfillment and delivery
- store operations
- workforce planning and operating capacity
- operational reporting and continuous improvement

Historical DEC records remain preserved as source history.

## Operating Domains

### 1. Supply Chain
Supplier planning, sourcing, replenishment, logistics, lead times, dependencies and resilience.

### 2. Procurement
Supplier selection, purchasing controls, approval authority, commercial terms, performance and ethical procurement.

### 3. Inventory
Stock accuracy, availability, receiving, transfers, cycle counts, reconciliation, shrinkage and exception management.

### 4. Fulfillment & Last Mile
Order capture, picking, packing, delivery, tracking, service promises, exceptions, returns and delivery economics.

### 5. Store Operations
Opening and closing routines, store readiness, merchandising execution, cash handling, customer service standards, safety and escalation.

### 6. Workforce Capacity
Workforce demand planning, staffing, scheduling, attendance, skills coverage, productivity and labor-cost management.

### 7. Quality & Continuous Improvement
Quality standards, defect prevention, root-cause analysis, corrective action, process measurement and improvement cycles.

## Design Principles

- Standardize what should be consistent.
- Preserve local flexibility where customer or market conditions require it.
- Make ownership explicit.
- Measure outcomes rather than activity alone.
- Prefer prevention over repeated correction.
- Use reliable data for operational decisions.
- Escalate material exceptions early.
- Connect operational improvements to customer, financial and strategic outcomes.

## Activation Rule

This master is **not part of v0.1.0 completion**. It becomes an active implementation decision during **v0.6.0 — Operations** after the preceding roadmap foundations are established.

## Historical Traceability

The historical DEC records that contributed to this cluster remain authoritative evidence of prior thinking. Future v0.6 implementation decisions should reference this master rather than creating unnecessary parallel systems.

## Success Standard

K&S can operate stores and supporting functions reliably at scale, with clear processes, measurable service levels, controlled costs, accurate inventory, capable workforce planning and repeatable improvement.
