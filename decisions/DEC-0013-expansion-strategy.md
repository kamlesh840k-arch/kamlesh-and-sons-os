## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will follow a phased geographic expansion strategy:

**Phase 1 — Nepalgunj Flagship**  
↓  
**Phase 2 — Western Nepal**  
↓  
**Phase 3 — Major Nepal Cities**  
↓  
**Phase 4 — Nationwide Chain**

Expansion should be capability-led and evidence-based rather than driven only by the desire to open more locations.

## Context

The company's vision is to transform retail in Western Nepal and become Nepal's most trusted premium technology brand. The first flagship is therefore intended to establish the operating model, customer experience, brand standards, technology systems, and economics that can support future expansion.

## Phase 1 — Nepalgunj Flagship

The Nepalgunj flagship is the foundation of the expansion strategy.

Its purpose is to:

- Establish the KAMLESH & SONS brand.
- Validate the premium retail experience.
- Develop operating procedures.
- Test product categories and services.
- Build customer trust.
- Develop staff capabilities.
- Validate the AI and digital customer experience.
- Establish sustainable unit economics.
- Capture lessons for future locations.

The flagship should be treated as a learning laboratory as well as a commercial store.

## Phase 2 — Western Nepal

After the flagship model is sufficiently validated, expansion should target suitable markets across Western Nepal.

Potential locations, market sizes, competition, customer demand, investment requirements, and operating economics must be researched before specific locations are approved.

## Phase 3 — Major Nepal Cities

Once the Western Nepal operating model is proven and the company has sufficient organizational capability, expansion may proceed into major Nepalese cities.

Expansion should preserve the premium experience rather than sacrificing standards for speed or store count.

## Phase 4 — Nationwide Chain

The long-term objective is a nationwide network of KAMLESH & SONS technology experience centers.

At this stage, the company should have repeatable systems for:

- Store design
- Hiring
- Training
- Product merchandising
- Inventory
- Customer experience
- Service
- CRM
- AI
- Finance
- Marketing
- Management
- Quality control

## Expansion Gates

Before entering a new phase, the company should evaluate evidence across at least these dimensions:

### Customer

- Brand awareness
- Customer satisfaction
- Repeat purchase
- Referral activity
- Local demand

### Commercial

- Revenue quality
- Gross margin
- Store-level profitability
- Cash-flow requirements
- Financing requirements

### Operational

- SOP maturity
- Staff capability
- Inventory systems
- Service capacity
- Management bandwidth

### Technology

- CRM maturity
- Customer portal readiness
- AI reliability
- Data systems
- Reporting and analytics

### Brand

- Consistency of the premium experience
- Visual standards
- Customer trust
- Service quality

A new location should not be opened merely because a market appears attractive; KAMLESH & SONS must also be capable of operating that location to its intended standard.

## Strategic Principle

> **Build one excellent system before attempting to build many stores.**

The flagship should establish a repeatable operating model before aggressive geographic expansion.

## Implementation Status

This decision establishes the strategic sequence only. It does not approve specific future locations, investment amounts, opening dates, store formats, or expansion budgets.

Those decisions require market research, financial modeling, operational planning, and separate decision records.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded. Geographic sequencing may be adjusted when new market research or operating evidence justifies a change.

Any material change to the expansion sequence should document the evidence, rationale, financial and operational implications, alternatives considered, and affected repository documents.
