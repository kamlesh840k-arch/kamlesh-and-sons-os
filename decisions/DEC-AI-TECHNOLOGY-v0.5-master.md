# DEC-AI-TECHNOLOGY-v0.5 — AI & Technology Master System

**Decision ID:** DEC-AI-TECHNOLOGY-v0.5  
**Status:** Deferred / Planned  
**Target Version:** v0.5.0 — AI & Technology

## Purpose

Consolidate technology, digital-product, AI, platform and integration decisions into one future KAMLESH & SONS technology system, avoiding fragmented implementation decisions before the foundation and operating model are ready.

## Scope

The master covers:

- Technology architecture
- Information and data foundations
- Cybersecurity and privacy implementation
- AI governance and responsible AI
- AI concierge and internal AI capabilities
- Digital products and customer-facing technology
- APIs and ecosystem integrations
- Automation and workflow technology
- Technology vendor and platform management
- Technology lifecycle and technical debt
- Technology performance and reliability

## Design Principles

- Technology serves business and customer outcomes.
- Security and privacy are designed in, not added later.
- AI use must have clear ownership, controls and measurable value.
- Prefer interoperable, maintainable systems over unnecessary complexity.
- Avoid premature platform commitments.
- Document important architecture and integration decisions.
- Protect company and customer data throughout the technology lifecycle.

## Relationship to v0.1

v0.1 establishes repository governance, documentation discipline, technology governance principles and foundational security expectations. It does not require implementation of AI, digital products, APIs or a complete technology platform.

## Activation

This master becomes active during **v0.5.0 — AI & Technology**, following the brand, store and customer-experience foundations defined in earlier roadmap milestones.

## Historical Traceability

Earlier technology-related DEC records remain preserved as historical source material. Future technology implementation decisions should reference this master rather than creating overlapping standalone frameworks.

## Success Standard

K&S has a coherent, secure and maintainable technology architecture with governed AI capabilities, reliable integrations, useful digital products and measurable technology-enabled business value.
