# DEC-0069 — Pricing, Revenue & Commercial Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0069  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will manage pricing and commercial decisions through disciplined rules that balance customer value, competitive context, margin, inventory, cash and long-term trust.

> **Price for value, protect margin, and never trade trust for a short-term sale.**

## Core Controls

- Price ownership and approval
- Cost and margin visibility
- Price-list governance
- Discount authorization
- Promotion economics
- Channel consistency
- Competitive monitoring
- Markdown and clearance rules
- Exception management
- Revenue and margin reporting

## Pricing Principles

Prices should be based on reliable costs, market context, customer value and strategic positioning. Material pricing changes should consider inventory and cash implications under DEC-0057 and supply constraints under DEC-0064.

## Discounts

Discounts require defined authority and should be measured against incremental demand and contribution rather than revenue alone.

## Promotions

Promotions must have clear eligibility, timing, economics and inventory readiness, coordinated with DEC-0059.

## Governance

Material pricing or commercial exceptions should be documented, approved and reviewable. Customer-facing claims must comply with DEC-0058.

## Success Standard

Leadership can explain how prices are set, where margin is earned or lost, which promotions create value and which commercial exceptions require intervention.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Establish price ownership and approval thresholds.
2. Build cost and margin visibility.
3. Standardize discount and promotion controls.
4. Establish price-change monitoring.
5. Connect pricing to inventory and demand planning.
6. Review commercial performance regularly.
