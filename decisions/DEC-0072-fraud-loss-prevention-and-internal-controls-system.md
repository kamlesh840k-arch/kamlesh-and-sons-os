# DEC-0072 — Fraud, Loss Prevention & Internal Controls System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0072  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-08

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will use proportionate preventive, detective and corrective controls to reduce fraud, theft, financial leakage and operational abuse.

> **Prevent what we can, detect what we miss, and investigate what matters.**

## Core Controls

- Segregation of duties
- Approval thresholds
- Cash controls
- Inventory controls
- Refund and discount controls
- Access controls
- Transaction monitoring
- Exception reporting
- Investigation procedures
- Corrective action

## Risk Areas

Controls should address risks across cash, payments, inventory, purchasing, refunds, discounts, payroll, supplier relationships, customer accounts and technology access.

## Store Controls

Store operations should use appropriate cash reconciliation, stock controls, refund approvals and exception monitoring under DEC-0053 and DEC-0057.

## Digital Controls

Online transactions should use appropriate authentication, authorization, monitoring and fraud-prevention measures.

## Investigations

Suspected material fraud should be handled confidentially, fairly and with evidence preservation, coordinated with DEC-0058 where legal advice is required.

## Reporting

Material incidents and recurring loss patterns should be escalated to appropriate leadership and included in enterprise risk management.

## Success Standard

The company can identify unusual activity, limit avoidable losses, preserve evidence and respond consistently to material control breaches.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Map material fraud and loss risks.
2. Establish preventive approval and segregation controls.
3. Implement transaction and inventory exception reporting.
4. Define investigation and escalation procedures.
5. Review control effectiveness periodically.
6. Feed lessons into enterprise risk management.
