## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will compete primarily through **trust, service, knowledge, experience, and convenience**, rather than competing only on price.

The guiding principle is:

> **Experience > Discounts**

The objective is to make the customer experience a defining reason to choose KAMLESH & SONS.

## Context

Technology products can often be compared by specifications and price. A premium technology retailer must therefore create value beyond the transaction itself.

KAMLESH & SONS intends to build a retail environment where customers can explore products, ask questions, receive knowledgeable guidance, and obtain professional support before and after purchase.

## Customer Promise

Customers should leave KAMLESH & SONS feeling:

- Respected
- Welcomed
- Informed
- Confident
- Comfortable
- Proud of their purchase
- Interested in returning

The intended emotional outcome is that customers remember **visiting KAMLESH & SONS**, not merely buying a device.

## Experience Principles

### 1. Knowledge Before Pressure

Staff should help customers understand their options rather than pressure them into a purchase.

### 2. Product Experience Before Purchase

Where practical, customers should be able to interact with live products and understand how they work before making a decision.

### 3. Calm and Professional Service

Customer interactions should be respectful, patient, knowledgeable, and professional.

### 4. Convenience

The company should continuously reduce unnecessary friction across discovery, purchase, setup, financing, warranty, repair, and support.

### 5. Service Beyond the Sale

The relationship should continue after purchase through setup assistance, warranty support, trade-in, repair coordination, education, and future recommendations.

## Competitive Philosophy

KAMLESH & SONS should not build its identity around being the cheapest retailer.

Price competitiveness remains commercially important, but long-term differentiation should come from the complete experience surrounding the product.

## Strategic Implications

This decision should influence:

- Store layout
- Product displays
- Staff hiring and training
- Customer service standards
- Premium services
- AI Concierge design
- CRM and customer portal development
- Marketing
- Loyalty programs
- After-sales support

Any initiative that materially improves customer experience should be evaluated as a potential strategic capability, provided it remains financially and operationally sustainable.

## Future Measurement

Customer experience should eventually be measured through appropriate metrics such as:

- Customer satisfaction
- Net Promoter Score or an equivalent loyalty measure
- Repeat visits
- Repeat purchases
- Referrals
- Service satisfaction
- Complaint resolution time
- Product-return experience
- Customer retention

Specific targets and measurement methods will be established in future operations and customer-experience documentation.

## Review / Supersession

This decision remains active until formally superseded. Any material change to the customer experience philosophy should document the rationale, expected customer impact, alternatives considered, and affected repository documents.
