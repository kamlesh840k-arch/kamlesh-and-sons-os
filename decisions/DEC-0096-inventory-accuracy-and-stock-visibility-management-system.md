# DEC-0096 — Inventory Accuracy & Stock Visibility Management System

**Decision ID:** DEC-0096  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Decision

KAMLESH & SONS will maintain reliable inventory records and stock visibility across stores, warehouses and channels so commercial and operational decisions are based on trustworthy availability information.

> **If the system says we have it, we should be able to find it.**

## Core Areas

- Stock records
- Receiving
- Transfers
- Cycle counts
- Reconciliation
- Shrinkage
- Availability visibility
- Stock adjustments
- Inventory ownership
- Exception reporting

## Governance

Material inventory discrepancies should be investigated, approved adjustments documented and recurring causes addressed through process or supplier improvements.

## Success Standard

Leadership and frontline teams can trust inventory availability, identify discrepancies quickly and understand the major sources of stock loss or inaccuracy.
