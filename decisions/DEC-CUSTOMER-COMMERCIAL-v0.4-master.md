# K&S Customer & Commercial Master Decision

**Master ID:** DEC-CUSTOMER-COMMERCIAL-v0.4
**Status:** Documentation-ready / activation pending
**Target Version:** v0.4 Customer Experience, with selected commercial components extending into v0.7 Marketing and v0.8 Finance

## Purpose

Consolidate the historical customer and commercial decision inputs into one future-facing master framework without prematurely activating detailed operating rules during v0.1 foundation.

## Historical Inputs

This master absorbs the conceptual inputs from DEC-0006, DEC-0019, DEC-0028, DEC-0046, DEC-0054, DEC-0067, DEC-0068, DEC-0091, DEC-0092, DEC-0093 and DEC-0094, as mapped in `docs/decision-consolidation-registry.md`.

## Scope

The future customer and commercial system will cover:

- Customer segments and needs
- Value proposition and positioning
- Customer research and market intelligence
- Customer journeys and service design
- CRM and customer information
- Channel and omnichannel experience
- Pricing and revenue principles
- Commercial performance
- Feedback and service recovery
- Loyalty and retention

## Roadmap Boundary

This document is a consolidation destination, not a v0.1 implementation decision.

- **v0.4:** Customer experience and service design
- **v0.7:** Marketing, customer acquisition and commercial activation
- **v0.8:** Financial/economic controls and detailed commercial finance

## Operating Principle

K&S should design the customer experience around trust, clarity, consistency and useful service while connecting commercial decisions to evidence and sustainable economics.

## Activation Gate

The detailed policies, KPIs, processes and system requirements should be defined when the relevant roadmap milestone becomes active. Historical DEC records remain traceable and should not be silently discarded.

## Success Standard

When activated, K&S has one coherent customer/commercial architecture rather than separate disconnected decisions for customer data, experience, channels, pricing, research and positioning.

## Canonical Implementation Reference

[v0.4 Customer Experience System](../docs/v0.4-customer-experience-system.md) and its [activation checklist](../docs/v0.4-customer-experience-checklist.md) define the current implementation-level documentation. Real-customer validation and activation remain pending.
